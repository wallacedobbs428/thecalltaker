How Every Call Gets Answered

The Call Flow

From Ring to Booked Job

Here is exactly what happens when a homeowner calls your business.

01

Phone Rings

A homeowner needs HVAC help. Could be 2pm or 2am.

02

AI Picks Up in Under 2 Seconds

No hold music, no voicemail, no "press 1 for...". A friendly voice answers immediately.

03

Identifies the Caller

Returning caller? The AI recognizes them: "Hi Mrs. Johnson, is this about your furnace repair from last week?"

04

Captures the Details

Name, phone, address, problem description. All logged automatically — nothing falls through the cracks.

05

Assesses Urgency

Gas leak? No heat with kids in the house? The AI flags emergencies and escalates immediately.

06

Books the Appointment

Checks your calendar, books the next available slot. No double-booking, no back-and-forth.

07

Sends Confirmation

Customer gets a text. You get a text with all details. Appointment lands on your calendar.

08

Follows Up Automatically

Next day: "Hi, confirming your appointment tomorrow at 10am." Reduces no-shows by 40%.
