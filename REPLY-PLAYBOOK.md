# Reply Playbook — Oracle Hot Lead Responses
## Goal: respond within 5 minutes. Every minute costs closes.

---

### TRIGGER: "Sounds cool" / "Interesting" / "Tell me more"
They're warm. Move to call or payment now. Don't over-explain.

**RESPONSE:**
"Glad you checked it out [name]! Honestly the easiest next step is just locking in your founding rate before the spots fill — it's $264/mo and that price never goes up. I can have Jessica live for [biz name] within 24 hours. Want me to send the payment link?"

---

### TRIGGER: "How does it work?" / "What exactly does she do?"
They need clarity. Give the 3-line answer, then close.

**RESPONSE:**
"Short version: you forward your number to Jessica (or get a new one), she answers every call 24/7, books appointments directly to your calendar, and texts customers confirmations. You just do the work — she fills your schedule. Setup is 24 hours. Want to move forward?"

---

### TRIGGER: "How much is it?" / "What's the price?"
They're already interested — price is just a checkpoint.

**RESPONSE:**
"Founding rate is $264/mo — that's locked forever for the first 20 customers. After that it goes to $497. You're looking at 7 spots left. I can send you the link right now: thecalltaker.com/pay#starter
Takes 2 minutes to get started."

---

### TRIGGER: "I already have a receptionist" / "We use voicemail"
Don't argue. Flip it.

**RESPONSE:**
"That makes sense — Jessica isn't replacing anyone, she just covers the hours nobody's available. Nights, weekends, holidays. That's when the expensive jobs come in for [their vertical]. No conflict with what you already have."

---

### TRIGGER: "I need to think about it" / "Maybe later"
They're not saying no. Create mild urgency, stay warm.

**RESPONSE:**
"Totally fair. Just so you know — the founding rate expires when spot 20 fills, and we're at 13 right now. No pressure, but wanted you to have that context. I'll check back in a few days unless you want to move sooner?"

---

### TRIGGER: "Not interested" / "Remove me"
Respect it. Leave the door open.

**RESPONSE:**
"No problem at all [name] — I'll take you off the list. If things change and you want to revisit, you know where to find me. Good luck with [biz name]."
Tag contact in GHL: "opted-out" — remove from all sequences.

---

### TRIGGER: "Yes" / "Send me the link" / "How do I pay?"
THIS IS THE CLOSE. Move in under 60 seconds.

**RESPONSE:**
"Here you go: thecalltaker.com/pay#starter
PayPal, takes 2 minutes. Once you're through I'll reach out within the hour to get Jessica configured for [biz name]. You'll be live within 24 hours."

Then: monitor GHL/PayPal for payment confirmation.
When payment hits:
- Tag contact "paid-founding" in GHL
- Trigger onboarding sequence immediately
