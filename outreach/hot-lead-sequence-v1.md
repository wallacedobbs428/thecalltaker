- On reply: STOP sequence, add `hot-replied`, notify Wallace via ntfy URGENT
- On demo call (>60s from this contact): STOP sequence, add `demo-called`
- On pilot signup: STOP sequence, add `pilot-active`

### Enrollment criteria:
- Contact has tag: `hot-lead`
- Contact does NOT have tags: `pilot-active`, `active-client`, `do-not-contact`, `unsubscribed`, `hot-seq-1`
- Contact has a phone number (for SMS + voicemail channels)
- Contact has an email (for email channel — skip email touch if no email, still send SMS)

### Sending rules:
- SMS: Send immediately at scheduled time
- Email: Send 2-3 minutes after SMS (so SMS arrives first — higher open rate)
- Voicemail drop: Touch 1 only (via Bland.ai API)
- Touch 1: Triggers 5 min after `hot-lead` tag is applied
- Touch 2: 1 hour after Touch 1
- Touch 3: Next morning 7-9am (local time if known, else Eastern)
- Touch 4: Day 3, 10am
- Touch 5: Day 7, 10am
- Touch 6: Day 14, 10am
- Never send between 9pm-7am
- If reply detected at any point: STOP all scheduled touches, notify URGENT

### For the 35 existing hot leads:
- Check last contact date in GHL
- If contacted in last 48 hours: start at next logical touch
- If NOT contacted in last 48 hours: start at Touch 1 immediately
- Batch first sends: 10 per hour to avoid spam triggers
- Priority order: leads with industry tags first (personalization is ready), untagged leads get generic version
