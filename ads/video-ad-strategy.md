## 1. THE 3 VIDEO FORMATS THAT WIN IN 2026

### Format A: Hook Video (Pattern Interrupt)
**What it is:** First 3 seconds break the scroll with something unexpected.
**Why it works:** 65% of viewers decide to keep watching in the first 3 seconds. If the hook fails, nothing else matters.

**Best hooks for service business owners:**
1. **The Phone Ring** — Show a phone ringing at an unusual time (11pm, 3am). Instant recognition.
2. **The Dollar Amount** — "$1,200. Gone." Large text on black screen. Gut punch.
3. **The Age Reveal** — "I'm 16 and I built..." Pattern interrupt — nobody expects a teen founder.
4. **The Challenge** — "Call this number right now." Direct engagement while watching.
5. **The Fast Cut** — Rapid comparisons of what $97 buys (dinner, gas, Netflix vs. 730 hours).

### Format B: Problem/Solution (Pain → Fix)
**What it is:** 10 seconds of pain, 15 seconds of solution, 5 seconds of CTA.
**Why it works:** Service business owners FEEL the pain of missed calls. Activating that emotion then showing the fix creates desire.

**Pain activation sequence:**
1. Show the missed call notification (everyone has experienced this)
2. Show the dollar amount lost (makes it concrete)
3. Show the competitor who got the job instead (fear of loss)
4. Transition: "Unless..." or "But what if..."
5. Show Gideon answering, booking, confirming
6. Show the result: money booked while sleeping

### Format C: Social Proof / Testimonial Style
**What it is:** A real or AI-generated person telling their story with your product.
**Why it works:** People trust other business owners more than brands. Even simulated testimonials (disclosed) work because the scenario is believable.

**Best formats:**
1. Direct to camera (Wallace — authentic, raw, young founder energy)
2. Before/after split screen (visual proof)
3. Screen recording of actual demo call (hardest proof to fake)
4. Stats overlay on dark background (data-driven trust)

```
└──────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Critical Rules:
- **No logo in first 3 seconds.** Hook must be brand-neutral.
- **One text element at a time.** Never stack 3+ overlays.
- **Voice starts at second 3**, not second 0. Let the visual hook work.
- **CTA must include phone number.** Not just "Learn More" — give them the demo line.
- **Red → Green transition.** Pain is red. Solution is green. The color shift IS the story.
- **Music fades at 25s.** Last 5 seconds = voice + CTA only.

## 3. COMPETITIVE VIDEO AD LANDSCAPE

### What's Running in the Answering Service Space

**Smith.ai** — Runs image-based ads primarily. When they do video, it's polished corporate talking-head. Clean but not emotional. No pattern interrupt. Their weakness: too professional, doesn't connect with blue-collar service owners.

**Ruby Receptionists** — Lifestyle-focused video ads. Happy people answering phones. Generic. Their weakness: doesn't show the PAIN of missed calls. All aspiration, no agitation.

**Nexa Receptionists** — Similar to Ruby. Professional, informational. "We answer your calls." Their weakness: no urgency, no demo, no way to experience the product.

**ServiceTitan** — Best video ads in the service business space (not answering, but adjacent). Uses real business owner testimonials, before/after revenue numbers, and "this changed my business" narrative. Their strength: social proof from real people.

**Housecall Pro / Jobber** — Facebook and YouTube video ads showing software demos + business owner stories. Feature-focused. Their weakness: too long (60-90s), lose viewers after 15s.

### The Gap The Call Taker Exploits

1. **Nobody shows the PAIN live.** We show the phone ringing at 11pm. We show the missed call notification. We show "$1,200 gone." The competitors skip the agitation and go straight to features.

2. **Nobody lets you TRY IT in the ad.** "Call this number right now" is unprecedented in this space. The demo line IS the ad.

3. **Nobody has the founder story.** A 16-year-old built an AI that answers calls? That's a pattern interrupt that no competitor can replicate.

4. **Nobody uses the price anchor.** "$97/mo = 730 hours" reframes the entire conversation. Competitors charge $200-500/mo and don't anchor against anything.

5. **Nobody runs 30-second vertical video.** The space is dominated by 60-90s landscape format. We're building for mobile-first Reels — where attention is.

### Budget Allocation

| Phase | Daily Budget | Duration | Goal |
|-------|-------------|----------|------|
| Testing | $5/ad × 5 ads = $25/day | 7 days | Find winning hook |

### KPI Targets

| Metric | Target | Kill Threshold |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| 3-Second View Rate | >40% | <25% (bad hook) |
| ThruPlay Rate | >15% | <8% |
| CTR | >2% | <0.8% |
| CPL | <$35 | >$50 (pause ad) |
| Cost per ThruPlay | <$0.10 | >$0.25 |
| Demo Line Calls from Ads | >3/day at scale | <1/week |

### Testing Protocol

**Week 1: Hook Test**
- Run all 5 scripts with $5/day each
- Measure 3-second view rate ONLY
- Kill anything below 30% thumb-stop
- Winner = highest 3-second view rate

**Week 2: Conversion Test**
- Take top 2 hooks, run with $15/day
- Measure CPL and demo line calls
- Split test CTA variants (tap-to-call vs learn more vs free pilot)

**Week 3: Scale**
- Winner gets $50/day
- Add Lookalike audience from demo line callers
- Add Creatomate variants (same hook, different verticals)

## 6. RETARGETING STRATEGY

### Audience Segments

1. **Demo Line Callers** — Highest intent. Retarget with Script 4 (social proof) and Script 5 (price anchor). These people have HEARD Gideon — now close them.

2. **Video Viewers (75%+)** — Watched most of the ad but didn't act. Retarget with different script (don't show same ad). Add urgency ("Only 3 pilot spots left").

3. **Website Visitors** — Landed on thecalltaker.com but didn't convert. Retarget with Script 4 ("Try it right now" — demo line CTA).

4. **Calculator Users** — Used the ROI calculator. They know the numbers. Retarget with Script 5 (price anchor reinforcement).

### Retarget Sequence
```
Day 1-3:  Different script than original touchpoint
Day 4-7:  Price anchor (Script 5) + urgency ("3 spots left")
Day 8-14: Testimonial/proof (case study link) + last chance
Day 15+:  Suppress (avoid fatigue)
```
