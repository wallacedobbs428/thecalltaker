# SCRIPT 05 — "What $97 Buys"

> Vertical: Universal (price anchor) | Length: 30s | Format: 9:16 Reels
> Tone: Fast, punchy, almost comedic — then hits hard with the value
> Goal: Destroy the "$97 is expensive" objection before it forms

---

## VISUAL STORYBOARD

### SECOND 0-1 | BLACK + QUESTION
- **Visual:** Pure black. Text appears: `"What does $97 buy?"`
- **Text:** Inter Black, 80px, white, center
- **Audio:** Silence, then a subtle "whoosh" as text appears
- **Music:** None

### SECOND 1-3 | FAST CUTS — THE COMPARISONS (HOOK)
- **Visual:** Rapid-fire cuts (0.5s each), each showing $97 worth of something underwhelming:
  - Cut 1: A single dinner plate at a restaurant. Text: `"One dinner out"`
  - Cut 2: Netflix logo on a TV. Text: `"3 months of Netflix"`
  - Cut 3: A gas pump showing $97.00. Text: `"One tank of gas"`
- **Text overlay:** Each comparison appears with the item, Inter Bold, 48px, white. Price `"$97"` appears in each frame in `#a3a3a3`.
- **Audio:** Quick "click" SFX on each cut. Fast, rhythmic.
- **Music:** Percussive beat begins — fast, energetic

### SECOND 3-7 | MORE COMPARISONS — GETTING ABSURD
- **Visual:** More rapid cuts, getting slightly more relatable to business owners:
  - Cut 4: A plumber's wrench on the ground. Text: `"Half a service call"`
  - Cut 5: A "Help Wanted" sign. Text: `"2 hours of a temp"`
  - Cut 6: A stack of business cards. Text: `"500 cards nobody reads"`
  - Cut 7: A Yellow Pages book (throwback). Text: `"One month of a dead ad"`
- **Text overlay:** Same style — item + `"$97"` in gray
- **Audio:** Same click rhythm, slightly faster now
- **Music:** Building — electronic, driving beat

### SECOND 7-9 | THE PAUSE
- **Visual:** Hard cut to black. 1.5 seconds of nothing. Then a single word fades in: `"OR"`
- **Text:** `"OR"` — Inter Black, 120px, `#00dc82`, center. Glows slightly.
- **Audio:** Music cuts. Complete silence. The "OR" lands like a punch.
- **Music:** Dead stop.

### SECOND 9-15 | THE REVEAL
- **Visual:** Clean, cinematic. A phone ringing. Gideon answering. Text builds on screen, one line at a time:
  - Line 1: `"730 hours"` — Inter Black, 80px, `#00dc82`, center
  - Line 2: `"of an AI receptionist"` — Inter Bold, 52px, white
  - Line 3: `"who never misses a call"` — Inter Bold, 52px, white
  Counter animation: "730 hours" counts up from 0.
- **Audio:** VO begins: "Or... seven hundred and thirty hours of an AI receptionist who never misses a single call."
- **Music:** Warm, confident electronic fades in. The hero reveal.

### SECOND 15-20 | WHAT JESSICA DOES
- **Visual:** Fast-cut montage of Gideon's capabilities, each with a green checkmark animation:
  - ✅ "Answers every call in under 2 seconds"
  - ✅ "Books appointments automatically"
  - ✅ "Sends follow-up texts"
  - ✅ "Works weekends, holidays, 3 AM"
  - ✅ "Knows your business inside and out"
  Each line appears for 1 second with a satisfying checkmark animation.
- **Text overlay:** Each capability stacks, staying on screen. Green checkmarks.
- **Audio:** VO: "She answers every call. Books appointments. Sends texts. Works at three AM on Christmas. And she knows your business better than a human receptionist."
- **Music:** Building energy, confident

### SECOND 20-23 | THE MATH
- **Visual:** Clean calculation on screen, each element appearing:
  - `"1 missed call = $200 - $1,500 lost"` — `#ef4444`
  - `"Gideon = $97/month"` — `#00dc82`
  - `"ROI:"` then a big number animating: `"2,000% +"` — Inter Black, 96px, `#00dc82`
- **Audio:** VO: "One missed call costs you two hundred to fifteen hundred dollars. Gideon costs ninety-seven. Do the math."
- **Music:** Peak — driving, rhythmic

### SECOND 23-27 | THE DEMO
- **Visual:** Phone number appears large. Clean dark background with green accent.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Hear her answer your call"` — Inter Bold, 52px, white
  - `"(629) 269-9697"` — Inter Black, 80px, `#00dc82`
  - `"Right now. Go ahead."` — Inter Medium, 40px, `#a3a3a3`
- **Audio:** VO: "Don't believe me? Call this number right now. She'll answer in under two seconds."
- **Music:** Confident, resolving

### SECOND 27-30 | CTA
- **Visual:** Full CTA bar. Behind it, a rapid replay of all the $97 comparisons from the beginning, now tiny and faded — reminding them of what else $97 buys (nothing good).
- **CTA bar:**
  - `"GET 730 HOURS FOR $97"` — Inter Black, 48px, black on `#00dc82`
  - `"14-day free pilot · Cancel anytime"` — Inter Medium, 28px, `rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`
- **Audio:** VO: "Seven hundred thirty hours of a receptionist. Ninety-seven dollars. You know what to do."
- **Music:** Final beat. Clean cut.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Fast-cut section (0-7s) can use stock footage or AI-generated images
- Each comparison should be slightly humorous — leaning into the absurdity of what $97 gets you
- The "OR" moment at second 7-9 is the emotional pivot — the silence must be FELT
- The 730 hours number (24 hours × 30.4 days) is the killer stat — it reframes everything
- This script works for any industry because it's about price perception, not a specific pain point
- Can easily adapt to $497 or $997 tiers: "What does $497 buy? Half a receptionist's salary. Or..."
