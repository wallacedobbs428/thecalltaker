# SCRIPT 01 — "The Missed Call"

> Vertical: Roofing (storm season) | Length: 30s | Format: 9:16 Reels
> Tone: Gut-punch → relief → urgency
> Goal: Make them feel the $1,200 they lost last week

---

## VISUAL STORYBOARD

### SECOND 0-1 | BLACK SCREEN
- **Visual:** Pure black. Text fades in: `"Last night at 11pm..."`
- **Text:** Inter Black, 80px, white, center screen
- **Audio:** Dead silence
- **Music:** None

### SECOND 1-3 | THE RING (HOOK)
- **Visual:** Close-up of an iPhone screen in a dark bedroom. Screen lights up — incoming call: "Storm Damage Emergency — Homeowner"
- **Text overlay:** None — let the visual speak
- **Audio:** iPhone ringtone (2 rings), loud in the silence
- **Music:** None — silence is the hook
- **SFX:** Phone vibrating on nightstand

### SECOND 3-5 | THE MISS
- **Visual:** Phone stops ringing. Screen shows "Missed Call" notification. Beat of silence. Then "Voicemail" notification appears.
- **Text overlay:** None yet
- **Audio:** Silence after ring stops. Subtle low bass tone.
- **Music:** Dark, minimal tension pad begins (BPM: 80)

### SECOND 5-8 | THE COST
- **Visual:** Same dark phone screen. Text overlay appears with a typewriter effect:
- **Text overlay:**
  - Line 1: `"That was a"` — Inter SemiBold, 48px, white
  - Line 2: `"$1,200"` — Inter Black, 96px, `#ef4444` (red), animates counting up from $0
  - Line 3: `"storm damage job"` — Inter SemiBold, 48px, white
- **Audio:** Subtle bass drop on the $1,200 reveal
- **Music:** Tension builds — dark synth pad

### SECOND 8-10 | THE TURN
- **Visual:** Quick cut to black. Single text: `"But what if..."`
- **Text:** Inter Bold, 64px, white, center. Fades in softly.
- **Audio:** Music transitions — tension releases into upbeat
- **Music:** Key change — dark → bright electronic

### SECOND 10-15 | JESSICA ANSWERS
- **Visual:** Same bedroom, same phone, same time. But this time — the call connects. Split screen or overlay shows a clean UI: "Jessica — AI Receptionist" answering. Chat bubbles appear showing the conversation:
  - Jessica: "Thank you for calling [Company], this is Jessica..."
  - Caller: "We just had a tree come through our roof..."
  - Jessica: "I'm so sorry to hear that. Let me get a crew scheduled for first thing tomorrow morning."
- **Text overlay:** `"Jessica answered in 1.2 seconds"` — Inter Bold, 44px, `#00dc82` (green), bottom third
- **Audio:** Notification ding when Jessica answers
- **Music:** Upbeat, modern electronic (BPM: 120), rising energy

### SECOND 15-20 | THE BOOKING
- **Visual:** Clean appointment confirmation UI: "Tomorrow 8:00 AM — Storm Damage Assessment — $1,200 estimate". Green checkmark animation.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"$1,200 job"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82` (green)
  - `"Booked while you slept"` — Inter SemiBold, 48px, white
- **Audio:** Cash register "ka-ching" SFX on the $1,200
- **Music:** Peak energy

### SECOND 20-25 | THE OFFER
- **Visual:** Clean dark background. The Call Taker logo fades in. Then the demo line number appears large.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Hear Jessica answer right now"` — Inter Bold, 52px, white
  - `"(615) 784-5747"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82` on dark pill
  - `"Free 14-day pilot · No card required"` — Inter Medium, 36px, `#a3a3a3`
- **Audio:** VO: "Call this number right now. Jessica will answer in under 2 seconds."
- **Music:** Resolving, energy maintaining

### SECOND 25-30 | CTA BAR
- **Visual:** Full-width green bar slides up from bottom.
- **CTA bar:**
  - Background: `#00dc82`
  - Text: `"TAP TO CALL JESSICA NOW"` — Inter Black, 52px, `#000000`
  - Below: `"$97/mo · Cancel anytime"` — Inter SemiBold, 32px, `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- **Audio:** VO: "Ninety-seven dollars a month. That's less than one missed call."
- **Music:** Final note, clean fade

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (word-for-word)

> **Voice:** Male, mid-30s, calm authority. Think podcast host, not car salesman.
> **Pace:** Slow and deliberate seconds 0-8. Picks up energy 8-20. Confident close 20-30.

```
[0-3]   (silence — phone ring only)

[3-8]   "That call? That was twelve hundred dollars...
         walking away from your business."

[8-15]  "But with Jessica — your AI receptionist —
         that call gets answered in under two seconds.
         Even at 11 PM on a Saturday."

[15-20] "She books the appointment. Sends the confirmation.
         And you wake up to a twelve-hundred-dollar job
         already on your calendar."

[20-25] "Call this number right now.
         Jessica will answer. Live. In under two seconds."

[25-30] "Ninety-seven dollars a month.
         That's less than one missed call."
```

## MUSIC DIRECTION

- **0-8s:** Dark tension pad. Minimal. Low rumble. Think "Inception" but quiet.
- **8-15s:** Transition at second 8 — key change, tempo increase to 120 BPM. Clean electronic.
- **15-25s:** Peak energy. Modern startup/tech feel. Confident.
- **25-30s:** Music pulls back. Single sustained note. Voice carries the close.

**Recommended tracks (Artlist search terms):** "dark to light transition", "tech reveal", "startup cinematic"
