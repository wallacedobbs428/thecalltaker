# SCRIPT 02 — "Side by Side"

> Vertical: HVAC | Length: 30s | Format: 9:16 Reels
> Tone: Direct comparison — make the choice obvious
> Goal: Show the before/after in real-time, same scenario, two outcomes

---

## VISUAL STORYBOARD

### SECOND 0-1 | BLACK + TEXT
- **Visual:** Pure black. Text punches in: `"Saturday. 9 PM."`
- **Text:** Inter Black, 80px, white, center
- **Audio:** Silence

### SECOND 1-3 | THE SPLIT (HOOK)
- **Visual:** Screen splits vertically — hard line down the middle.
  - LEFT side: Red tint. Label at top: `"WITHOUT"` in `#ef4444`
  - RIGHT side: Green tint. Label at top: `"WITH JESSICA"` in `#00dc82`
  - Both sides show an iPhone receiving a call: "AC Emergency — Homeowner"
- **Text overlay:** Labels only
- **Audio:** Phone ring (shared across both sides)
- **Music:** None — just the ring

### SECOND 3-6 | THE DIVERGENCE
- **Visual:**
  - LEFT: Phone rings 4 times → goes to voicemail. Voicemail greeting plays. Caller hangs up immediately.
  - RIGHT: Ring once → Gideon answers: "Thank you for calling [HVAC Co], this is Gideon. How can I help?"
- **Text overlay:**
  - LEFT: `"Ring... ring... ring... voicemail"` — Inter SemiBold, 40px, `#ef4444`, typewriter effect
  - RIGHT: `"Answered in 1.2 seconds"` — Inter SemiBold, 40px, `#00dc82`
- **Audio:** Left side: voicemail beep. Right side: Gideon's voice.
- **Music:** Subtle tension on left, warmth on right. Split-channel audio.

### SECOND 6-10 | THE OUTCOME
- **Visual:**
  - LEFT: Phone screen shows notification: "Voicemail from Unknown (0:03)". Three-second hang-up. Nothing happens.
  - RIGHT: Chat UI shows Gideon booking: "Tomorrow 8 AM confirmed. I'll send you a text with the details."
- **Text overlay:**
  - LEFT: `"$350 job — gone"` — Inter Black, 64px, `#ef4444`, center of left half
  - RIGHT: `"$350 job — booked"` — Inter Black, 64px, `#00dc82`, center of right half
- **Audio:** LEFT: sad trombone / silence. RIGHT: booking confirmation ding.
- **Music:** Building — electronic, modern

### SECOND 10-15 | THE MULTIPLIER
- **Visual:** Split screen stays but LEFT fades to dark. Text overlay cascades down the LEFT side showing all the calls that went to voicemail this week:
  - "Monday 7pm — $280 repair"
  - "Tuesday 9pm — $420 install quote"
  - "Thursday 11pm — $350 emergency"
  - "Saturday 9pm — $350 emergency"
  Total counter adds up in red.
- **Text overlay on LEFT:**
  - Stack of missed jobs, fade-in one by one (0.5s each)
  - Running total at bottom: `"$1,400 lost this week"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#ef4444`
- **Text overlay on RIGHT:**
  - Same stack but all green checkmarks
  - Running total: `"$1,400 booked this week"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82`
- **Audio:** Counter ticking sound on each addition
- **Music:** Energy building

### SECOND 15-20 | ZOOM INTO SOLUTION
- **Visual:** LEFT side slides away. RIGHT side expands to full screen. Clean view of Gideon's UI — calendar filling up with appointments, texts going out automatically.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Gideon works 24/7"` — Inter Bold, 56px, white
  - `"No sick days. No overtime. No missed calls."` — Inter SemiBold, 40px, `#a3a3a3`
- **Audio:** VO: "Gideon never sleeps. Never takes a break. Never sends a call to voicemail."
- **Music:** Peak — confident, forward-moving

### SECOND 20-25 | THE OFFER
- **Visual:** Clean dark background. Large phone number.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Call Gideon right now"` — Inter Bold, 56px, white
  - `"(629) 269-9697"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82`
  - `"She'll answer before you blink"` — Inter Medium, 36px, `#a3a3a3`
- **Audio:** VO: "Call six-one-five, seven-eight-four, fifty-seven-forty-seven. She'll answer before the second ring."
- **Music:** Resolving

### SECOND 25-30 | CTA
- **Visual:** Full-width green bar from bottom. Split-screen thumbnail freeze-frame behind it (reminder of the contrast).
- **CTA bar:**
  - `"START YOUR FREE PILOT"` — Inter Black, 52px, black on `#00dc82`
  - `"14 days free · No card · No contract"` — Inter SemiBold, 28px, `rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`
- **Audio:** VO: "Fourteen days free. No card required. See the difference tonight."
- **Music:** Final resolve, clean cut
