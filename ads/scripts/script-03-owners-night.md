# SCRIPT 03 — "The Owner's Night"

> Vertical: Universal (any service business) | Length: 30s | Format: 9:16 Reels
> Tone: Cinematic, emotional, aspirational
> Goal: Make them feel the anxiety of missed calls, then the peace of Gideon

---

## VISUAL STORYBOARD

### SECOND 0-1 | TIME STAMP
- **Visual:** Black screen. Digital clock reads `11:47 PM`. Fades in.
- **Text:** Inter Black, 96px, white, center
- **Audio:** Silence. Single clock tick.
- **Music:** None

### SECOND 1-3 | GOING TO SLEEP (HOOK)
- **Visual:** POV shot — a tired business owner setting their phone on a nightstand. The phone screen shows 2 missed calls already. They sigh, plug in the charger, turn off the lamp. Darkness.
- **Text overlay:** None — cinematic, no text yet
- **Audio:** Ambient room sounds. Phone being placed down. Lamp click off.
- **Music:** Barely audible low drone begins

### SECOND 3-6 | THE CALLS COME IN
- **Visual:** Time-lapse effect. Clock changes: 12:15 AM → 1:30 AM → 3:45 AM. Each time, the phone screen lights up briefly in the dark room. Three separate calls, three glows, three voicemail notifications. The owner sleeps through all of them.
- **Text overlay (appears after 3rd call):**
  - `"3 calls."` — Inter Bold, 56px, white, fade in
  - `"3 voicemails."` — Inter Bold, 56px, white, fade in below
  - `"3 customers... gone."` — Inter Bold, 56px, `#ef4444`, fade in below
- **Audio:** Muffled phone vibration (x3), each softer than the last
- **Music:** Tension building — low strings, minimal electronic

### SECOND 6-10 | THE MORNING
- **Visual:** Morning. Sunlight. Owner picks up phone. Face drops. Close-up of phone screen:
  - "3 Missed Calls"
  - "2 New Voicemails"
  - "1 Text: 'nvm called someone else'"
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"$2,400 in jobs"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#ef4444`
  - `"Lost while you slept"` — Inter SemiBold, 48px, white
- **Audio:** Morning alarm briefly, then somber tone
- **Music:** Tension peaks, then cuts to silence at second 10

### SECOND 10-11 | THE TURN
- **Visual:** Hard cut to black. Single word: `"Unless."`
- **Text:** Inter Black, 96px, `#00dc82`, center
- **Audio:** Complete silence (1 second)
- **Music:** None — the silence IS the transition

### SECOND 11-18 | THE JESSICA VERSION
- **Visual:** Same night. Same bedroom. Same phone. But now there's a subtle green glow on the screen. Clock: 12:15 AM — phone lights up, but instead of "Missed Call" it shows:
  - "Gideon answered — Emergency lockout"
  - "Appointment booked: Tomorrow 7 AM"
  Clock: 1:30 AM —
  - "Gideon answered — AC not working"
  - "Appointment booked: Tomorrow 9 AM"
  Clock: 3:45 AM —
  - "Gideon answered — Pipe burst"
  - "Emergency dispatch confirmed"
  The owner sleeps peacefully through all three. Green notification badges stack up.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"3 calls answered"` — Inter Bold, 48px, `#00dc82`
  - `"3 appointments booked"` — Inter Bold, 48px, `#00dc82`
  - `"0 effort from you"` — Inter Bold, 48px, white
- **Audio:** Soft notification dings (positive), owner breathing peacefully
- **Music:** Warm, gentle electronic. "Everything is handled" energy. BPM: 100

### SECOND 18-22 | THE MORNING (VERSION 2)
- **Visual:** Same morning. Same sunlight. Owner picks up phone. But this time — a SMILE. Phone shows:
  - "3 appointments booked overnight"
  - "$2,400 in jobs scheduled"
  - "Good morning! Your calendar for today: 7 AM, 9 AM, 11 AM"
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"$2,400 booked"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82`
  - `"Before your alarm went off"` — Inter SemiBold, 44px, white
- **Audio:** VO: "Twenty-four hundred dollars in jobs. Booked before your alarm went off."
- **Music:** Building to peak — upbeat, aspirational

### SECOND 22-27 | THE OFFER
- **Visual:** Clean dark background. Phone number prominent.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Meet Gideon"` — Inter Bold, 56px, white
  - `"(629) 269-9697"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82`
  - `"Your AI receptionist that never sleeps"` — Inter Medium, 36px, `#a3a3a3`
- **Audio:** VO: "Call six-one-five, seven-eight-four, fifty-seven-forty-seven. Meet Gideon. She answers every call so you can actually sleep."
- **Music:** Confident resolution

### SECOND 27-30 | CTA
- **Visual:** Green CTA bar slides up. Behind it, a freeze-frame of the peaceful sleeping owner with green phone notifications.
- **CTA bar:**
  - `"TRY JESSICA FREE — 14 DAYS"` — Inter Black, 48px, black on `#00dc82`
  - `"$97/mo after trial · Cancel anytime"` — Inter Medium, 28px, `rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`
- **Audio:** VO: "Free for fourteen days. Because you deserve to sleep."
- **Music:** Single warm note. Fade.

- **3-10s:** Dark, sparse piano + subtle electronic. "Night anxiety" mood.
- **10-11s:** Complete silence (the "Unless" moment)
- **11-18s:** Warm, gentle electronic. Lullaby meets tech. Piano returns but in major key.
- **18-27s:** Building confidence. Modern, clean electronic. Aspirational.
