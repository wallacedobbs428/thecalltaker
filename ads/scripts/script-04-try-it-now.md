# SCRIPT 04 — "Try It Right Now"

> Vertical: Universal (demo-first) | Length: 30s | Format: 9:16 Reels
> Tone: Raw, authentic, young founder energy — Wallace speaking directly to camera
> Goal: Get them to call the demo line RIGHT NOW while watching the ad

---

## VISUAL STORYBOARD

### SECOND 0-3 | DIRECT TO CAMERA (HOOK)
- **Visual:** Wallace (16yo founder) looking straight into camera. No fancy setup — iPhone selfie cam, natural lighting, maybe a garage or simple office. Raw and real. He starts talking immediately — no intro, no greeting.
- **Text overlay:** `"I'm 16. I built this."` — Inter Black, 64px, white, top third, appears at second 1
- **Audio:** Wallace speaking directly. No music. No effects. Raw audio.
- **Music:** None — authenticity IS the hook

### SECOND 3-7 | THE SETUP
- **Visual:** Still direct to camera. Wallace is animated, confident, slightly leaning in. Hand gestures. Energy of someone who KNOWS they built something good.
- **Text overlay:** Key phrases appear as he says them:
  - `"AI receptionist"` — Inter Bold, 48px, `#00dc82`, flashes in
  - `"answers your calls 24/7"` — Inter Bold, 48px, white
- **Audio:** Wallace: "I built an AI receptionist that answers your phone calls 24/7. And it sounds so real that nobody can tell the difference."
- **Music:** None — still raw

### SECOND 7-10 | THE CHALLENGE
- **Visual:** Wallace holds up his phone to the camera. The phone fills 60% of the screen. On it: the demo line number is visible.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Call this number"` — Inter Black, 64px, white
  - `"RIGHT NOW"` — Inter Black, 80px, `#00dc82`, pulse animation
  - `"(629) 269-9697"` — Inter Black, 72px, white on green pill
- **Audio:** Wallace: "Don't take my word for it. Call this number RIGHT NOW while you're watching this."
- **Music:** Single low beat drops — attention-grabbing but subtle

### SECOND 10-15 | JESSICA ANSWERS LIVE
- **Visual:** Screen recording of an actual call to the demo line. Shows:
  - Dialing animation
  - Call connects (under 2 seconds)
  - Gideon's voice plays over the video
  - Show the call timer: 0:01, 0:02... she answers almost instantly
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"1.2 seconds"` — Inter Black, 72px, `#00dc82`, appears when Gideon answers
  - `"That's Gideon."` — Inter Bold, 48px, white
- **Audio:** Actual Gideon audio from demo line: "Hey, thanks for calling! This is Gideon. How can I help you today?"
- **Music:** Subtle upbeat electronic begins — "this is cool" energy

### SECOND 15-20 | THE CONVERSATION
- **Visual:** Continue the screen recording. Show Gideon handling a realistic scenario — someone says "my AC broke" and Gideon responds naturally, books an appointment, gets their info. Split between screen recording and Wallace's reaction face (impressed/proud).
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"She books appointments"` — Inter Bold, 44px, `#00dc82`
  - `"She answers questions"` — Inter Bold, 44px, `#00dc82`
  - `"She never takes a day off"` — Inter Bold, 44px, white
  (Stack these, appear one by one, 1.5s apart)
- **Audio:** Gideon's actual conversation from the demo call
- **Music:** Building — clean electronic, confident

### SECOND 20-25 | THE CLOSE
- **Visual:** Back to Wallace, direct to camera. He leans in even closer. Gets serious for a moment.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"$97/mo"` — Inter Black, 80px, `#00dc82`
  - `"Less than one missed call"` — Inter SemiBold, 44px, white
- **Audio:** Wallace: "Ninety-seven bucks a month. That's literally less than one missed call costs your business. And I'll let you try it free for two weeks."
- **Music:** Building to peak

### SECOND 25-28 | THE DARE
- **Visual:** Wallace smiles, holds phone up again. Phone number fills the screen.
- **Text overlay:**
  - `"Go ahead. Call her."` — Inter Bold, 56px, white
  - `"(629) 269-9697"` — Inter Black, 80px, `#00dc82`, large and centered
- **Audio:** Wallace: "Go ahead. Call her. Right now. I dare you."
- **Music:** Peak energy, then pulls back

### SECOND 28-30 | CTA
- **Visual:** Green CTA bar slides up. Wallace's face still visible behind it, smiling confidently.
- **CTA bar:**
  - `"TAP TO CALL JESSICA"` — Inter Black, 52px, black on `#00dc82`
  - `"Free 14-day pilot · Built by a 16-year-old"` — Inter Medium, 28px, `rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`
- **Audio:** Wallace: "I built this at sixteen. Imagine what she'll do for your business."
- **Music:** Clean resolve, fade

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **This script is designed to be filmed on an iPhone by Wallace.** No professional equipment needed.
- Film in natural light, casual setting (not a studio)
- One take is ideal — the rawness is the entire point
- Screen recording of the demo call should be captured separately and edited in
- The "I'm 16" angle is a massive pattern interrupt on Facebook — lean into it hard
- This will likely be the highest-performing script because it's the most authentic
- If Wallace isn't comfortable on camera, this can be adapted to voiceover-only with screen recordings
