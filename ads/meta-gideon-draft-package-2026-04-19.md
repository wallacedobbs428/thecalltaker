## Targeting Recommendations

### Core Audience 1 — Service Business Owners

- Age: `28-60`
- Locations: United States, start with top-performing service metros or service radius clusters
- Languages: English
- Detailed targeting:
  - Small business owners
  - Engaged shoppers
  - Field service management
  - Housecall Pro
  - Jobber
  - ServiceTitan
  - QuickBooks
  - Google Business Profile
  - Business Page Admins
  - Facebook Page Admins
  - HVAC
  - Plumbing
  - Roofing
  - Locksmith
  - Electrical contractor
  - Towing
  - Property management
  - Dental practice management
  - Chiropractic
  - Law firm

### Core Audience 2 — Operational Pain / Hiring Pain

- Interests / behaviors:
  - Hiring
  - Recruiting
  - Payroll
  - Staffing
  - Call center
  - Answering service
  - CRM software
  - Scheduling software
  - Local services ads

### Retargeting

- Website visitors 30/60/90 days
- Video viewers 25%+ and 50%+
- Meta lead form opens but no submit
- Demo line clickers / call intent traffic if event tracking exists

### Lookalikes

- 1% to 3% lookalikes from:
  - Qualified demo signups
  - Won customers
  - High-intent callers
  - Website leads that reached booking page

### Exclusions

- Existing customers
- Internal team
- Agency partners if not relevant
- Low-fit consumer audiences
- Job seekers if hiring intent starts polluting traffic

## Budget + Bidding

- Testing budget: `$100-$150/day` across 2 to 3 ad sets
- Allocation:
  - 60% core owner audience
  - 25% retargeting
  - 15% lookalike
- Bid strategy:
  - Start with `Highest volume`
  - Add cost controls only after stable lead flow
- Optimization notes:
  - Kill obvious low-CTR / low-quality creative fast
  - Refresh hooks before changing the entire audience stack
  - Hold one control ad with the cleanest direct-response message
