## Quick Reference: The Ask and The Introduction Script

### What you're asking for:
An introduction (via text, email, or in person) to anyone who owns or manages an HVAC company.

### What they should say when introducing:
Keep it to three things:
1. **Who Wallace is:** "My friend/colleague Wallace Dobbs"
2. **What it does:** "He built an AI that answers phone calls for HVAC companies 24/7 so they don't miss business"
3. **Why it's worth their time:** "He does a free audit of your phones to show you where you're losing calls - no cost, no commitment"

### Universal copy-paste intro (for text or email):

> "Hey [HVAC owner name] - want to introduce you to Wallace Dobbs. He built an AI phone system specifically for HVAC companies. It answers every call, books appointments, and makes sure nothing goes to voicemail. He does a free audit where he tests your phone setup and shows you exactly where calls are slipping through. No cost. Figured it was worth a look. Wallace, meet [HVAC owner name]. Connecting you two."

### After the intro is made:
- Wallace responds within 1 hour during business hours
- Leads with the free audit, not a sales pitch
- Always follows up with the person who made the intro to let them know how it went
- Never embarrasses the referrer with aggressive sales tactics
