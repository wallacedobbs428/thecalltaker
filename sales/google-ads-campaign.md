## 4. BUDGET RECOMMENDATION

### Daily Budget

| Phase | Duration | Daily Budget | Monthly Spend |
|-------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Testing / Learning | Weeks 1-4 | $50/day | ~$1,500/mo |
| Optimization | Weeks 5-8 | $75/day | ~$2,250/mo |
| Scaling (if ROAS positive) | Weeks 9+ | $100-150/day | ~$3,000-4,500/mo |

### Bid Strategy

| Setting | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Initial strategy (weeks 1-2) | Maximize Clicks with $15 max CPC | Gather data quickly across all ad groups |
| Transition strategy (weeks 3-4) | Maximize Conversions | Let Google optimize once 15+ conversions recorded |
| Mature strategy (week 5+) | Target CPA at $75-100 | Optimize for cost-per-lead once sufficient conversion data exists |

**Conversion tracking setup required:**
- Primary: Demo line calls (30+ seconds)
- Primary: Website form submissions / sign-ups
- Secondary: Click-to-call from ad
- Secondary: Landing page engagement (time on site 60s+)

### Geographic Targeting

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|
| Target | United States (all states) |
| Exclusions | Alaska, Hawaii (optional - lower HVAC density) |
| Location options | People IN or REGULARLY IN targeted locations |
| Bid adjustments | +20% for Sun Belt states (TX, FL, AZ, GA, NC, SC, TN, AL, LA) where HVAC demand is highest year-round |
| Bid adjustments | +15% for top metro areas (population 500K+) |
| Bid adjustments | -10% for rural areas with low HVAC business density |

### Device Targeting

| Device | Bid Adjustment | Rationale |

## Campaign Performance Benchmarks

### Expected Metrics (Industry Baseline for B2B SaaS / Answering Services)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | 4-7% |
| Cost per click (CPC) | $6-12 |
| Conversion rate (landing page) | 5-10% |
| Cost per lead | $60-120 |
| Cost per customer acquired | $300-600 |
| Customer lifetime value (12 mo) | $3,564 - $5,964 |
| Target ROAS | 6:1 - 10:1 |

### Optimization Checklist (Weekly)

- [ ] Pause keywords with 100+ clicks and zero conversions
- [ ] Add converting search terms as exact match keywords
- [ ] Add irrelevant search terms as negative keywords
- [ ] Review ad copy performance and pause underperformers (CTR below 3%)
- [ ] Check device and location bid adjustments against conversion data
- [ ] Verify landing page load speed (under 3 seconds)
- [ ] Review competitor keyword performance separately (typically lower ROAS)
- [ ] Test new headline and description variations monthly

### A/B Testing Roadmap

| Test | Variable | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Test 1 | Price in headline vs. no price | 2 weeks |
| Test 2 | Demo line CTA vs. website CTA | 2 weeks |
| Test 3 | Pain-point headlines vs. benefit headlines | 2 weeks |
| Test 4 | Industry-specific ("HVAC") vs. broader ("contractor") | 2 weeks |

6. Add all extensions (sitelinks, callouts, snippets, call)
7. Set geographic and device bid adjustments
8. Configure ad schedule
