## Why Plumbers?

Plumbers are the perfect referral channel for The Call Taker. Here is why:

- Plumbers and HVAC techs share job sites daily (new construction, remodels, commercial buildouts)
- They share the same customers -- homeowners who need both plumbing and HVAC work
- They hang out at the same supply houses (Ferguson, Johnstone, Winsupply, etc.)
- They attend the same trade shows and local contractor meetups
- They hear HVAC company owners complain about the same problems: missed calls, losing leads, not enough office staff
- Plumbers are NOT competitors to HVAC companies, so there is zero conflict of interest

A single well-connected plumbing company owner probably knows 5-15 HVAC company owners personally. That is a goldmine.

## Quick-Reference: Best Places to Find Plumbing Company Owners

1. **Supply houses** -- Ferguson, Johnstone Supply, Winsupply, HD Supply. Go during early morning (6:30-8:00 AM) when plumbers are picking up parts.
2. **Local plumbing association meetings** -- Check your area chapter of PHCC (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors).
3. **Trade shows** -- AHR Expo, local home shows, contractor expos.
4. **Facebook groups** -- "Plumbers of [Your City]", "[City] Contractors Network", etc.
5. **Home Advisor / Angi / Thumbtack** -- Look up top-rated plumbers in your area and reach out.
6. **New construction job sites** -- Plumbers and HVAC are often there at the same phase of the build.
7. **Contractor breakfasts / networking groups** -- BNI, local chamber events, etc.
