## Post 4: The Value Post

**Post in:** HVAC Business Owners & Entrepreneurs, HVAC School
**Best time to post:** Tuesday, 11:00 AM-12:00 PM

```
3 things you can do THIS WEEK to stop losing calls to voicemail (none of these cost money):

1. Change your voicemail greeting right now. Stop saying "leave a message and we'll call you back." Instead say: "Hey, thanks for calling [your company]. We return every call within 2 hours during business days. If this is an emergency, press 1 and we'll get right back to you." Specificity builds trust. "We'll call you back" means nothing.

2. Turn on missed call text-back. Most phone systems or even Google Voice can auto-send a text when you miss a call. Something simple like "Hey, sorry we missed you. We got your call and will ring you back within 30 minutes. Need faster help? Reply here." That one text keeps people from calling your competitor.

3. Track your missed calls for one week. Just write down every missed call -- time, day, whether they left a voicemail. You can't fix what you don't measure. I promise the number will surprise you.

None of this requires software or spending money. Just a little attention to the front door of your business. The phone IS the front door.

What else is working for you guys? Always looking for more ideas.
```

**Do not:** Pitch anything. This is pure value. You are the helpful guy in the group. When people comment things like "this is great advice," reply and build the relationship. That goodwill pays off later.
