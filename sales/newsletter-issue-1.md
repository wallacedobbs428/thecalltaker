### The Part Where I Tell You Something You Can Actually Do Today

I'm not here to just scare you with math. I want to give you something useful in every issue. So here's something you can set up in the next 15 minutes that will immediately reduce the number of calls you're losing:

**Set up call forwarding on your business line.**

I know. It sounds too simple. But you'd be amazed how many HVAC companies have never done this. Here's the play:

1. **Call your phone provider** (or log into your account online -- most carriers let you do this from the app now).

2. **Set up "no-answer forwarding."** This means if nobody picks up after 3-4 rings, the call automatically forwards to another number.

3. **Forward it to your cell phone.** Or to your most senior tech's cell phone. Or to your spouse. Literally anyone who can say "Thank you for calling, let me get some information and we'll get someone out to you."

4. **Set it up especially for after-hours.** Most phone systems let you set different rules for different times of day. Forward to the office during business hours, forward to a cell after 5 PM and on weekends.

That's it. Fifteen minutes of setup. Zero dollars per month.

Is it a perfect solution? No. You're going to get calls while you're at dinner. You're going to get calls while you're at your kid's ballgame. And eventually, you're going to stop answering those forwarded calls, and you'll be right back where you started.

But for right now, today, this will plug the biggest hole in your bucket. And it'll give you a front-row seat to something important: **you'll finally see how many calls you've actually been missing.**

That number is going to surprise you. And once you see it, you'll understand why this matters so much.
