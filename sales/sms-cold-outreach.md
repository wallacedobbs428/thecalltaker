## Sending Rules

- **Best days to text HVAC owners:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.
- **Best times:** 7-8:30 AM before they head to jobs, or 5:30-7 PM after the day winds down.
- **Always text right after a voicemail.** Never leave a voicemail without following up with a text.
- **Never send more than one text in a day.** You are a person, not a drip campaign.
- **Use their name when you have it.** "Hey Mike" beats "Hey" every time.
- **If they respond with anything other than "stop" or "not interested," keep the conversation going.** Even "who is this?" is an opening.
- **If they say "not interested," respond with:** "Totally get it. If anything changes you've got my number. Take care." Leave the door open.
- **If they say "stop" or "unsubscribe,"** immediately remove them. Respond with "Done. You're removed. Sorry for the bother." Never try to squeeze in a last pitch.
- **Save their responses.** The words they use tell you what they care about. Mirror that language back in your next message.
- **Track response rates.** After 50+ texts you'll know which angle pulls the best. Double down on what works.
