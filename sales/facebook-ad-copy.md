## Testing Plan

**Week 1:** Run all 4 ad sets with $10/day each ($40/day total, $280/week)
**Week 2:** Kill the bottom 2 performers. Double budget on top 2.
**Week 3:** Create 2 variations of the winner. Test new images.
**Week 4:** Scale the winner to $50-100/day.

**Key metrics to watch:**
- Cost per click (CPC) — target under $2
- Click-through rate (CTR) — target above 1.5%
- Cost per demo booked — target under $50
- Cost per paying customer — target under $500 (that means you profit in month 2)
