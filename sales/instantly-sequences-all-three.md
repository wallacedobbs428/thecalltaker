- Interested ("tell me more", "sounds good", "what's the cost"): Stop sequence. Move to personal reply. Use sales playbook — pain → demo line → price last.
- Price question first: Frame it. "$497/month — that's less than one service call. But before I give you a number, can I ask — how many calls do you think you're missing per week?"
- Skeptical ("AI won't work for my customers"): Acknowledge. "Totally fair. That's why I set up a demo line — call (615) 784-5747 yourself and tell me what you think."
- Not interested: Reply warmly, wish them luck, remove from sequences. Never push.
- No reply after all 7: Wait 30 days, then re-enroll in a different sequence angle.
