## Content Rules

1. **Never use hashtags excessively.** One or two max if relevant (#HVAC, #smallbusiness). Twitter/X algorithm doesn't reward hashtag stuffing.
2. **Post between 7-9 AM Central.** HVAC owners check their phones early before heading to jobs.
3. **Engage with every reply.** Month one is about building relationships, not broadcasting.
4. **Retweet/quote relevant HVAC content.** Industry news, extreme weather reports, relatable contractor content.
5. **Pin Tweet 5 or Tweet 20** to the profile so visitors immediately see the value prop and demo number.
6. **Thread potential:** Tweets 7, 12, and 17 can be expanded into longer threads if they get traction.
7. **No emojis in every tweet.** Use them sparingly if at all. The tone is direct and professional, not bubbly.
8. **Alternate between short punchy tweets and longer story tweets.** Variety keeps the feed interesting.
