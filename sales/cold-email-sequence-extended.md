## Sequence Notes

### Timing Strategy
- **Days 1-7 (Emails 1-3):** Tight spacing. Three emails in one week. Initial push while you're top of mind.
- **Days 10-22 (Emails 4-7):** Wider spacing. Give them room to breathe. Pushing too hard here kills the deal.
- **Total sequence length:** 22 days from first email to breakup.

### Best Send Times
| Email | Time (CT) | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| 4 -- Free Audit | 5:00-6:00 PM | End of day. Thinking about the calls they might've missed. |
| 5 -- Numbers | 6:00-7:00 AM | Math sticks with them all day on the job. |
| 6 -- Video | 12:00-1:00 PM | Lunch break. Sitting in the truck. Will actually watch a video. |
| 7 -- Breakup | 6:00-7:00 AM | Fresh mind. All day to think about it and reply. |

### Instantly.ai Setup
- Load emails 1-3 (from cold-email-sequence.md) and emails 4-7 (from this file) into a single sequence.
- Set delays: Day 1, 4, 7, 10, 14, 18, 22.
- Enable open tracking and click tracking.
- Use warmed-up sending domain.
- Daily send limit: 30-40 per sending account.
- Merge tags: {{firstName}} for [First Name], {{companyName}} for [Company Name].
- Auto-stop on reply is ON.

### Subject Line Notes
- **Email 4** ("Want me to prove it? Free.") -- Curiosity + zero commitment. The word "free" does the heavy lifting.
- **Email 5** ("85% of your callers won't leave a voicemail") -- Stat-driven. Pattern interrupt. Makes them think.
- **Email 6** ("I recorded something that might surprise you") -- Curiosity gap. Feels personal, not mass-sent.
- **Email 7** ("closing your file") -- Lowercase on purpose. Looks like an internal note they weren't supposed to see. Loss aversion drives opens. Breakup emails consistently get the highest open and reply rates in any sequence.

### A/B Test Alternatives
- Email 4 alt subject: "Free missed call audit for [Company Name]"
- Email 5 alt subject: "The $54,000 problem nobody talks about"
- Email 6 alt subject: "Quick video for you [First Name]"
- Email 7 alt subject: "Should I stop reaching out?"

### What Happens After Email 7
- **If they reply at any point:** Stop the sequence. Move to personal conversation. Use demo-script.md to book the call.
- **If they reply to the breakup email:** This is your hottest lead. Breakup emails convert 2-3x higher than any other email. Respond within 30 minutes. Be warm, not desperate.
- **If they reply "send it" to Email 6:** Send the Loom video. Record it once using loom-video-script.md and reuse for every prospect. Keep it under 2 minutes.
- **If they request the audit (Email 4):** Run it using secret-shopper-template.md and send them missed-call-audit-report.md.
- **If no reply after all 7:** Mark as "cold -- completed sequence" in GHL. Wait 60 days. Try one more touchpoint -- cold call (cold-call-script.md) or text (sms-follow-up-sequences.md). If still nothing, move on. Some people aren't ready yet.

### Key Reminders
- If you say Email 7 is your last email, make it your last email. Your word is your brand.
- Email 6 needs a Loom video recorded. Do it once, reuse it. Casual -- jeans and a t-shirt, not a suit. Show your face for 10 seconds, then screen-share the two calls.
- If you secret-shopped them before the sequence, reference specific details in Email 4: "When I called Tuesday at 6:45pm, it rang 8 times and went to a generic voicemail."
- All times are Central Time. Adjust for prospects outside Tennessee.
- Never send more than one email per day to the same prospect.
