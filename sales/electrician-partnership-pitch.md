## Quick-Reference: Best Places to Find Electrical Contractors

1. **Job sites** -- New construction and remodel sites where electrical and HVAC rough-in happen simultaneously. Perfect captive audience during downtime.
2. **Electrical supply houses** -- Graybar, CED, Rexel, WESCO, local independent distributors. Early mornings (6:00-8:00 AM) when electricians are grabbing wire and breakers.
3. **Code inspection waiting areas** -- Electrical and HVAC inspections often happen the same day on the same job. Contractors sit around waiting for the inspector together.
4. **Local IBEW or IEC chapter meetings** -- International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers or Independent Electrical Contractors events.
5. **Trade shows** -- Local home shows, contractor expos, electrical industry events.
6. **Facebook groups** -- "Electricians of [City]", "[State] Electrical Contractors", local contractor networking groups.
7. **General contractor networks** -- GCs work with both electrical and HVAC subs. Ask your GC contacts for introductions to their electrical subs.
8. **Permit offices** -- Electricians and HVAC contractors pull permits at the same building departments. Strike up conversations in line.
