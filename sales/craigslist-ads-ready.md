## Craigslist Posting Tips

- **Repost every 3-5 days.** Craigslist posts get buried fast. Rotate between the three ads.
- **Rotate the ads.** Don't post the same one every time. Use Ad 1, then Ad 2 three days later, then Ad 3 three days after that. Repeat.
- **Post timing:** Tuesday-Thursday mornings (8-10 AM) tend to get the most views in the services section.
- **Do NOT include images with stock photos.** Craigslist users are skeptical. A clean text post performs better than one with a generic image.
- **Include the phone number multiple times.** People skim. Make sure (615) 784-5747 is easy to find.
- **Watch for flagging.** If an ad gets flagged/removed, slightly rewrite the title and opening paragraph before reposting. Don't change the core content.
- **Reply-to email:** Use a dedicated email like craigslist@thecalltaker.com so you can track which leads come from CL specifically.
