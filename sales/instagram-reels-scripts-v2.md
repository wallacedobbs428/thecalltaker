# Instagram Reels / TikTok Scripts - Volume 2

## Production Notes

- **Shoot vertical (9:16)** -- always.
- **Captions are mandatory.** 85% of people watch without sound.
- **First 3 seconds decide everything.** Nail the hook or they scroll.
- **Keep energy high but authentic.** You're a business owner, not a hype man.
- **Lighting matters more than camera.** Natural light or a ring light. Clean background.
- **Post consistently:** Aim for 3-5 Reels per week.
