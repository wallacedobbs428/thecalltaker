## Production Notes

- **Filming:** Shoot in a real environment — shop, truck, job site, garage. Not a studio. Not a ring light setup. Keep it raw.
- **Framing:** Vertical 9:16. Wallace talking direct to camera. Handheld is fine. Movement is fine.
- **Pacing:** Cut the dead air. These need to be tight. If a line doesn't earn its place, cut it.
- **On-screen text:** Phone number and website should be on screen for the entire back half of each video. Use large, clean text — white with a dark outline.
- **Captions:** Auto-caption everything. Most viewers watch on mute first.
- **Posting cadence:** One per week minimum across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Repurpose the same video on all three — tweak the caption for each platform.
