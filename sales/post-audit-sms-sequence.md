# Post-Audit SMS Follow-Up Sequence

After delivering the free missed call audit report via email, use this text sequence to drive engagement and convert the lead into a demo.

**Sender:** Wallace / (615) 653-9004
**Trigger:** Audit report emailed to the prospect
**Goal:** Get them to open the report, react to the findings, and book a demo

---

## The Sequence

### Text 1 -- Same Day (Send 1-2 hours after emailing the report)

**Purpose:** Make sure they know the report is in their inbox. Keep it casual so it feels like a real person, not a drip campaign.

```
Hey [First Name], it's Wallace. Just sent your missed call audit to your email. Some interesting stuff in there -- take a look when you get a sec.
```

*Characters: ~140 (varies with name)*

---

### Text 2 -- Day 2

**Purpose:** Nudge them to actually open it. Most people saw the email notification but got busy. This is a gentle bump.

```
Did you get a chance to look at that audit? No rush, just don't want it buried in your inbox.
```

*Characters: 91*

---

### Text 3 -- Day 4

**Purpose:** Pull a specific finding from their audit and use it as a hook. Make the cost of inaction feel real and concrete.

**Option A (if you found missed after-hours calls):**
```
One thing that jumped out -- you had [X] calls after hours last month that went unanswered. At your avg ticket that's probably $[X] sitting on the table. Want me to walk you through it?
```

*Characters: ~175 (slightly over 160, will send as 2 segments -- that is fine for this one)*

**Option B (if you found high voicemail rate):**
```
Your report showed about [X]% of calls hitting voicemail. Most of those callers are calling the next company on Google. Happy to walk through it if you want.
```

*Characters: ~155*

**Option C (generic, if no standout stat):**
```
Pulled some numbers on your missed calls. A few things in there I think would surprise you. Got 5 min this week to walk through it?
```

*Characters: 131*

---

### Text 4 -- Day 7

**Purpose:** Soft close. Shift from the report to the solution. Paint a picture of what life looks like with The Call Taker handling their phones.

```
Hey [First Name] -- no pressure on the audit. Quick question though: want to hear what it sounds like when every call gets answered? I can set up a 5 min demo of the AI.
```

*Characters: ~165 (2 segments -- worth it for the close)*

**Alternate (shorter):**
```
Curious -- want to hear what it sounds like when every call to your shop gets answered live? Takes 5 min. I'll set it up.
```

*Characters: 118*

---

### Text 5 -- Day 14 (Breakup Text)

**Purpose:** Last touch. The breakup text works because it removes pressure and triggers loss aversion. Keep it light, leave the door open.

**Option A (casual breakup):**
```
Hey [First Name], I'll stop bugging you. If you ever want to stop losing calls to voicemail, I'm here. Good luck this season.
```

*Characters: ~122*

**Option B (with a little humor):**
```
Alright I'll take the hint haha. If you ever want an AI answering your phones 24/7 so you can stop missing jobs, you know where to find me. Good luck out there.
```

*Characters: ~158*

**Option C (super short):**
```
No worries if the timing's off. I'll be around whenever you're ready. Good luck this season [First Name].
```

*Characters: ~100*

## Sequence Summary

| Text | Day | Purpose | Tone |
|------|-----|---------|------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Heads up, report sent | Helpful |
| 2 | Day 2 | Did you see it? | Casual nudge |
| 3 | Day 4 | Key finding + walk-through offer | Value-driven |
| 4 | Day 7 | Soft close, demo pitch | Curious |
| 5 | Day 14 | Breakup, leave door open | Friendly exit |

---

## Rules for This Sequence

1. **Personalize Text 3.** Pull a real number from their audit. Generic texts convert half as well as specific ones.
2. **Do not send all options.** Pick ONE version of each text. The options are there so you can match the tone to the lead.
3. **If they reply at any point, stop the sequence.** Switch to a real conversation. The goal is dialogue, not automation.
4. **Send during business hours.** Between 8am-5pm in their local time. HVAC owners are early risers, so 8-9am often gets fast replies.
5. **If they ask to stop, stop immediately.** Mark them as opted-out. Do not text again.
6. **If they ghost the whole sequence**, wait 30 days and re-engage with a new angle (seasonal campaign, new feature, etc.). Do not re-run this same sequence.

---

## Tracking

After each send, log the following:
- Date/time sent
- Which option (A/B/C) you used
- Whether they opened the audit email (if you have tracking)
- Their reply (if any)
- Next action

This data will help you optimize the sequence over time. After 20-30 leads, you will know which versions of Text 3 and Text 5 perform best.
