### The Problem: Calls Going Straight to Voicemail

Mike, the owner of Comfort Pro, knew he was missing calls. He just did not know how many -- or how much it was costing him.

During a busy summer week, Mike tracked his missed calls for the first time. The numbers were worse than he expected:

- **12 missed calls per week** on average
- **85% of those callers never left a voicemail** -- they called someone else
- That is roughly **10 lost leads per week**
- At a 30% close rate and $375 average job, that equals **3 lost jobs per week**
- **$1,125/week in lost revenue -- or $4,875/month**

Mike was spending money on Google Ads and Home Advisor leads, then losing those same leads because no one answered the phone when they called.

> "I was paying for leads and then sending them to voicemail. I was basically funding my competitors' businesses." -- Mike R., Owner, Comfort Pro Heating & Air

---

### The Solution: The Call Taker -- AI Receptionist for HVAC

Mike signed up for The Call Taker Professional plan at $497/month. Setup took less than a day.

**What changed immediately:**

- Every call answered on the first ring, 24/7
- Callers heard a professional greeting using his company name
- The AI asked the right questions: system type, issue, address, availability
- Mike received a text summary within seconds of each call
- Appointments booked directly onto his calendar
