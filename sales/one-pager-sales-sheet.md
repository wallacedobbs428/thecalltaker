## How The Call Taker Works

### Step 1 -- Customer Calls Your Business Number
When you are on a ladder, under a unit, or off the clock, The Call Taker answers your phone in seconds. It sounds professional, knows HVAC terminology, and greets the caller by your company name.

### Step 2 -- AI Handles the Conversation
The Call Taker asks the right questions: What is the issue? What type of system? What is the address? When are they available? It captures every detail you need to dispatch or follow up -- no scripts, no hold music, no "press 1 for..."

### Step 3 -- You Get a Text Summary + Appointment Booked
Within seconds you receive a complete text summary of the call: caller name, number, issue, urgency, and address. If booking is enabled, the appointment goes straight onto your calendar. You never miss a lead again.
