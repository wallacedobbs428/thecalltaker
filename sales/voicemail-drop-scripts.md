## Tips for Leaving Voicemails

- **Say your phone number slowly.** They're probably scrambling for a pen. Say it once at normal speed, then repeat it slower.
- **Don't sound like you're reading.** Practice each script out loud 4-5 times until it feels like you're just talking to someone.
- **Leave the voicemail standing up.** It puts more energy and confidence in your voice.
- **Smile while you record.** Sounds stupid. Works every time.
- **Best times to call HVAC owners:** Before 7:30 AM (before they dispatch crews) or after 5 PM (when the day winds down). Saturday mornings between 8-10 AM are surprisingly good.
- **Track everything.** Log which script you used, what day, what time. After a few weeks you'll know exactly which version pulls the most callbacks.
- **Say their name if you have it.** "Hey Mike, this is Wallace..." beats "Hey, this is Wallace..." every time. Personalization increases callbacks significantly.
- **Never leave the same script twice for the same person.** Rotate through them.
