# The Call Taker — Demo Call Script

## Overview

**Total demo time:** 15 minutes
**Format:** Phone call or video call (phone is fine — HVAC owners don't want to sit at a computer)
**Goal:** Get them to experience The Call Taker firsthand, then close for signup or a firm follow-up

---

## Pre-Demo Setup (Before the Call)

- [ ] Demo phone number is live and working
- [ ] Demo dashboard is loaded and ready to screen share (if video) or ready to describe (if phone)
- [ ] You know their company name, owner's name, number of trucks (from prior conversation)
- [ ] You have pricing ready for their size
- [ ] Calendar is open to offer setup dates

---

## SECTION 1: Pre-Demo — Build Rapport (2 Minutes)

> "Hey [Name], it's Wallace. Thanks for taking the time — I know you're busy. This'll be quick, I promise. 15 minutes."

> "Before I show you anything, I just want to confirm a couple things. You're running [Company Name] in [City], right? And you've got about [X] trucks on the road?"

[Let them confirm/correct]

> "And when we talked before, you mentioned [their pain point — missed calls, answering their own phone at night, bad answering service, etc.]. Is that still the biggest headache, or is there something else bugging you?"

[Let them talk. Take notes. The more they tell you about their problem, the easier the close.]

> "Got it. Well, what I'm going to show you is going to fix exactly that. Let me walk you through it."

---

## SECTION 2: Set Up the Demo (2 Minutes)

> "OK, here's what we're going to do. I'm going to give you a phone number. I want you to call it and pretend you're a homeowner whose AC just died. Talk to it like you'd want your customers to be talked to. Ask it questions. Be difficult if you want. Just have a normal conversation."

> "While you're on the call, I'm going to watch everything come through on our end — the call log, the customer info it captures, the appointment it books, the text it sends. When you're done, I'll walk you through all of it."

> "Ready? Here's the number: (629) 269-9697. Go ahead and call it right now. I'll stay on the line — just put me on hold or mute me while you call."

**Important:** Let them make the call. Don't rush them. This is the most powerful part of the demo — they're experiencing it as a customer.

---

## SECTION 3: Live Demo — The Call (5 Minutes)

**While they're on the call with the AI, monitor:**
- Call coming in on the dashboard
- Caller information being captured in real time
- Appointment being scheduled
- Text notification being generated

**When they finish the call and come back to you:**

> "So — what'd you think?"

[Let them react. Most people are impressed. If they are, ride that energy. If they have concerns, address them now.]

> "Pretty natural, right? Callers can't tell the difference. That's the whole point."

**Now walk them through what happened on the back end:**

> "Here's what just happened while you were on that call:"

> "**One** — The Call Taker picked up in under 2 seconds. No ringing. No voicemail. No hold music."

> "**Two** — It captured your name, your phone number, your address, and the issue — [read back the details it captured]. All accurate, right?"

> "**Three** — It booked an appointment on the calendar. [Read the appointment details.] Your tech would see this show up immediately."

> "**Four** — You got a text. Check your phone."

[Give them a second to look at their phone and see the text notification.]

> "That text went out the instant the call ended. So even if you're on a roof, in a crawl space, or eating dinner — you know exactly what's going on. Customer name, number, what they need, when they're booked. Everything."

---

## SECTION 4: Dashboard Walkthrough (3 Minutes)

**If on a video call, share your screen. If on a phone call, describe what you're seeing.**

> "Let me show you what your dashboard looks like."

### Call Log

> "Every call that comes in gets logged right here. Date, time, caller's number, how long the call lasted, and the outcome. You can see every call your business has ever received in one place. No more wondering if you missed something."

### Customer Details

> "Click into any call and you see everything — the customer's name, their phone number, their address, what they called about, and the full conversation summary. If the caller said 'My AC is blowing hot air and I've got a baby in the house,' you'll see that right here."

### Calendar / Appointments

> "Here's the appointment it just booked. It shows up on your calendar automatically. If you use Google Calendar, it syncs there too. Your techs can see their schedule update in real time."

### Text Notification

> "And here's the text that went to your phone. You can set this up to go to you, your dispatcher, your lead tech — whoever needs to know. You can even set different rules for emergencies. If someone calls at 2am with a burst pipe or no heat in January, it can flag it as urgent and text you immediately."

---

## SECTION 5: Close (3 Minutes)

> "So you saw how that works. Every call to your business would get answered exactly like that — 24/7, 365 days a year. No voicemail. No missed jobs. No waking up to a full inbox of leads that already called someone else."

**Now transition to their situation:**

> "Let me ask you a few things so I can figure out the right setup for you."

- "How many trucks are you running right now?"
- "What are your business hours? When do the phones currently go unanswered?"
- "Do you have a dispatcher, or is it all going to your cell?"
- "Roughly how many calls would you say you miss in a given week?"
- "What's your average job ticket — $300, $500 range?"

[Do the quick math for them:]

> "OK so if you're missing roughly [X] calls a week, and your average job is [$Y], that's [$Z] per month in potential lost revenue. Even if half of those were real jobs, that's [$Z/2] a month you're not getting."

**Present pricing:**

> "For a shop your size, The Call Taker runs [PRICING]. So the math works out pretty clearly — you'd make that back with [X] recovered calls a month. Everything after that is profit."

---

## Handling Objections at the Close

If they push back, reference the objection handling from the cold call script. The most common at this stage:

### "I need to think about it."

> "I respect that. But let me ask — what's there to think about? You just called it yourself. You heard how it sounds. You saw the text come through. The only question is: how many calls are going to go to voicemail between now and when you sign up?"
>
> "How about this — let me get you set up and you try it for [trial period if applicable]. If it doesn't deliver, you walk. No hard feelings."

### "It's more than I expected."

> "I understand. But think about it this way — you're not paying for an answering service. You're paying for a revenue recovery tool. If it brings back even [X] calls a month at [$Y] each, it pays for itself [X] times over. This isn't a cost line. It's a profit line."

### "I need to talk to my partner/wife/business partner."

> "Totally fair. What would help them make the decision? I can send over a one-page proposal you can show them — has the numbers, what's included, the ROI. Want me to send that over now so you've got it?"

[Send the one-page proposal immediately.]

> "And let's put a time on the calendar to reconnect — how about [Day]? That gives you a couple days to talk it over, and I can answer any of their questions."

---

## Post-Demo — If They Close

> "Awesome, [Name]. Let's do this. Here's what happens next:"

> "**One** — I'm going to send you a quick setup form. Takes 5 minutes — your business hours, service area, scheduling preferences, where you want the text notifications sent."

> "**Two** — We'll build your custom Call Taker setup within 48 hours. I'll call you when it's ready and we'll test it together."

> "**Three** — We flip the switch and every call to your business gets answered from that point forward. No more voicemail. No more lost jobs."

> "Sound good? I'll send that setup form right now."

---

## Post-Demo — If They Don't Close

> "No pressure at all, [Name]. I want you to feel good about it. Let me send you a one-page breakdown of everything we talked about — the features, the ROI, all of it. You can look it over and share it with whoever you need to."

> "Let's touch base on [specific day]. I'll give you a quick call — 5 minutes — and see where your head's at. Fair?"

[Confirm the follow-up date. Log it. Send the one-page proposal within 10 minutes.]

---

## Post-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Send one-page proposal (whether they closed or not)
- [ ] If closed: send setup form immediately
- [ ] If not closed: set specific follow-up date and log it
- [ ] Log demo notes: what impressed them, what objections they raised, their call volume, their truck count, their average ticket
- [ ] Send a follow-up text within 1 hour: "Hey [Name], good talking to you. Here's that info I mentioned: [link]. Let me know if any questions come up. — Wallace"
