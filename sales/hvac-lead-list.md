## Secret Shopping Script

Call after 6pm. When you get voicemail:

1. Note the company name, date, and time
2. Note how many rings before voicemail
3. Note if it's a custom greeting or generic carrier voicemail
4. DON'T leave a message

Then use this in your cold email:
> "I called [Company] at [time] last [day]. Got your voicemail."

This is your opening line in Email 1 of the cold sequence.
