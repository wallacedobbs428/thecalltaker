## 3. Referral Commission Structure

### Recommended Structure: Hybrid (Upfront Bonus + Monthly Recurring)

| Component | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Signup Bonus** | **$100** | Paid when the referred HVAC company becomes a paying customer (first invoice paid) |
| **Monthly Recurring** | **$25/mo** | Paid every month the referred client remains an active subscriber |
| **Payment Method** | Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, or check | Partner's choice |
| **Payment Schedule** | Monthly | Recurring commissions paid on the 15th of each month for the prior month |
| **Minimum Payout** | $25 | No payout below $25 (carries over to next month) |

### Why This Structure Works

- **$100 upfront** is enough to motivate a casual mention. It's real money for a 30-second conversation.
- **$25/mo recurring** creates long-term incentive. A partner who refers just 10 clients earns $250/mo in passive income. That adds up and keeps them actively referring.
- **Your margin stays healthy:**

| Plan | Monthly Price | Upfront Commission | Monthly Commission | Your Net After Month 1 | Your Net Ongoing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter ($297/mo) | $297 | $100 (one-time) | $25/mo | $172 | $272/mo |
| Professional ($497/mo) | $497 | $100 (one-time) | $25/mo | $372 | $472/mo |

### Tier Upgrade (Optional — Activate After 6 Months)

Once the partner program has traction, consider adding tiers:

| Tier | Referrals | Signup Bonus | Monthly Recurring |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | 1-5 clients | $100 | $25/mo |
| **Silver** | 6-15 clients | $150 | $35/mo |
| **Gold** | 16+ clients | $200 | $50/mo |

This gives top partners a reason to keep pushing. But don't overcomplicate it early — start with the flat structure and add tiers when volume justifies it.

### Commission Rules

1. **Attribution window:** 90 days. If a referred lead signs up within 90 days of the referral, the partner gets credit.
2. **No double-dipping.** If a lead comes through two partners, first referral wins.
3. **Active clients only.** Recurring commissions stop if the client cancels or pauses.
4. **No self-referrals.** Partners cannot refer themselves or their own companies.
5. **No minimum commitment.** Partners can stop referring at any time. No clawbacks on already-paid commissions.

## 5. Tracking System

Use a **Google Sheet** as the source of truth. Zero cost, easy to maintain, shareable with partners.

**Referral Tracker Spreadsheet — Columns:**

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Partner Name | Who referred them |
| Partner Email | For commission payment coordination |
| Partner Type | Supply house, coach, agency, etc. |
| Referred Company | Name of the HVAC company |
| Referred Contact | Owner's name and phone |
| Referral Date | When the referral came in |
| Referral Source | Link click, email intro, name drop, etc. |
| Demo Completed? | Yes/No |
| Signed Up? | Yes/No + date |
| Plan | Starter ($297) or Professional ($497) |
| Signup Bonus Paid? | Yes/No + date |
| Monthly Commission Status | Active / Cancelled + last paid date |
| Notes | Any context |

### Phase 2: Referral Link Tracking (Add When Volume Grows)

Give each partner a **unique referral link** using UTM parameters:

```
https://thecalltaker.com/demo?ref=partnername
```

**Implementation:**

1. Create the link for each partner (e.g., `?ref=johnstone-nashville`, `?ref=coach-tommy`)
2. Add a hidden field to the GHL demo booking form that captures the `ref` parameter
3. When a lead books a demo, the referral source is automatically attached to their GHL contact record
4. Use the GHL custom field `referral_partner` to store this value
5. Filter contacts by `referral_partner` to see which partners are driving signups

**GHL Setup Steps:**

- Create a custom field: `Referral Partner` (text field)
- Add a hidden form field on demo.html that reads the `ref` URL parameter
- On form submission, map it to the `referral_partner` custom field
- Create a GHL workflow: When contact tag = "paying-client" AND custom field `referral_partner` is not empty, send Wallace a notification to pay the partner commission

### Phase 3: Automated Commission Tracking (Scale Phase)

When the partner program grows past 10-15 active partners, consider:

| Tool | Cost | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **FirstPromoter** | $49/mo | Full affiliate/referral tracking with dashboards, auto-payouts, and partner portals |
| **Rewardful** | $49/mo | Stripe-integrated referral tracking with automatic commission calculation |
| **PartnerStack** | Custom pricing | Enterprise-grade partner management (overkill until 50+ partners) |
| **Manual + GHL** | Free | Keep using the spreadsheet + GHL custom fields until volume demands automation |

**Recommendation:** Stay on Phase 1 (spreadsheet) until you have 5+ active referral partners. Move to Phase 2 (UTM links + GHL) as soon as the first partner sends a referral. Don't invest in paid tools until the program is generating consistent revenue.

### Commission Payment Workflow

1. On the 1st of each month, Wallace checks the referral tracker spreadsheet
2. Cross-reference with GHL: which referred contacts became paying clients in the prior month?
3. Calculate commissions owed (signup bonuses + monthly recurring)
4. Send payments via Venmo/PayPal/Zelle by the 15th
5. Update the spreadsheet with payment dates
6. Send each partner a quick text or email: "Hey [Name], your referral commission for [month] just hit — $[amount]. Thanks for the referrals!"

That monthly message is important. It reminds them The Call Taker exists, confirms the money is real, and keeps them motivated to refer.
