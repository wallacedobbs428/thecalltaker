## 1. Program Overview

The Call Taker is an AI-powered receptionist built specifically for HVAC companies. It answers calls 24/7, books appointments, captures lead information, and eliminates the missed-call problem that costs HVAC companies thousands in lost revenue every month.

Pricing: $297/month (Starter) | $497/month (Professional)

This is not an afterthought or a side project. This is a structured revenue channel with three distinct tiers, clear economics, and a real onboarding process.

## 2. Why This Matters to Agencies and Consultants

If you serve HVAC companies in any capacity -- marketing, consulting, coaching, technology -- you already know the following:

- **Your clients lose calls.** They run Google Ads, SEO, direct mail, and social campaigns. Then the phone rings and nobody answers. You get blamed for "leads that don't convert."
- **Call handling is not your core service**, but it directly impacts every service you do provide. If the phones are broken, your marketing ROI looks bad regardless of how good the campaigns are.
- **Your clients ask you for solutions.** They want you to fix the phone problem. You either cobble together an answering service recommendation (which you earn nothing from) or you ignore the problem.
- **Recurring revenue stabilizes your agency.** Project-based and retainer-based agencies benefit from adding a sticky, subscription product to their stack. AI call handling has extremely low churn because once an HVAC company experiences zero missed calls, they do not go back.

The Call Taker Partner Program solves all four problems. You offer a proven product, your clients' call handling improves (making all your other services perform better), and you earn recurring revenue on every client you bring in.

## 6. How to Find and Approach Agency Partners

### Where They Are

**Facebook Groups (highest density, easiest to engage):**
- HVAC Marketing / HVAC Business Owner groups -- agencies lurk and post in these groups regularly. Look for people offering marketing services in the comments.
- "Digital Marketing Agency Owners" groups (general) -- search within these groups for members who mention HVAC or home services.
- "Home Services Marketing" groups -- groups specifically focused on marketing for contractors, plumbers, HVAC, electricians.
- GoHighLevel (GHL) user groups -- many agencies using GHL serve home services clients. These are ideal reseller and white-label candidates because they already understand SaaS reselling.

**LinkedIn (best for direct outreach):**
- Search: "HVAC marketing agency" / "home services marketing" / "contractor marketing"
- Search: "HVAC consultant" / "HVAC business coach"
- Look at who runs HVAC-related LinkedIn ads -- those are agencies actively spending to acquire HVAC clients.
- Join HVAC industry groups on LinkedIn and note who posts marketing-related content.

**Google Search (to build a target list):**
- "HVAC marketing agency" -- these companies literally advertise that they serve HVAC. They are your primary targets.
- "home services marketing company"
- "HVAC SEO" / "HVAC PPC" / "HVAC lead generation"
- Review the websites of the top 20 results. Look for agencies that list HVAC in their client verticals.

**YouTube:**
- Search "HVAC marketing" or "HVAC business tips" -- find channels run by coaches, consultants, or agencies. Reach out via their website or social profiles.

**Podcasts:**
- Search for HVAC business podcasts. The hosts and guests are often coaches and consultants with large HVAC audiences. A partnership with one of these people is extremely high-leverage.

**Industry Events and Trade Shows:**
- AHR Expo, ACCA conferences, local HVAC association events
- Marketing vendors and consultants often have booths or attend as sponsors
- Collect cards. Follow up with the partner pitch.

**ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber Ecosystems:**
- These platforms have partner directories and integration marketplaces. Agencies listed there serve HVAC companies and are already comfortable with tech-forward products.

### How to Approach Them

1. **Lead with their problem, not your product.** The approach is: "Your clients miss calls. That hurts your campaign performance. I have something that fixes it and puts money in your pocket."

2. **Offer the demo first.** Tell them to call (615) 784-5747 and press 1. Let the product sell itself. Then have the partner economics conversation.

3. **Start with Referral Partner tier.** Do not push Reseller or White-Label on the first call. Let them refer 1-2 clients, see the product work, and then upgrade to Reseller when they are ready.

4. **Make it about their revenue.** Agencies and consultants understand margins. Show them the math: "You have 15 HVAC clients. If 8 sign up at $500/month and your cost is $197, that is $2,424/month in new margin. That is almost $30,000/year from a product you did not have to build."

5. **Provide a one-page partner overview** (pull from this document) that they can review after the call. Do not send the full program document cold -- it is too much. Send the overview, have the call, then share details.

### What This Means

The partner channel has the potential to become the largest revenue driver for The Call Taker within 12 months, surpassing direct sales. The key metrics to track:

1. **Number of active partners** (target: 10-15 within 6 months)
2. **Average clients per partner per month** (target: 1-3)
3. **Partner-sourced client churn rate** (target: under 10%/month)
4. **Weighted average revenue per partner-sourced client** (target: $250+/month)

The economics work at every tier. Even a single Reseller partner bringing in 3 clients per month generates $861/month in wholesale revenue. Five of those partners and the business is generating over $4,000/month from the partner channel before direct sales are even counted.

## 9. Implementation Priorities

### Phase 1: Weeks 1-4 (Foundation)

- Finalize the partner agreement and have it reviewed
- Build a simple partner tracking spreadsheet or GHL pipeline
- Identify 20 target agencies (use the sourcing methods in Section 6)
- Send the first 10 cold outreach emails

### Phase 2: Weeks 5-8 (First Partners)

- Close 2-3 Referral Partners
- Close 1 Reseller Partner
- Onboard all partners using the processes in Section 7
- Support partners through their first 1-2 client sales
- Collect feedback and refine the pitch and materials

### Phase 3: Weeks 9-16 (Scale)

- Expand outreach to 50+ agencies
- Begin engaging in Facebook groups and LinkedIn where agencies congregate
- Develop case study materials from early partner-sourced clients
- Introduce the White-Label tier to qualified Reseller partners
- Target: 8-10 active partners by end of month 4

### Phase 4: Month 5+ (Optimize)

- Identify top-performing partners and invest in those relationships
- Create a partner newsletter (monthly, brief) with updates, tips, and success stories
- Explore co-marketing opportunities with top partners
- Evaluate adding partner tiers or incentives based on volume thresholds
- Target: 15+ active partners, 10+ new clients/month from the partner channel
