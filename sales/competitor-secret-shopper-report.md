# Competitor Secret Shopper Report

## Sales Playbook: The After-Hours Secret Shopper Strategy

**What this is:** A ready-to-use report template and complete sales workflow. You call the prospect's top 3 competitors after hours, document exactly what happens, and present the findings. This single move demonstrates more value than any sales pitch ever could.

**Why this works:**

- It shows the prospect hard evidence of what their competitors are doing (or not doing)
- If competitors answer and the prospect does not, it creates real urgency and FOMO
- If competitors also miss calls, it reveals a wide-open opportunity to leapfrog the entire market
- It proves you did the homework — you are not just another vendor reading a script
- It positions The Call Taker as the obvious, logical next step

# PART 3: HOW TO USE THIS REPORT — STEP BY STEP

---

## Step 1: Identify the Prospect's 3 Closest Competitors

**Best methods:**

- **Google Maps:** Search "[CITY] HVAC repair" or "[CITY] heating and cooling." The top 3 results in the map pack that are NOT the prospect are your targets. These are the companies directly competing for the same local search traffic.
- **Ask the prospect directly:** During your initial conversation, ask: "Who do you run into the most on bids?" or "Who are the other big HVAC companies in your area?" Business owners always know their competitors.
- **Google Search Ads:** Search "HVAC repair [CITY]" and look at who is running paid ads. These are companies actively spending money to capture the same customers.
- **Yelp / Angi / HomeAdvisor:** Check the top-rated HVAC companies in the prospect's service area.

**Pick competitors that are:**
- Similar in size to the prospect (do not compare a 5-truck shop to a 50-truck operation)
- In the same geographic market
- Competing for the same type of work (residential vs. commercial)

---

## Step 2: Make the Calls Between 7:00 PM and 9:00 PM on a Weekday

**Why this window:**
- It is definitively after business hours for almost every HVAC company
- It is a realistic time for a homeowner to discover a problem (AC stops working after dinner, furnace dies when the temperature drops at night)
- It is late enough that most offices are closed but early enough that an answering service should still be active

**When you call:**
- Use your personal cell phone or any non-business number
- Call all 4 companies within the same 30-minute window so conditions are equal
- Start with the prospect's business so you know their baseline before calling competitors

**What to say if someone answers:**

> "Hi, I am having a problem with my [air conditioning / furnace / heat pump]. It stopped working about an hour ago and I am trying to figure out if someone can come take a look at it. Do you have any availability?"

Keep it simple. You are a homeowner, not a technician. Do not use industry jargon. Just be a person who needs help.

**If you get voicemail:**
- Note whether the greeting is professional (company name, hours, emergency instructions) or generic
- Note if the voicemail box is full
- Do NOT leave a message (you do not want a callback)

---

## Step 3: Document Everything in Real Time

Fill in the report template as you go. Do not rely on memory. Specifically capture:

- Exact time you called
- How many rings before answer or voicemail
- Exact words of the voicemail greeting (or a summary)
- If someone answered: what they said, what questions they asked, whether they could book
- Your genuine reaction as a "homeowner" — did you feel taken care of? Frustrated? Ignored?
- Anything that stood out, good or bad

**Pro tip:** Rate each company immediately after the call while the experience is fresh. The 1-5 star rating should reflect how confident you would feel as a homeowner that this company will help you.

- **5 stars:** Someone answered, asked the right questions, and booked an appointment or dispatched a tech
- **4 stars:** Someone answered and took a thorough message with a clear callback commitment
- **3 stars:** Answering service picked up but could only take basic info, unclear when/if someone would call back
- **2 stars:** Professional voicemail with company name and hours, but still just a voicemail
- **1 star:** Generic voicemail, voicemail box full, no answer at all, or disconnected number

---

## Step 4: Build the Report

- Choose the scenario conclusion (Part 2) that best fits what you found
- Fill in all the bracketed fields in the template
- Review the scoreboard table — make sure it is accurate and the comparison is clear
- Read the whole thing once from the prospect's perspective: does it make the case without being pushy?

**Keep it factual.** Do not trash the competitors or editorialize. Let the data speak for itself. The prospect will draw their own conclusions, and those conclusions will be more powerful than anything you could tell them.

---

## Step 5: Send the Report via Email

Use the email template in Part 4 below. Send it between 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM so it is at the top of their inbox when they start their day.

**Subject line:** I secret-shopped your 3 biggest competitors last night

This subject line works because:
- It is specific and intriguing — they HAVE to open it
- It implies you did real work on their behalf, which you did
- It triggers competitive instinct — they want to know what their competitors are doing
- It does not sound like a sales email

---

## Step 6: Follow Up with a Call

Call the prospect 2-4 hours after sending the email. Use the follow-up script in Part 5 below. The goal of the call is not to sell — it is to get them to open and read the report, then book a 15-minute conversation to discuss it.
