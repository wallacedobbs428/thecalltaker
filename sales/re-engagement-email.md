**When to send:** 3-4 weeks after final email in the 7-email sequence went unanswered.
**Goal:** Re-open the conversation with a fresh angle. No guilt-tripping. No "just checking in." Give them a genuine new reason to respond.

## Sending Notes

- **Variation A** works best from April through early June when summer urgency is real and believable. Swap "summer AC season" for "heating season" if sending in September-November.
- **Variation B** works year-round because the free audit is always relevant. Lead with value, not a sales pitch.
- **Do not** reference previous emails or say "I've been trying to reach you." That creates pressure, not curiosity.
- If they respond to the audit offer, run the audit promptly and deliver the report within 48 hours. The audit itself is the selling tool.
- If still no response after this re-engagement attempt, move them to a quarterly touch list (once every 3 months with a short, value-driven email).
