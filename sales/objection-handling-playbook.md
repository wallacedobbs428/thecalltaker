## Tone Reminders

- **Be a peer, not a salesperson.** You're one business owner talking to another.

- **Acknowledge before redirecting.** Always validate their concern before offering your perspective.
- **Ask questions, don't lecture.** The best objection handlers are the ones who ask the right question and let the prospect talk themselves into it.
- **Push for the demo call.** The product sells itself. Your job is to get them to experience it.
- **Never trash the prospect's current setup.** Respect what they're doing now, then show them what's possible.
