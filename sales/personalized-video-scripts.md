## RECORDING CHECKLIST

Before recording each video, Wallace should:

- [ ] Pull up the company's website or Google Business listing on screen
- [ ] Have their phone number ready to dial
- [ ] Have (615) 784-5747 ready as the second call
- [ ] Face cam on (bottom right corner)
- [ ] Good lighting, clean background
- [ ] Speak conversationally — NOT reading a script
- [ ] Keep total video under 75 seconds (aim for 60)

## SENDING PROTOCOL

1. Record video on Loom
2. Copy Loom link
3. Send via SMS in GHL:

```
Hey [First Name], I made a quick 60-second video specifically for [Company Name]. No pitch — I just want to show you something about your phones. Here it is: [Loom Link] -Wallace
```

4. If no reply in 24 hours, follow up:

```
Did you get a chance to watch that video? The part where I call your shop live is worth seeing. Just 60 seconds: [Loom Link] -Wallace
```

## BEST TIME TO SEND

- **Ideal:** Tuesday-Thursday between 7:00-7:30 AM (before they head to first job)
- **Backup:** Same days, 6:00-7:00 PM (after last job, winding down)
- **Avoid:** Monday mornings (too hectic), Friday afternoons (checked out)
