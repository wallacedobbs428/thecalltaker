## WHAT'S INCLUDED

### 1. Missed Call Text-Back (normally $49/mo — INCLUDED)

Every call that doesn't connect triggers an automatic text within 60 seconds:

> "Hey, this is [Company Name]. We saw you just called — sorry we missed you! How can we help? Reply here or we'll call you back ASAP."

**Why it matters:** Recovers 30-40% of missed calls that would otherwise go to competitors.

### 2. Review Booster (normally $79/mo — INCLUDED)

After every completed job, the customer automatically gets a text:

> "Thanks for choosing [Company Name]! If we did a great job, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps. [Link]"

Followed 24 hours later by:

> "Just a friendly reminder — your review means the world to us. Takes 30 seconds: [Link]"

**Why it matters:** HVAC companies with 100+ Google reviews get 3-5x more calls than companies with 20. Reviews compound. Every new review = more organic calls = more revenue.

**Expected results:** 15-30 new Google reviews per month for an active company.

### 3. Automated Lead Follow-Up Sequence

Every lead who calls but doesn't book gets a 7-touch follow-up over 14 days:

| Day | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Text | "Thanks for calling! We'd love to help. Reply to book or call us back anytime." |
| Day 1 | Text | "Still need help with your [issue]? We have availability this week." |
| Day 3 | Email | Helpful tips related to their issue + CTA to book |
| Day 5 | Text | "Just checking in — we're here whenever you're ready." |
| Day 7 | Email | Customer testimonial + "limited availability this week" |
| Day 10 | Text | "Last follow-up — if you still need help, we're a call away." |
| Day 14 | Email | Final gentle nudge with seasonal maintenance offer |

**Why it matters:** Most HVAC companies follow up ZERO times. The first company to follow up wins the job. This sequence runs automatically — no work from the owner.

**Expected results:** 10-20% of unconverted leads book from follow-up alone.

### 4. Monthly Performance Report + Strategy Call

Every month, the HVAC company gets:

- **Total calls answered** and breakdown by type (emergency, maintenance, new install, etc.)
- **Appointments booked** vs. calls received (conversion rate)
- **Revenue impact** estimate (calls answered x avg job value)
- **Review count** growth and Google rating trend
- **Lead follow-up results** (how many unconverted leads came back)
- **Recommendations** for the next month

Plus a **15-minute strategy call** with Wallace to review the numbers and optimize.

**Why it matters:** This makes you a trusted advisor, not just a vendor. Clients who get monthly reports and strategy calls have 90%+ retention rates.

### 5. Seasonal Campaign Automation

We build and run seasonal campaigns for them:

- **Spring:** "AC tune-up before the heat hits" — automated text blast to past customers
- **Fall:** "Furnace check before winter" — same thing
- **Peak season:** "Emergency priority line" messaging for existing customers

**Why it matters:** Past customers are the cheapest source of new revenue. A $49 maintenance visit from a text blast often turns into a $500-$2,000 repair or replacement.

### 6. Dedicated Priority Support
