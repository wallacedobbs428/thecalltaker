# RETARGETING AUDIENCE SETUP INSTRUCTIONS

## Facebook Custom Audiences for Retargeting

### Prerequisites:
- A Facebook Business Manager account
- A Facebook Pixel installed on thecalltaker.com
- A Facebook Ads Manager account

### Step 1: Install the Facebook Pixel on thecalltaker.com

If the pixel is not already installed:

1. Go to **Facebook Business Manager** (business.facebook.com)
2. Navigate to **Events Manager** in the left sidebar
3. Click **Connect Data Sources** > **Web** > **Facebook Pixel**
4. Name it "The Call Taker Pixel"
5. Choose **Install code manually** (or use a partner integration if your site is on WordPress, Wix, etc.)
6. Copy the base pixel code and paste it in the `<head>` section of every page on thecalltaker.com
7. Verify the pixel is firing by using the **Facebook Pixel Helper** Chrome extension

### Step 2: Create Custom Audiences

Go to **Ads Manager** > **Audiences** > **Create Audience** > **Custom Audience** > **Website**

Create these four audiences:

---

**Audience 1: All Website Visitors (Broad Retargeting)**
- Source: Your Facebook Pixel
- Event: All website visitors
- Retention: **30 days**
- Exclude: People who completed a purchase/signup (if you have a thank-you page, exclude visitors to that URL)
- Name: "CT - All Visitors 30 Days (No Conversion)"

**Use this for:** Ads 1, 2, 4, 5

---

**Audience 2: Pricing Page Visitors (High Intent)**
- Source: Your Facebook Pixel
- Event: People who visited specific web pages
- URL contains: `/pricing` (adjust to match your actual pricing page URL)
- Retention: **14 days**
- Exclude: People who completed a purchase/signup
- Name: "CT - Pricing Page 14 Days (No Conversion)"

**Use this for:** Ads 2, 3, 6 (these are your warmest leads -- hit them with social proof, free audit, and testimonials)

---

**Audience 3: Visited Multiple Pages (Engaged Visitors)**
- Source: Your Facebook Pixel
- Event: People who visited specific web pages
- Frequency: Visited **2 or more** times
- Retention: **30 days**
- Exclude: People who completed a purchase/signup
- Name: "CT - Engaged Visitors 30 Days (No Conversion)"

**Use this for:** Ads 1, 3, 5

---

**Audience 4: Started Signup But Didn't Complete (Cart Abandonment Equivalent)**
- Source: Your Facebook Pixel
- Event: People who visited specific web pages
- URL contains: `/signup` or `/checkout` or `/register` (adjust to match your actual signup flow URL)
- Retention: **7 days**
- Exclude: People who visited the thank-you/confirmation page
- Name: "CT - Started Signup 7 Days (Didn't Complete)"

**Use this for:** Ads 1, 2, 6 (these people were ready to buy -- gentle nudge and social proof)

---

### Step 3: Create Lookalike Audiences (Bonus)

Once you have conversions flowing, create a Lookalike Audience:

1. Go to **Audiences** > **Create Audience** > **Lookalike Audience**
2. Source: Your converters/customers (use a Custom Audience of people who completed signup)
3. Location: United States
4. Audience size: 1% (most similar to your customers)

This gives you a cold audience of people who look like your buyers -- useful for prospecting campaigns alongside your retargeting.

---

### Step 4: Set Up the Retargeting Campaign

1. Go to **Ads Manager** > **Create Campaign**
2. Campaign objective: **Leads** or **Traffic** (use Leads if you want form submissions; use Traffic if you want website visits)
3. Campaign name: "The Call Taker - Retargeting"
4. At the **Ad Set level:**
   - Select your Custom Audience (start with Audience 1 for broadest reach)
   - Placements: **Automatic** (Facebook + Instagram feeds, stories, reels)
   - Budget: See budget recommendations below
   - Schedule: Run continuously
5. At the **Ad level:**
   - Upload your ad creative (image or video)
   - Paste the ad copy from above
   - Set the destination URL to thecalltaker.com
6. Publish and monitor

# BUDGET RECOMMENDATIONS

## The $5/Day Strategy (Conservative / Getting Started)

**Total monthly spend:** ~$150/month
**Best for:** Just starting retargeting, small website traffic (under 500 visitors/month)

**Setup:**
- One campaign, one ad set, rotating 2-3 ads at a time
- Target: Audience 1 (All Website Visitors, 30 days)
- Run Ads 1, 4, and 5 in rotation
- Let Facebook optimize delivery

**Expected results:**
- Reach: 500-1,500 people per month (depending on audience size)
- Frequency: 3-5 times per person over 30 days (ideal for retargeting)
- Clicks: 50-150 per month
- Conversions: 1-3 sign-ups per month (at $297/mo, that's $297-$891/mo revenue from $150 spend)

**Why this works:** Retargeting audiences are small and warm. You don't need a big budget because you're only showing ads to people who already visited your site. $5/day is enough to stay visible without overexposing.

**Total monthly spend:** ~$225/month
**Best for:** Moderate website traffic (500-2,000 visitors/month)

**Setup:**
- Two ad sets running simultaneously:
  - **Ad Set 1: Broad retargeting** ($4/day) -- Audience 1 (All Visitors, 30 days) -- Run Ads 1, 4, 5
  - **Ad Set 2: High-intent retargeting** ($3.50/day) -- Audience 2 (Pricing Page, 14 days) -- Run Ads 2, 3, 6
- This lets you show different messaging to different intent levels

**Expected results:**
- Higher conversion rate from the high-intent audience
- 2-5 sign-ups per month
- Revenue: $594-$2,485/mo from $225 spend
- ROI: 2.5x to 11x return

## The $10/Day Strategy (Aggressive / Maximum Capture)

**Total monthly spend:** ~$300/month
**Best for:** Higher website traffic (2,000+ visitors/month), or when you want to be more aggressive during peak HVAC season

**Setup:**
- Three ad sets:
  - **Ad Set 1: All visitors** ($3/day) -- Audience 1 -- Ads 1, 5
  - **Ad Set 2: Pricing page visitors** ($4/day) -- Audience 2 -- Ads 2, 3, 6
  - **Ad Set 3: Signup abandoners** ($3/day) -- Audience 4 -- Ads 1, 2, 6
- This covers every stage of the funnel with tailored messaging

**Expected results:**
- 3-8 sign-ups per month
- Revenue: $891-$3,976/mo from $300 spend
- ROI: 3x to 13x return

---

## Budget Optimization Tips:

1. **Start at $5/day.** Scale up only after you see data. Don't throw money at ads that aren't converting.
2. **Check results weekly.** Look at cost per click (CPC), click-through rate (CTR), and cost per conversion. For retargeting, aim for: CPC under $2, CTR above 1.5%, cost per conversion under $100.
3. **Increase budget during peak season** (June-August for AC, December-February for heating). These are the months when HVAC owners feel the pain of missed calls most acutely.
4. **Decrease budget during slow periods** if your website traffic drops. No point retargeting if nobody is visiting the site.
5. **Refresh ad creative every 3-4 weeks.** Retargeting audiences are small, so they'll see your ads frequently. Swap in new copy and images to avoid ad fatigue.
6. **Kill underperformers fast.** If an ad has spent $20 and gotten zero clicks, turn it off and try something else.

# RECOMMENDED RETARGETING TIMELINE

## How Long to Follow Up (And When to Move On)

### Overview:

Retargeting is most effective in the first 7 days after someone visits your site. Interest decays rapidly after that. Here's the optimal timeline:

---

### Days 1-3: Hot Window (Highest Priority)

**What to show:** Gentle reminder (Ad 1), Demo line CTA (Ad 4)

**Why:** They just visited your site. The problem is fresh in their mind. They were actively looking for a solution. A simple reminder is often enough to bring them back.

**Email:** If they started the form, Email 1 fires at 1 hour, Email 2 fires at 24 hours.

**Budget allocation:** 40% of your daily retargeting budget should focus on this window (use Audience 4 -- signup abandoners at 7-day retention, and weight it here).

---

### Days 4-7: Warm Window (Build Urgency)

**What to show:** Social proof/FOMO (Ad 2), Free audit offer (Ad 3), Seasonal urgency (Ad 5)

**Why:** They didn't come back immediately. Now you need to give them a reason. Social proof shows them others are doing it. The free audit gives them a no-commitment way to engage. Seasonal urgency adds a time element.

**Email:** Email 3 fires at 72 hours (the final direct ask).

**Budget allocation:** 35% of your daily retargeting budget.

---

### Days 8-14: Cooling Window (Re-Engage with Value)

**What to show:** Testimonial/proof (Ad 6), Free audit offer (Ad 3)

**Why:** Interest is fading. At this point, you need a stronger hook. A testimonial with real numbers (jobs booked, revenue captured) can reignite interest. The free audit is a low-barrier way to restart the conversation.

**Budget allocation:** 15% of your daily retargeting budget.

---

### Days 15-30: Cold Window (Low-Frequency Reminder)

**What to show:** Seasonal urgency (Ad 5), Demo line CTA (Ad 4)

**Why:** They've gone two weeks without converting. Don't hammer them. Show an ad once or twice a week at most. The seasonal angle keeps it relevant. The demo line CTA gives them an easy, zero-commitment action.

**Budget allocation:** 10% of your daily retargeting budget.

---

### After 30 Days: Stop Active Retargeting

**Why:** After 30 days, the visit is too old. Continuing to retarget past this point leads to ad fatigue, wasted budget, and potential negative brand perception ("why won't they stop showing me this ad?").

**What to do instead:**
- Move them to a **Lookalike Audience** seed (use their data to find similar people)
- Add them to a **monthly email newsletter** (if they gave you their email) with HVAC industry tips, missed call stats, and occasional soft pitches
- If they visit the site again, the retargeting window resets and they re-enter the funnel at Day 1

## Key Rules for Retargeting:

1. **Frequency cap:** No more than 1-2 ad impressions per person per day. More than that and you become annoying instead of persuasive.
2. **Exclude converters immediately.** The moment someone signs up, remove them from all retargeting audiences. Nothing kills trust faster than showing ads for something they already bought.
3. **Rotate creative.** Swap ad copy and images every 3-4 weeks to prevent fatigue.
4. **Track the full funnel.** Don't just look at ad clicks. Track: ad impression > click > site visit > form start > form completion > paying customer. This shows you where the drop-off is happening.
5. **Retargeting is a supplement, not a strategy.** It only works if you're driving traffic to thecalltaker.com in the first place. Retargeting recaptures lost visitors -- it doesn't create new ones. Pair it with your prospecting campaigns (Google Ads, Facebook cold ads, SEO, webinars) for maximum impact.
6. **Respect the timeline.** Aggressive retargeting works for days 1-7. After that, ease off. After 30 days, stop. Knowing when to let go is just as important as knowing when to push.
7. **Test everything.** Run two ads at a time in each ad set. After 7 days, kill the lower performer and introduce a new variation. Continuous testing beats "set it and forget it" every time.
