## Usage Guide

| Situation | Signature to Use |
|---|---|
| Cold email to a new lead | Full signature |
| Sending a proposal or audit report | Full signature |
| Replying to an ongoing thread | Short signature |
| Following up after a demo | Short signature |
| LinkedIn message that moves to email | Full signature (first email), then short |
| Email to a referral partner | Full signature |
| Casual check-in with existing client | Short signature |

## Pro Tips

- **Always include the demo line in cold emails.** It is the single best CTA. Curiosity makes people call it.
- **Do not use the tagline in every reply.** It gets repetitive. Save it for first impressions.
- **Link the Free Audit page** whenever you are prospecting. It gives them an easy, no-commitment next step.
- **The green accent (#10b981)** matches The Call Taker brand. Keep it consistent across all touchpoints.
