## PLATFORM RULES & TIPS (Read Before Posting)

### Craigslist Nashville — What Gets You Flagged
- Do NOT use ALL CAPS in the title (a few caps words are fine, full caps = flagged)
- Do NOT include links in the body more than once — one URL max, put it at the bottom
- Do NOT use the words "guaranteed," "free money," "make $$$," or anything that sounds like MLM
- Avoid phrases like "work from home" or "be your own boss" — they trigger spam filters
- Do NOT repost the same ad within 48 hours. Delete the old one first, then repost.
- Craigslist Nashville URL: https://nashville.craigslist.org
- Post from a verified Craigslist account with a Nashville-area phone number

### Nextdoor
- You MUST have a verified Nextdoor account tied to a real address in the Nashville metro
- Do NOT hard-sell. Nextdoor users flag overly promotional posts fast.
- Frame everything as neighbor-to-neighbor, not business-to-consumer
- Post in "General" or "Recommendations" — NOT in "For Sale" (services aren't items)
- You can post from a Business Page if you've claimed one, but personal posts get more reach

### Facebook Marketplace
- Services go under "Home Services" or you can list in the general Marketplace feed
- Facebook may restrict your account if you post the same thing too many times — rotate copy
- Include a real photo (even a simple one of your desk, phone, or logo) — posts with images get 3x more views
- Do NOT include your phone number in the main image — Facebook sometimes flags that

### Google Business Profile Posts
- Posts expire after 7 days. You MUST repost weekly.
- Keep posts under 300 words (Google truncates at ~1,500 characters)
- Always include a CTA button ("Call now," "Learn more," or "Book online")
- Upload an image with every post — plain color backgrounds with text work fine
