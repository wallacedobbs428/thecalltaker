## SECTION 2: GUERRILLA STRATEGY -- Working a Trade Show WITHOUT a Booth

> You do NOT need to spend $5,000 on a booth to sign clients. Some of the best salespeople at trade shows never have a booth. They work the floor.

---

### How to Network the Floor

**Before the Show:**
- Register for the expo hall pass (often free or cheap)
- Download the event app -- study the exhibitor list and attendee list if available
- Identify HVAC companies in attendance and research them (Google their company, check reviews, see if they have an answering service)
- Set a goal: **Talk to 30 HVAC owners per day. Collect 15 business cards. Book 5 demos.**
- Charge your phone to 100%. Bring a portable battery pack.

**At the Show:**
- **Arrive early.** The first hour is when people are fresh and open to talking.
- **Walk the floor slowly.** Do not rush. Stop at booths. Talk to the people NEXT to you at booths -- they are often contractors, not vendors.
- **Hang out at the high-traffic booths** (Lennox, Trane, Carrier, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro). The contractors flock there. Strike up side conversations.
- **Work the food areas.** Breakfast, coffee stations, lunch lines. People are relaxed and talkative.
- **Attend the breakout sessions.** Sit next to people. Chat before and after.
- **Hit the hotel lobby bar at 5-7 PM.** This is where the REAL deals happen at every trade show. Buy someone a drink.
- **Wear your branded polo or shirt** (The Call Taker logo). It is a conversation starter even when you are not talking.

**Body Language:**
- Keep your hands free (no food plates, no bags in both hands)
- Make eye contact and nod at people as you walk
- Stand at the edges of groups -- people will naturally include you
- Never sit down in the expo hall unless you are having a conversation

---

### Conversation Starters with HVAC Owners

> The goal is to get them talking about THEIR business. Then bridge to missed calls.

**Opener 1 -- The Compliment:**
> "Hey, I saw your company shirt -- [Company Name]. How long have you guys been in business?"

**Opener 2 -- The Question:**
> "So what's been the best thing you've seen at the show so far?"

**Opener 3 -- The Pain Point:**
> "Man, I've been talking to a lot of HVAC owners today and everyone keeps saying the same thing -- they can't find enough techs. You dealing with that too?"

**Opener 4 -- The Direct:**
> "Hey, I'm Wallace. I work with HVAC companies. Quick question -- do you know how many calls your shop misses every week?"

**Opener 5 -- The Lobby Bar:**
> "Long day on the floor, huh? What company are you with?"

**The Bridge to The Call Taker (use after any opener):**
> "Yeah, so what I do is kind of interesting -- I built an AI receptionist specifically for HVAC companies. It answers every call, 24/7, books the appointment right into your calendar. No hold music, no voicemail, no missed revenue. Want to hear something wild? Call this number right now."

---

### Business Card Strategy

**Giving Your Card:**
- Do NOT hand your card to everyone you see. That is spam.
- Only hand your card AFTER a real conversation.
- When you hand it, say: **"Here's my card -- that demo number is on there. Call it tonight from your couch and you'll see exactly what your customers would experience."**
- Write their name and a note on the BACK of your card before handing it: *"John -- call the demo line, it'll blow your mind."* This makes them keep it.

**Collecting Their Card:**
- Always ask: **"Can I grab your card? I'd love to send you a quick case study on what this does for shops like yours."**
- If they do not have a card, ask: **"What's the best number to text you? I'll shoot you the demo link right now."** Then text them immediately while standing there.
- On the back of EVERY card you collect, write:
  - Where you met them (AHR Expo, Booth 4200, etc.)
  - What they said their biggest problem was
  - Whether they saw the demo (Y/N)
  - Rate their interest: HOT / WARM / COLD

### How to Do Live Demos on the Spot

> This is your nuclear weapon. No other vendor at the show can do this.

**The Move:**
1. Pull out your phone
2. Say: **"Here, I can show you in 30 seconds. Call this number."**
3. Hand them YOUR phone with the number already dialed: **(615) 784-5747**
4. Let them listen to it ring once and the AI picks up
5. Tell them: **"Ask it to book an appointment for an AC repair. Just talk to it like a normal person."**
6. Watch their face. They will be impressed.
7. When they hang up, say: **"That's what your customers hear instead of voicemail. Every call. 24/7. And it books right into your calendar."**

**Pro Tips for Live Demos:**
- If the trade show floor is LOUD, say: **"It's noisy in here -- let me text you the demo line. Call it from your hotel room tonight when it's quiet. You'll be blown away."**
- If they say they are busy: **"Totally get it. Let me text you the number right now -- call it tonight at 10 PM. That's actually the point -- it works at 10 PM."**
- If you are at dinner or at the bar: This is actually the BEST time to demo. It is quiet, people are relaxed, and you have a captive audience. Demo for one person and the whole table will want to hear it.
- **Always demo on THEIR phone if possible.** When they call from their phone, the number is now in their call log. They WILL call it again later.

## SECTION 5: THE FOLLOW-UP -- What to Do With Every Business Card

> **Rule: Every business card must be acted on within 24 hours. After 48 hours, they have forgotten your face.**

---

### The 24-Hour Follow-Up System

**Hour 0-2 (Same Day, After the Event):**

1. **Photograph every card** using your phone camera or a card-scanning app (CamCard, HubSpot card scanner, or just your iPhone camera). This creates a digital backup.

2. **Sort cards into three piles:**
   - **HOT** -- They saw the demo, were impressed, asked questions about pricing, or said "send me info"
   - **WARM** -- Good conversation, showed interest, but did not commit to anything
   - **COLD** -- Polite exchange, grabbed your card too, no strong signal

3. **Enter every contact into your CRM or spreadsheet** with these fields:
   - Name
   - Company
   - Phone
   - Email
   - Where you met them
   - What they said / pain point
   - Interest level (HOT/WARM/COLD)
   - Demo seen? (Y/N)
   - Follow-up date

---

**Hour 2-12 (Same Evening):**

**For HOT leads -- Personal text message:**
> "Hey [First Name], it's Wallace from The Call Taker. Great meeting you at [Event Name] today. Loved hearing about [something specific they said]. As promised, here's the demo line if you want to call it again: (615) 784-5747. I'll send over a custom proposal tomorrow. Talk soon."

**For WARM leads -- Personal text message:**
> "Hey [First Name], Wallace here -- we chatted at [Event Name] today about [topic]. Wanted to send you our demo line in case you want to hear the AI in action: (615) 784-5747. Call it anytime, even at 2 AM -- that's kind of the whole point. Let me know if you'd like to see how it'd work for [their company name]."

**For COLD leads -- Simple text:**
> "Hey [First Name], it was good meeting you at [Event Name]. I'm Wallace with The Call Taker -- AI receptionist for HVAC companies. If you ever want to check it out: (615) 784-5747. Have a good one."

---

**Hour 12-24 (Next Morning):**

**For HOT leads -- Email with proposal:**
- Subject: "[First Name] -- Here's the Call Taker proposal for [Company Name]"
- Attach the one-page proposal (customized with their company name)
- Include a link to thecalltaker.com
- Include the demo line number
- End with: "Want to jump on a 15-minute call this week to walk through it?"

**For WARM leads -- Email with value:**
- Subject: "The missed call problem we talked about at [Event Name]"
- Share a quick stat: "Just wanted to follow up on our conversation. The average HVAC company misses 37% of incoming calls. That is real money walking out the door."
- Include the demo line
- End with: "Happy to run a free missed-call audit for [Company Name] if you're curious. Just reply and I'll set it up."

**For COLD leads -- Add to email nurture sequence:**
- Add them to your cold email drip (from your existing cold email sequence)
- Tag them with the event name so you can reference it

---

**Day 3-5:**
- Call every HOT lead who has not responded to your text or email
- Script: "Hey [Name], it's Wallace -- we met at [Event]. Just wanted to follow up on that proposal I sent over. Did you get a chance to call the demo line?"

**Day 7:**
- Send a second follow-up to WARM leads who have not responded
- Try a different channel (if you texted, now email; if you emailed, now text)

**Day 14:**
- Final personal follow-up for non-responders
- "Hey [Name], just circling back one last time. If the timing isn't right, no worries at all. The demo line is always live if you want to hear it: (615) 784-5747. Whenever you're ready."

**Day 14+:**
- Move non-responders into your long-term nurture email sequence
- They will hear from you monthly with case studies, tips, and reminders

### Design Specifications

- **Size:** Standard 3.5" x 2"
- **Color scheme:** Dark background (black or dark navy) with white text and one accent color (orange, green, or electric blue)
- **Font:** Clean, modern sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or similar)
- **Logo:** The Call Taker logo, prominently placed top of front
- **Paper:** Thick cardstock (16pt minimum) with matte or soft-touch finish -- feels premium
- **The demo number should be the LARGEST text on the card** -- bigger than your name, bigger than the logo. The number IS the pitch.
- **QR code (optional):** Small QR code on the back that dials the demo line or goes to thecalltaker.com

---

### What NOT to Put on the Card

- **Do NOT put "AI" in giant letters.** Some older HVAC owners are skeptical of AI. Lead with the benefit ("Never miss a call"), not the technology.
- **Do NOT list your personal cell phone number.** Use only the demo line and your email. If they call the demo line, you win. If they want to reach you personally, email works.
- **Do NOT put a long tagline or paragraph.** The card is not a brochure. Keep it minimal.
- **Do NOT put your home address.** "Brentwood, TN" is enough.
- **Do NOT put every social media handle.** No Twitter, no Instagram, no LinkedIn on the card. One website. One phone number. One email. That is it.
- **Do NOT use a generic title like "CEO" or "President."** Use "Founder" -- it is more relatable and less corporate for a small-business audience.
- **Do NOT use clip art, stock photos, or phone icons.** Clean and professional. Let the words do the work.
- **Do NOT print on thin, flimsy paper.** A cheap card says cheap product. Invest in quality stock.
