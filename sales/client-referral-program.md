## 1. Program Overview

The Inner Circle Referral Program rewards existing Call Taker clients for introducing other HVAC companies (and qualifying trade businesses) to the service. Every referral that converts to a paying client earns the referring client real money -- either as a one-time payout or applied as a credit toward their monthly subscription.

This is not a favor. It is a formal, trackable program with clear terms and reliable payouts.

## 2. Commission Structure (Recommended: Hybrid Model)

After evaluating flat-fee, percentage-based, and tiered approaches, the recommended structure is a **hybrid model** that combines an attractive upfront payout with a smaller recurring component. This gives referrers an immediate reason to act while also rewarding long-term value.

### Payout Table

| Referred Client Signs Up For | One-Time Bonus (Paid at 30 Days) | Recurring Monthly Credit | Referrer Earns in Year 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter Plan ($297/mo) | $150 cash or account credit | $25/mo credit for 6 months | $300 total |
| Professional Plan ($497/mo) | $200 cash or account credit | $35/mo credit for 6 months | $410 total |

### Key Rules

- The referred client must remain active and paid for at least 30 days before the one-time bonus is issued.
- Recurring credits begin in month 2 of the referred client's subscription and continue for 6 months, contingent on the referred client remaining active.
- There is no cap on the number of referrals. A client who refers 5 companies earning Professional plans would receive $2,050 in Year 1.
- Referrers may choose cash (via check or Zelle) or account credit. Account credit is applied automatically.
- Referrals must be net-new clients. A referral does not count if the prospect is already in conversation with The Call Taker.

### Why This Structure Works

- **$150-$200 upfront** is large enough to motivate action. Flat $50 payouts do not move the needle for business owners.
- **Recurring credits** keep referrers thinking about the program month after month, not just once.
- **6-month cap on recurring** protects margin. At $25-$35/mo for 6 months, total cost per referral stays well under the lifetime value of an acquired client.
- **30-day activation window** prevents payouts on clients who cancel immediately.

## 9. Launch Timing

### Recommended: Launch After Your 3rd Paying Client

**Rationale:**

- With 3 active clients, you have enough social proof that the service works and enough operational experience to handle incoming demos smoothly.
- Launching with 0-2 clients risks referrals coming in before you have refined the onboarding process.
- 3 clients also means 3 potential referrers, which is a meaningful starting pool.

### Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] Referral tracking sheet or GHL pipeline is set up and tested
- [ ] Referral agreement template is finalized (printed and digital versions)
- [ ] Email templates are loaded into your email tool or GHL
- [ ] Text message templates are saved in your phone or GHL
- [ ] Social media post is drafted and scheduled
- [ ] A simple referral page exists at thecalltaker.com/referral (even a basic landing page that explains the program and has a contact form)
- [ ] Payout method is established (Zelle account funded, checks available, or credit system ready in billing)

### Launch Sequence

## 10. Revenue Projection

### Conservative Scenario

- 10 active clients after 6 months
- 30% participate in the referral program (3 clients)
- Each participating client refers 1 company per quarter
- 50% of referrals convert

**Result:** 3 referrers x 1 referral/quarter x 50% close rate = ~1.5 new clients per quarter from referrals alone, at a cost of ~$300-$410 per acquisition (well below the LTV of a client paying $297-$497/month).

### Growth Scenario

- 25 active clients after 12 months
- 30% participate (7-8 clients)
- Each refers 1-2 per quarter
- 50% convert

**Result:** 4-8 new clients per quarter from referrals. At Professional pricing, that is $1,988-$3,976/month in added recurring revenue per quarter.

Referral programs typically account for 15-30% of total new client acquisition for service businesses. This program is designed to hit that range within the first year.
