# The Call Taker — Secret Shopper Log

## What This Is

**This is your best sales weapon.**

Call HVAC companies after hours. The ones that go to voicemail are your hottest prospects.

Every HVAC owner thinks they're handling their calls fine — until you show them proof that they're not. This log gives you that proof. When you call a prospect and say, "I called your business last night at 7pm and got voicemail. So did 5 of your customers," that's not a sales pitch. That's a fact they can't argue with.

Do this consistently, and you'll never run out of warm leads.

### Result Codes

- **VM** — Went to voicemail (HOT LEAD)
- **NA** — Rang but no answer (HOT LEAD)
- **ANS** — Answered by a person
- **SVC** — Answered by an answering service
- **AI** — Answered by an AI/automated system (competitor already using something)
- **OOO** — Out of office recording, no voicemail option

## How to Use This

### When to Call

- **Weekday evenings:** 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM (prime time — this is when customers call with emergencies)
- **Saturday mornings:** 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM
- **Saturday evenings:** 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM
- **Sundays:** Anytime (most shops are closed — high voicemail rate)

### What to Do

1. **Pull a list.** Google "HVAC companies near me" or "AC repair [city]." Go through the results one by one. Target companies with 1-50 trucks — check their website and reviews to estimate size.

2. **Call the number.** Use your cell phone. Don't block your number — you want it to look like a real customer call.

3. **If it rings to voicemail:** Log it as VM. This is your hottest prospect. They're losing money right now.

4. **If it rings and nobody answers:** Log it as NA. Same as voicemail — they're losing calls.

5. **If a person answers:** Log it as ANS. Note what happened — were they professional? Did they seem rushed? They might still be a prospect if the owner is the one answering and they're burned out.

6. **If an answering service picks up:** Log it as SVC. Still a prospect — answering services are expensive and often do a bad job. Ask them a couple of questions and see how they handle it.

7. **If an AI answers:** Log it as AI. They already have a solution. Move on, or note it for later if you want to pitch a switch.

8. **Log everything.** Fill in every column. The Notes field is important — write down the voicemail greeting, whether it sounded professional, anything useful.

### How to Follow Up

**For VM and NA results (your hottest leads):**

Call them during business hours the next day or the day after. Open with:

> "Hey [Name], this is Wallace with The Call Taker. I called your business last night at [time] and got your voicemail. I'm not a customer, but I guarantee some of your real customers did the same thing — and 85% of them aren't going to leave a message. They're going to call someone else."

Then transition into the cold call script.

**For SVC results (answering service users):**

> "Hey [Name], this is Wallace with The Call Taker. I called your business last night and talked to your answering service. Quick question — are you happy with what they're doing? Because what I heard was [describe the experience]. I've got something that does a better job for less money."

**For ANS results (owner answered personally):**

> "Hey [Name], this is Wallace with The Call Taker. I called your business last night at [time] and you actually picked up — respect. But I'm guessing you'd rather not be answering the phone at 8pm every night. Am I wrong?"

## Tips for Maximizing Effectiveness

1. **Do 20-30 calls per session.** Block out 1-2 hours. Make it a routine — Tuesday and Thursday evenings, for example.

2. **Track your conversion rate.** After a month, look at how many VM/NA results turned into demos, and how many demos turned into customers. This tells you how many calls you need to make to hit your sales targets.

3. **Call the same company twice.** If they went to voicemail on a Tuesday night, try again Saturday morning. If they go to voicemail both times, that's even stronger proof to use in your pitch.

4. **Screenshot the voicemail.** If your phone shows a missed call log or voicemail notification, screenshot it. Some salespeople email the screenshot to the prospect: "This is what your customers see."

5. **Batch by area.** Call every HVAC company in one city or ZIP code in one session. This way you can say to each prospect, "I called 15 HVAC companies in [city] last night. 11 went to voicemail. Yours was one of them."

6. **Prioritize companies with strong Google presence.** If they're paying for ads or have a lot of reviews, they're investing in getting the phone to ring. But if nobody's answering, that investment is wasted. That's a powerful angle.

7. **Don't just call — take notes.** Write down the voicemail greeting. Was it professional? Was it the owner's personal cell voicemail? Was the mailbox full? Every detail is ammunition for the follow-up call.

8. **Re-shop every 30 days.** Companies change. The one that answered last month might be going to voicemail now. Keep the list fresh.

## Your Goal

**Build a pipeline of 50+ secret-shopped companies, with follow-up dates set for every one that went to voicemail.**

If 60-70% of HVAC companies go to voicemail after hours (they will), a list of 50 gives you 30-35 hot leads. Even a 10% close rate from there is 3-4 new customers per batch.

Rinse and repeat every month in new areas.
