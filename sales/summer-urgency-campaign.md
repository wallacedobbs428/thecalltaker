## Sequence Notes

- **Timing:** Start sending mid-February. Email 1 on Monday/Tuesday. Each follow-up 3-4 days apart. Entire sequence runs about 2.5 weeks.
- **Best send times:** 6:00-7:00 AM or 5:00-6:00 PM (before they're on a job or after they wrap up).
- **If they reply to any email:** Stop the sequence immediately and move to personal conversation.
- **If no reply after all 5:** Wait 2 weeks, then move them into the standard cold email sequence or call them using the cold call script.
- **Resend strategy:** This campaign is seasonal. If a prospect doesn't bite in February/March, send a condensed 2-email version in late April with updated urgency ("Summer is 6 weeks away").
- **Personalization matters:** Use their real company name and city. If you've secret-shopped them and found they go to voicemail, mention it.
