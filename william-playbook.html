01 — The Problem We Solve

What Is The Call Taker?

The Call Taker is an AI receptionist built for service businesses across 8 industries: HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing, Dental, Med Spa, Legal, and Property Management.

The scenario: A customer has an urgent need at 9pm on a Saturday. They call your business. Nobody answers. They get voicemail.

80% of people who hit voicemail will never call back — they just call the next company on the list. That one missed call is a $350 - $10,000+ job gone forever.

The Call Taker answers every call in under 2 seconds, 24/7/365. It sounds like a real person. It gets the caller's name, address, and what they need. It books the appointment. Then it texts the business owner all the details.

The owner wakes up to a text with a booked job instead of a missed call they never knew about.

08 — Handle Every Objection

Objection Handling

Memorize these responses. Deliver them naturally, not scripted.

"It's too expensive"
"How much is one job or client worth to you? $350? $1,000? $5,000? The Starter plan is $497/mo. If it saves ONE missed call per month, it's already paid for itself. Everything after that is pure profit. Ruby charges $705 for less."
"I already have an answering service"
"That's great. When's the last time you called your own business after hours? A lot of owners are surprised. Our AI is specifically trained for your industry — it knows the terminology, knows what's urgent, and it's the same quality every single time."
"I need to think about it"
"Absolutely. That's exactly why we do the 7-day pilot — there's nothing to think about because there's no risk. Let me set it up for free. Use it for a week. See the calls. Then decide with real data. Sound fair?"
"I'm not sure AI can handle calls for my business"
"You already heard it on the demo line. Did it sound like a robot? It handles calls for your industry all day — whatever your customers call about. Anything it can't handle routes to your emergency line. Call (615) 784-5747 anytime."
"Can I talk to other customers using it?"
"We're in early launch — which is great for you. We're offering the 7-day free trial and the lowest pricing we'll ever have. After launch, prices go up. Lock in $497/mo now with no contract."
"My wife/partner handles the phones"
"What about when she's busy, or it's 10pm, or you're both at your kid's game? This doesn't replace her — it backs her up. Every call she can't get to, the AI handles. She gets her time back, you get every job."
"I don't get that many after-hours calls"
"You only know about the calls where someone left a voicemail. The ones who hung up and called your competitor? You never knew about those. That's the invisible revenue leak. The 7-day pilot will show you exactly how many calls you're missing."
"Let me check with my business partner"
"Of course. Would it help if I set up the free pilot so you both can see it in action? That way you're showing real results, not describing it. No commitment. I just need 10 minutes to set it up."

What Happens After They Say Yes

  1. Tell Wallace: "[Company Name] is in. Here's their info."
