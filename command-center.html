Cold Call Scripts
Rule #1: You're not selling. You're pointing out a problem they already have. Say less. Let them talk. The second they start venting about missed calls, you've won.
Opener A — Straight Up
Direct — no games, just a real question
Hey [Name], this is Mills. I'll be real with you — I'm not a customer. I work with restoration and HVAC companies on their phones. Quick question though, when someone calls your shop and you're out on a job, what happens to that call?
When they say "voicemail" or "yeah we miss some"
Yeah that's what most guys tell me. The problem is like 80% of people won't leave a voicemail — they just hang up and call the next company on Google. So you're losing jobs and you don't even know it because the phone never rang twice.
Opener B — Cold (no prior call)
Use when calling straight from the call sheet
Hey [Name], real quick — I work with restoration companies and I gotta ask, what happens to your calls when you're out on a job? Like if someone's got water pouring through their ceiling right now and they call you, who's picking up?
The Pitch — Only after they admit the problem
Keep it casual — don't sound rehearsed
So we built this thing that basically answers your phone for you. Like, it actually talks to the person, figures out what's wrong, gets their address, and books them on your schedule. Then it texts you everything. It's not a robot menu thing — it sounds like someone at your front desk.
The Close — Get them to call the demo line
This is the move — hearing it sells itself
Tell you what, I'm not gonna talk your ear off about it. Pull out your phone and call (615) 784-5747. Just pretend you've got water damage or a broken AC and see what happens. Takes like 30 seconds. If you think it sounds fake I'll never bother you again.
When They Push Back
"How much?" / "I can't afford that"
It's $997 a month. But think about it this way — how much is one job worth to you? Like one water damage call, one AC install? If this thing catches even one call a month that would've gone to voicemail, it's already paid for itself. Most guys are missing way more than one.
"I don't trust AI" / "That's gonna sound weird"
I said the same thing honestly. But just call the number — (615) 784-5747. Talk to it for 30 seconds like you're a customer. If it sounds off to you, cool, no hard feelings. But every person I've had call it goes "wait, that's the AI?" It doesn't sound like what you think.
"Not right now" / "Let me think about it"
For sure, no rush. But real talk — how many calls came in this week that you couldn't get to? Every one of those was someone ready to pay you. I'll shoot you a text with the demo number so you can try it whenever. Sound good?
"I already have an answering service"
How much you paying for that? Most guys I talk to are spending $200-500 a month and the person answering doesn't know the difference between a slab leak and a toilet overflow. They just take a message. This actually handles the call — books the job, gets the details, texts you everything. And the customer never waits on hold.
"I answer all my own calls"
Respect. But what about when you're under a house or ripping out drywall and your phone's in the truck? Or it's 11pm and you're trying to sleep? Even if you catch 90% — that other 10% is real money walking to somebody else.
