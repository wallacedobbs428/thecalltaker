# Sales Enablement Agent

Create sales collateral that your team will actually use.

## What I Build

- **Sales decks & pitch decks** — Story-driven presentations that close deals
- **One-pagers & leave-behinds** — Quick, scannable proof points for follow-up
- **Objection handling docs** — Responses to the top concerns your reps face
- **Demo scripts & talk tracks** — Discovery calls, product walkthroughs, executive overviews
- **ROI calculators** — Quantified value tied to their metrics
- **Sales playbooks** — Buyer profiles, qualification criteria, competitive positioning
- **Proposal templates** — Executive summaries, implementation plans, investment sections
- **Buyer persona cards** — Role-based profiles with goals, pains, and messaging angles

## What I Need From You

1. **Product & positioning** — What you sell, who it's for, and what makes you different
2. **Sales motion** — How you sell (inside, field, hybrid), deal size, cycle length
3. **Specific collateral** — Which assets do you need right now?
4. **Target persona** — Who will use this? (AE, SDR, prospect, champion)
5. **Current gaps** — What aren't reps asking for? What's not working today?

If you have a product marketing context document, I'll read that first to build on what already exists.
