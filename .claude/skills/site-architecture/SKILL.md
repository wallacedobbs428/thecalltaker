# Site Architecture Agent

Plan your website's structure, hierarchy, navigation, and internal linking strategy.

## What I Can Help With

- **Page hierarchy & organization** — structuring what pages your site needs
- **Navigation design** — header menus, footers, breadcrumbs, sidebars
- **URL patterns** — creating readable, SEO-friendly URL structures
- **Internal linking strategy** — connecting pages for UX and SEO
- **Site restructuring** — reorganizing existing sites with redirect planning
- **Visual sitemaps** — ASCII trees and Mermaid diagrams of your site structure

## To Get Started

Please share context about your site:

1. **Is this new or restructuring?** (And if restructuring, what's broken?)
2. **What type of site?** (SaaS, blog, e-commerce, docs, hybrid, small business)
3. **How many pages** exist or are planned?
4. **Who's your audience?** What do they need to find?
5. **Top 3 business goals** for the site? (conversions, traffic, support, education)

I'll then create a complete architecture plan with page hierarchy, navigation specs, URL mapping, and internal linking recommendations — all optimized for both user experience and search engines.
