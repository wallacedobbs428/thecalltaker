# Post to X — Social Media Content Engine

Create platform-optimized content that drives engagement and leads.

---

## Platform Formats

### X (Twitter)
- **Max length:** 280 chars (threads for longer content)
- **Best times:** 8-10am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm (weekdays)
- **Format:** Hook → Value → CTA
- **Hashtags:** 1-2 max, industry-specific

### LinkedIn
- **Max length:** 3,000 chars
- **Best times:** Tue-Thu 8-10am
- **Format:** Hook line → Story → Insight → CTA
- **Hashtags:** 3-5

### Instagram
- **Max length:** 2,200 chars (caption)
- **Best times:** Mon/Wed/Fri 11am-1pm
- **Format:** Strong visual + caption with hook
- **Hashtags:** 10-15 in first comment

### Facebook
- **Max length:** ~500 chars optimal
- **Best times:** Wed 11am, Fri 10-11am
- **Format:** Question/story → Value → CTA

## Content Pillars for The Call Taker

### 1. Pain Posts (40% of content)
Hit the nerve — missed calls, lost revenue, after-hours failures.

```
Your phone rang at 9:47 PM last night.

Nobody picked up.

That was a $2,400 emergency plumbing job
that went to your competitor instead.

How many times has this happened this month?

(DM "CALLS" and I'll show you exactly how many jobs you're losing)
```

### 2. Proof Posts (30% of content)
Results, numbers, before/after, testimonials.

```
3 weeks ago, Arctic Air Pros was missing 40% of after-hours calls.

Today:
- 0 missed calls
- 47 calls answered by AI
- $12,800 in booked jobs
- Owner sleeps through the night

$97/month changed their business.
```

### 3. Education Posts (20% of content)
Tips, industry insights, how-tos.

```
The #1 reason HVAC companies lose customers:

Not bad work.
Not high prices.

Slow response time.

78% of customers hire the first company that picks up.

If your phone goes to voicemail after hours,
you're giving away 40% of your revenue.

Here's what the top shops do differently...
```

### 4. Behind-the-Scenes (10% of content)
Building in public, founder story, team updates.

```
I'm 16 and I built an AI that answers phones
better than most receptionists.

It doesn't take breaks.
It doesn't call in sick.
It doesn't put people on hold.

And it costs less than a single lunch.

Building @TheCallTaker has taught me more
than any class ever could.
```

## Thread Format (X/Twitter)

```
Thread template:

1/ Hook tweet (this is what people see first — make it stop the scroll)

2/ Context / problem setup

3/ The insight or solution

4/ Proof / data / example

5/ CTA + what to do next

---

Example:

1/ I analyzed 500 service businesses.

The ones making $1M+ all have one thing in common.

(It's not what you think)

2/ It's not better marketing.
It's not lower prices.
It's not more trucks.

3/ It's SPEED.

They answer every single call.
First ring. Every time. 24/7.

4/ The data:
• 78% of customers hire whoever picks up first
• After-hours calls are 3x more likely to convert
• Average missed call = $300-2,400 in lost revenue

5/ We built an AI that answers in under 2 rings.

$97/month. 14-day free pilot.

DM me your industry and I'll show you a live demo.
```

## Engagement Rules

1. **Reply to every comment** within 2 hours
2. **Quote-tweet** industry news with your take
3. **DM** anyone who engages with a CTA post
4. **Follow** HVAC/plumbing/service business owners daily
5. **Never** use AI-sounding language (avoid: leverage, utilize, revolutionize, game-changer)
6. **Always** include a specific number or data point in pain/proof posts
