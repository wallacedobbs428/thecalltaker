## General Campaign Notes

### Landing Page
All ads link to: `thecalltaker.com/facebook-lead-ads/landing.html`
- Add `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={campaign_name}&utm_content={ad_name}` to each ad URL

### Budget Recommendations
- Start: $20/day per industry ($60/day total)
- Test all 3 variations per industry for 5 days
- Kill any ad with CPL (cost per lead) above $25
- Scale winners to $50-100/day

### Audience Size Targets
- Each industry audience: 50,000 - 500,000 people
- Lookalike: 1% based on website visitors or hot-100 lead list
- Retargeting: Website visitors last 30 days, video viewers 50%+

### Creative Notes
- Use real photos of service work (not stock)
- Video performs 2-3x better than static — record a 30-second Loom of the demo line
- Carousel: Before/after (missed call → answered call → booked job)

### Tracking
- Facebook Pixel fires `Lead` event on form submit
- UTM params captured by tct-tracking.js
- All leads tagged `facebook-lead` in GHL for attribution
- Weekly: Check CPL by industry, pause losers, scale winners

### What NOT to Do
- Never say "AI" or "artificial intelligence" — people don't trust it in ads
- Never say "bot" or "automated" — say "smart receptionist" or "24/7 answering"
- Never use fear tactics about technology — focus on MISSED REVENUE
- Never target competitors by name in ad copy (Meta will reject)
