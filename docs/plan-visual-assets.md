feel more operational and less like generic SaaS. These images are visual

## QA Verdict

Accepted for pricing-page use with dark overlay treatment:

- No readable phone numbers.
- No fake dashboard UI.
- No client proof or testimonial implication.
- No live activation claim.
- No provider routing implication.
- Works as background imagery under card overlays.

Known limitations:

- The visuals are generated stills, not real customer footage.
- They should not be used as proof images.
- If paid traffic increases, replace them with real service-business footage or
  approved founder-shot operational photography.

## Future Replacement Standard

Replacement images should preserve:

- Real service-business environment.
- Cinematic but believable lighting.
- No readable generated text.
- No fake UI or fake analytics.
- No claim that calls are already routed, activated, booked, or automated.
