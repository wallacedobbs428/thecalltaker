### Week 9: Focus on Highest-Converting Channels

**Monday: Analyze Your Data**

- Pull your tracking spreadsheet. For every channel, calculate:
  - Cost per conversation (time + money)
  - Cost per demo booked
  - Cost per client closed
- Rank your channels from best to worst
- **Cut the bottom 2 channels.** Reallocate that time to the top 3.

**Example Decision Matrix:**

| Channel | Convos | Demos | Closes | Time/Week | Keep? |
|---------|--------|-------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Cold calls | 15 | 5 | 2 | 8 hrs | YES |
| Cold emails | 8 | 2 | 1 | 3 hrs | YES |
| Loom videos | 6 | 3 | 1 | 4 hrs | YES |
| Cold texts | 4 | 1 | 0 | 2 hrs | REDUCE |
| Facebook groups | 3 | 1 | 0 | 5 hrs | REDUCE |
| LinkedIn | 2 | 0 | 0 | 3 hrs | CUT |
| Craigslist | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 hr | CUT |

Your numbers will be different. Trust the data, not your gut.

**Rest of Week 9:** Operate on your optimized schedule. Spend 80% of outreach time on your top 3 channels.
