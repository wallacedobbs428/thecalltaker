## 4. Data & Privacy

- **Call Recording:** Calls handled by Jessica may be recorded for quality assurance and script improvement. Jessica's greeting includes a brief recording notice where required by law.
- **Data Storage:** Call data (recordings, transcripts, caller information) is stored securely within GoHighLevel's infrastructure. Provider does not sell or share Customer data with third parties.
- **Data Retention:** Call records are retained for 90 days after cancellation unless Customer requests earlier deletion.
- **Customer Responsibility:** Customer is responsible for informing their own customers of call recording where required by applicable state law (one-party vs. two-party consent states).
- **HIPAA Notice (Healthcare Providers):** This service is NOT HIPAA-compliant. Jessica should not be used to collect, store, or transmit Protected Health Information (PHI). For dental, medical, or veterinary practices, Jessica should limit interactions to scheduling and general inquiries only.
