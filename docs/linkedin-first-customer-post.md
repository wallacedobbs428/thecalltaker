## CONTEXT BEFORE YOU POST

- Publish within 2 hours of the client signing — while the emotion is real
- Do not overthink this. The rawness is what makes it land.
- Pick one variant. Do not mix them. Each has a different angle.
- Character counts are listed. LinkedIn cuts captions at 210 characters before "see more" — the hook must land in the first 2 lines.
- Add a real photo if you have one: you at your desk, your laptop screen, anything that shows the real environment. Authenticity outperforms polished.

## HASHTAG GUIDANCE

All three variants include a hashtag block. Notes:
- `#buildinpublic` is the highest-engagement community for this type of post — keep it in all variants
- `#Nashville` tags you into a local business community that occasionally surfaces posts
- `#teenager` is a calculated choice — it invites attention and filters for the right audience. Use it.
- Do not add more than 7-8 hashtags. LinkedIn does not reward hashtag volume the way Instagram does.

## TIMING

Post between 7am and 9am on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday. These are peak LinkedIn engagement windows. If the client signs on a Friday night, draft the post immediately, schedule it for Monday morning.

## FOLLOW-UP COMMENT

After 30 minutes, add a comment on your own post with one specific line:
"The demo line is live right now if you want to hear it: (615) 784-5747. It handles any industry."

This drives action without cluttering the post body.
