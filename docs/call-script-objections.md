# Objection Handling — 3 Tiers Per Objection

> Short = quick redirect (5 sec). Medium = with proof (15 sec). Long = full story (30 sec).
> Use whichever fits the conversation. If they push back twice, escalate to the next tier.

---

## 1. "Will she understand accents or noisy calls?"

**Short:** "She handles job-site background noise and heavy accents all day — trained on thousands of real service calls."

**Medium:** "She's trained on thousands of real service calls — not just clean studio audio. We're talking table saws running, trucks on the highway, thick southern accents. She pulls the details out every time. If she can't understand something, she asks them to repeat it — just like a real person would."

**Long:** "So our HVAC clients in Nashville get calls from crews on rooftops with wind blowing, homeowners calling from their car on the highway, and people with every accent you can imagine. Gideon handles all of it. She's not like Siri — she's trained specifically on service business calls. And if she genuinely can't catch something, she says 'sorry, can you spell that for me?' — which is exactly what your best front desk person would do. Try her right now — call from a noisy room and see for yourself."

## 2. "What if she books the wrong jobs?"

**Short:** "You set the rules — service area, job types, hours. She only books what you tell her to."

**Medium:** "You set every rule — what jobs to take, your service area, your hours, even minimum job sizes. She follows them exactly, every time. No judgment calls, no mistakes. And you get a notification for every booking so you can approve or adjust before anyone shows up."

**Long:** "Here's how it works: during setup — which takes like 2 minutes — you tell Gideon your service area, what jobs you take, your hours, and any special rules. She follows those rules to the letter. She won't book a commercial HVAC install if you only do residential. She won't schedule someone outside your zip codes. And every single booking sends you a notification with the details. If something looks off, you adjust it in one tap. After 14 days, she knows your business better than a new hire would after a month."

## 3. "Too busy to set up"

**Short:** "I do it for you — takes 2 minutes. You just forward your after-hours line."

**Medium:** "I completely set it up for you — you don't touch anything. All I need is your business hours and your calendar tool. Then you set one call forward on your phone: when you're busy or after hours, calls go to Gideon. That's it. Two minutes, and she's live tonight."

**Long:** "I hear that a lot, and honestly that's exactly why people need Gideon — you're too busy to even set her up because you're answering calls while running jobs. Here's what I'll do: I set everything up on my end. I'll call you for 2 minutes to confirm your hours and what jobs to book. Then you go to your phone settings, set a conditional forward — 'when busy or unanswered, forward to this number.' Done. She's live. Most of our clients do this while eating lunch. And if you want, I'll walk you through the forwarding part on a 2-minute call tomorrow morning before your first job."

## 4. "Is it cheaper than my answering service?"

**Short:** "Flat $497, unlimited calls. Most answering services hit $300+ at 100 calls and still sound like a call center."

**Medium:** "Most answering services charge $1.50–$2 per call plus a base fee. At 100 calls a month you're already at $250–$300, and that's for someone reading from a script who puts your customer on hold. Gideon is a flat $497 for unlimited calls — no per-minute charges, no overage fees. And she actually books the jobs instead of just taking a message."

**Long:** "Let me break it down. A typical answering service charges $150–$200 base plus $1.50–$2.00 per call. At 100 calls a month — which most [vertical] companies hit easily — you're at $300–$400. At 200 calls you're at $500+. And for that money, you get someone who puts your customer on hold for 30 seconds, reads a script, and says 'we'll have someone call you back.' Gideon is $497 flat. Unlimited calls. She answers instantly — no hold time. She books the appointment right there on the call. She texts the customer a confirmation. And she never charges you extra because you had a busy week. One busy month with an answering service costs more than Gideon does. And Gideon actually converts the calls into jobs."

## 5. "My customers will hate talking to a robot"

**Short:** "You just heard her — did she sound like a robot to you? Most callers don't realize it's AI."

**Medium:** "I get that concern a lot — and then people hear her. She doesn't sound like Alexa or a phone tree. She sounds like a dispatcher who's been doing this for 10 years. She uses their name, asks follow-up questions, and handles the call in 45 seconds. Most callers have no idea. And the ones who do? They're just glad someone picked up at 2 AM instead of getting voicemail."

**Long:** "Totally fair — nobody wants their customers talking to a robot. But here's what actually happens: a customer calls at 9 PM with an emergency. Right now they get voicemail. They hang up and call your competitor. With Gideon, she picks up on the first ring, says 'Thanks for calling [Business Name], this is Gideon, how can I help?' She asks what's going on, gets their address, and books them for first thing tomorrow — or dispatches your on-call guy if it's an emergency. The customer hangs up relieved. They don't care if Gideon is AI or human — they care that someone answered and solved their problem. We've had customers leave 5-star reviews specifically saying 'someone picked up right away and got me scheduled.' That's Gideon."

## 6. "Does it integrate with what I use?"

**Short:** "Google Calendar, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber — if it has a calendar, she connects to it."

**Medium:** "She integrates with all the big ones — Google Calendar, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Calendly, and more. During setup I'll connect her to whatever you're using. If it's something custom, we can usually make it work with a calendar link. Takes about 30 seconds to connect."

**Long:** "She connects natively to Google Calendar, Outlook, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Calendly, and about 20 other platforms. During the 2-minute setup, I'll ask what you use and connect it right there. If you're running something less common, we can usually hook in through a calendar feed URL or a simple integration. And even if for some reason we can't connect directly, Gideon can still take all the details — name, number, address, job type — and text them to you instantly so you can book it yourself in 10 seconds. Nobody's ever been blocked by an integration issue."
