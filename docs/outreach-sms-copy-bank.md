# Outreach SMS Copy Bank

Status: draft copy only. No sending enabled.

These messages are intentionally less polished than email. They should sound like Wallace texting a business owner, while still keeping identity, truth, and opt-out safety intact.

## Rules

- identify Wallace or The Call Taker early
- keep one idea per text
- do not claim calls are live
- do not claim provider routing is active
- do not guarantee jobs
- include opt-out language where required
- pause on any reply

## Permission / Warm Lead

1. hey [First Name], Wallace with The Call Taker. saw your demo request - want me to send the preview for [Company]?

2. yep i can send it. just to be clear, its a preview - not live call routing yet

3. want the short version or should i call and walk you through it?

## After Website Demo

1. hey [First Name], Wallace here. looks like you checked out The Call Taker demo. want me to show what Gideon would say for [Company]?

2. quick note - nothing goes live from the demo. its just the caller experience preview

3. if missed calls arent an issue, all good. if they are, i can send the preview

## After Phone Conversation

1. good talking earlier. i wrote down the main issue as: [call path note]. that right?

2. next step would be a reviewed setup preview. no routing gets turned on from this

3. i can send the preview here if thats easiest

## Cold SMS Candidate, Hold Until Approval

1. hey [First Name], Wallace with The Call Taker. quick question - what happens when calls hit after hours at [Company]? reply stop and i wont text again

2. not trying to spam you. i help businesses catch missed calls and prep cleaner handoffs. worth sending the preview?

3. last text from me - if missed calls arent a problem, no worries. if they are, i can show the preview

## Industry Variants

HVAC:

> hey [First Name], quick question - when someone calls about AC after hours, does it hit a person or voicemail?

Plumbing:

> hey [First Name], Wallace here. what happens if somebody calls [Company] with a leak after hours?

Roofing:

> quick one - after storms, do all the roof calls get answered or do some end up waiting?

Water damage:

> if somebody calls about water damage at night, who catches it right now?

Medical/surgical supply:

> hey [First Name], Wallace with The Call Taker. do urgent supply calls go straight to a person, or is there a callback path?

## Do Not Use

Do not send messages like:

- The Call Taker answers every call for [Company] 24/7.
- Your AI receptionist is live.
- Call forwarding is active.
- We guarantee more booked jobs.
- Calling you now.
