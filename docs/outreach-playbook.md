## 4. Closing Script

When someone says "I'm interested" or "let's do it" — here's exactly what to say and do.

---

### Step 1 — Lock It In

> "awesome, let's get you set up. the pilot is 14 days completely free — no card, no contract. if you love it, it's $97/mo for after-hours or $297 for 24/7. sound good?"

Wait for confirmation. If they ask about pricing, explain all three tiers:
- $97/mo — after-hours only (evenings + weekends)
- $297/mo — full 24/7 coverage
- $497/mo — premium (24/7 + priority support + custom features)

---

### Step 2 — Collect Info

> "cool, i just need a few things to get your AI built. takes like 2 minutes."

Collect:
1. **Business name** (exactly as they want it answered)
2. **Owner name** (who gets the text notifications)
3. **Phone number** (their business line they want forwarded)
4. **Email** (for onboarding + account)
5. **Industry** (hvac, plumbing, dental, roofing, locksmith, etc.)
6. **Hours** (when do they want the AI answering — after hours only or 24/7?)
7. **What should the AI do?** (book appointments? get caller info? dispatch? transfer emergencies?)

---

### Step 3 — Set Expectations

> "here's what happens next — i'm going to build your AI receptionist tonight. it'll be trained on your business, your services, your hours. tomorrow you'll get a text from me with your forwarding number. you just set up call forwarding on your business line and you're live. whole thing takes like 5 minutes on your end."

Key points to mention:
- AI is customized to their business (not generic)
- They'll get text notifications for every call
- Setup takes them about 5 minutes (just call forwarding)
- They can listen to every call recording
- They can change anything anytime — just text or call

---

### Step 4 — Create Urgency (Without Being Pushy)

> "just so you know, i only run 3-5 pilots at a time because i personally set up each one. right now i have [X] spots. once those are filled, new people go on a waitlist. so the sooner we get your info, the sooner you stop missing calls."

---

### Step 5 — Confirm and Close

> "alright [NAME], you're locked in. i'll have everything built by tomorrow and you'll get a text from me with next steps. if you have any questions before then, just text me. sound good?"

After the call:
1. Tag them `pilot-signup` in legacy CRM immediately
2. The onboarding engine auto-fires (welcome email, welcome SMS, setup instructions)
3. Build their AI prompt that night
4. Text them the forwarding number next morning
5. Follow up Day 1 to make sure forwarding is working

---

### Transition Phrases (For Awkward Moments)

If they hesitate after you ask for info:
> "no pressure at all — it's free for 14 days. if you hate it, just turn off forwarding and you're done. i just need the basics to build it."

If they want to think about it:
> "totally, but just so you know — there's nothing to think about money-wise since it's free. the only question is do you want to stop missing calls starting tomorrow or keep waiting."

If they ask "what's the catch?":
> "honestly? the catch is i'm trying to prove this works so you become a paying customer after 14 days. that's it. if it doesn't work for you, you cancel and owe me nothing."

If they want to talk to someone else first:
> "yeah of course. want me to call you both tomorrow? or i can just set up the pilot and your partner can see it working in real time. sometimes that's easier than explaining it."
