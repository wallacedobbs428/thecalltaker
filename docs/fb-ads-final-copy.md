## Compliance Notes

**Words AVOIDED (Meta policy):**
- ~~AI~~ → "smart receptionist" or "virtual receptionist"
- ~~never miss a call~~ → "stop losing calls" or "catch more calls"
- ~~guaranteed~~ → removed
- ~~automated~~ → "done for you" or just described the feature
- No specific income claims ("$10K/month") → reframed as "thousands" or "jobs"
- No "you" accusations ("Are you failing?") → reframed as situation descriptions

**Images guidance:**
- Avoid before/after comparisons
- No fake screenshots or fabricated reviews
- Stock photos of phones, business owners, service trucks are safe
- Avoid text-heavy images (Meta penalizes >20% text in ad images)
