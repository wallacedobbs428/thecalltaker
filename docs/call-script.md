# The Call Taker — Live Call Script

**Use this on every sales call. Memorize the flow, not the words.**

---

## OPENER (30 seconds)

> "Hey [Name], this is Wallace from The Call Taker. Thanks for hopping on — I know you're busy so I'll keep this tight. You said you were interested in [what they mentioned / how they found us]. What's going on with your calls right now?"

**Goal:** Get them talking about their pain. Shut up and listen.

---

## DISCOVERY (2-3 minutes)

Ask until you find the pain:

- "How many calls you think you're missing a week?"
- "What happens when someone calls after hours right now?"
- "You got someone answering the phone full-time, or is it hit or miss?"
- "What's a typical job worth for you?"
- "How'd you hear about us?"

**Listen for:**
- Number of missed calls
- Dollar value of lost jobs
- Current solution (voicemail, answering service, nothing)
- Frustration level

---

## PAIN ANCHOR (30 seconds)

Use their own numbers against them:

> "So you're missing [X] calls a week, and a typical job is [$X]. That's [$X × 4] a month walking out the door to whoever picks up first."

Or if they don't know:

> "Most [industry] owners we talk to are losing $2,000-$5,000 a month in jobs that called and got voicemail. The caller doesn't leave a message — they just call the next guy."

---

## DEMO (2 minutes)

> "Let me show you what Jessica sounds like. I'm going to call our demo line right now on speaker — just listen."

1. Call (615) 784-5747 on speaker
2. Let Jessica answer and handle a sample scenario
3. After 30-60 seconds: "That's what your customers would hear. Every call, 24/7."

Or if they've already called the demo line:

> "You already talked to her — you heard it. That's exactly what your customers get."

---

## PITCH (60 seconds)

> "Here's how it works. Jessica answers your calls in under 2 seconds. She sounds like a real dispatcher — takes their info, books the job on your calendar, and texts you the summary. Nights, weekends, holidays — she never misses."
>
> "Setup takes 15 minutes. You forward your line, share your calendar, and she's live. We walk you through the whole thing."

---

## TRIAL FRAME (use during the call, before pricing)

> "Test Jessica free for 14 days. If she doesn't catch at least 2 jobs you were missing, it costs you nothing."

---

## PRICING (30 seconds)

> "Two plans. Starter is $497 a month — one line, 24/7, appointment booking, the whole thing. Pro is $997 — same thing but up to 3 lines, emergency escalation, and priority support."
>
> "Both come with a 14-day free trial. No setup fee for founding customers. Cancel anytime."

**If they hesitate on price:**

> "One extra booked job a month more than pays for it. You told me a typical job is [$X] — Jessica has to catch one call you would've missed and she's already paid for herself."

---

## THE CLOSE

### They say yes:

> "Perfect. Texting you the payment link now — PayPal or card both work, takes 45 seconds."

1. Open iMessage
2. Type `pps` (Starter $497) or `ppp` (Pro $997)
3. Send to their number
4. Stay on the line

> "Let me know when it goes through."

*Wait for confirmation.*

> "Great — let's get Jessica forwarding live right now. What's your main business number?"

Then:
- Walk them through call forwarding setup
- Send welcome email (use template from welcome-email-templates.md)
- Set 48-hour check-in reminder

### They say "let me think about it":

> "Totally get it. Here's what I'd say — the trial is free for 14 days. You're not committing to anything tonight except letting Jessica answer a few calls. If she catches even one job you would've missed, you'll know. If not, cancel and it costs you zero."

If still hesitant:

> "Tell you what — let's just set it up tonight so Jessica can start catching calls. You've got 14 days to decide. Worst case, you cancel and you got 2 weeks of free coverage."

### They say "too expensive":

> "I hear you. Let me ask — what's a typical job worth to you? [$X]. So if Jessica catches just one extra call a month that you would've missed, she's already more than paid for herself. Most of our owners are catching 5-10 extra jobs a month."

### They say "I need to talk to my partner":

> "Makes sense. Want me to text you the demo line number so they can call and hear Jessica themselves? Usually once the partner hears her handle a call, they get it."

Send demo line number and follow up in 24 hours.
