## 48-Hour Review Rules

Do not make structural edits in the first 48 hours unless an ad is obviously broken.

Review:
- hook clarity
- CTA alignment
- click-through rate
- landing page continuity
- obvious junk-lead signals

## Kill Rules

Do not kill before at least one of these is true:
- `3,000 impressions`
- `$60 spend`
- clear creative or landing break

### Green

- link CTR above `1.5%`
- landing-page-to-lead rate above `8%`
- CPL below `$35`

### Yellow

- CTR `1.0%` to `1.5%`
- lead rate `5%` to `8%`
- CPL `$35` to `$50`

### Red

- CTR below `1.0%`
- lead rate below `5%`
- CPL above `$50`

If the `CALL_NOW` ladder yields fewer leads but clearly stronger buyers, it stays alive.
