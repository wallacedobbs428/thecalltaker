## Section 3 — AU Cold Email Compliance (Spam Act 2003)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Legal to cold B2B email AU businesses without prior consent? | **YES** — Spam Act 2003 Section 16 permits unsolicited commercial email to businesses where there is a *reasonable inference* of business interest |
| Required elements in every email | Sender identity, physical/mailing address, functional unsubscribe mechanism |
| Unsubscribe must be honoured within | **5 business days** — non-compliance triggers ACMA enforcement |
| Penalties for violations | Up to AU$2.2M per day for corporations (ACMA enforcement) |
| Instantly.ai (US infrastructure) legal for AU targeting? | **YES** — the Spam Act governs the *sender's* obligations regardless of infrastructure location. US sending tools are fully viable. |
| GDPR equivalent for AU? | Australian Privacy Act 1988 — applies to businesses with AU$3M+ revenue. Under that threshold, most small dental/legal/property targets are exempt. |

**Actionable setup:** Add to every cold email footer: `The Call Taker | [Nashville address] | Unsubscribe: [link]`. That's full compliance. Instantly.ai handles unsubscribe links automatically.

### Exploitation Angles

- AU B2B cold email is **more permissive than UK GDPR**. Run AU sequences at the same 50/day cadence as US — full legal clearance.
- Subject line that works for AU professional services: **"Quick question about your after-hours calls"** — curiosity + relevance, no spam triggers.
