## What To Look For

### Hook Strength

- stops the scroll in first `1-3 seconds`
- obvious CTA
- no confusion about who this is for

### Landing Continuity

- ad promise matches page promise
- CTA text matches landing intention
- no sudden context shift

### Quality

- serious business owners
- right business types
- not junk info
- not price-only tire kickers

## Decision Rules

### Keep

- clear hook strength
- quality is good
- page match is good

### Fix

- hook is good but page is wrong
- clicks happen but intent dies on page
- lead quality is mismatched but not junk

### Replace

- hook is weak
- CTA is weak
- bad response after enough spend

## Threshold Rules

Do not make hard calls until one of these is true:
- `$60` spend
- `3,000` impressions
- obvious creative failure

## First Replacement Wave

If one call ad loses:
- replace with `m1-owner-night-01` only if quality was good but hook was weaker

If one signup ad loses:
- replace with `m1-emergency-01` if stronger urgency is needed

## End Of Review Output

At the end of each review, mark each ad as:
- `control`
- `keep testing`
- `fix landing`
- `replace`
