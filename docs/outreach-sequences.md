# The Call Taker Outreach Sequences

## Purpose

Provide safe, implementation-ready outreach drafts for manual review. These are not sending instructions. Do not send email, SMS, DMs, calls, or webhooks from this doc.

Core positioning:

> The Call Taker helps service businesses recover missed-call revenue by capturing missed or after-hours callers, qualifying the request, preparing a clean handoff, and keeping setup review honest before any live activation.

Approved CTAs:

- "Want me to send the preview?"
- "Want to hear what Gideon would say?"
- "Worth a quick call with Wallace?"

Avoid:

- "Your AI is live."
- "We answer every call now."
- "Guaranteed booked jobs."
- "Provider routing is active."
- "Backend sync is automatic."

## Objection Handling

"We answer our own calls."

> That is good. The question is where the gaps are: after hours, lunch, job sites, weekends, or when multiple calls come in. If there are no gaps, you may not need this.

"We have voicemail."

> Voicemail is better than nothing, but urgent callers may not wait. The goal is to give them a clear next step and give you a better handoff.

"We already have an answering service."

> That may be enough. The question is whether it captures the right details, handles urgency the way you want, and gives your team a clean handoff. We can compare the process without assuming you need to switch.

"I do not trust AI."

> Fair. That is why the first step is only a preview. You hear it, judge the experience, and nothing goes live without review.

"Can it dispatch?"

> Not automatically from this outreach path. We can design escalation preferences, but provider routing and live activation require reviewed setup and verification.

"Send info."

> I can. I will send the preview and a short summary of what it does and does not do. If it looks relevant, we can talk through your call path.

## Secret-Shopper Audit Process

Purpose:

Find call-path friction without deception, harassment, or creepy framing.

What to check:

- Public phone number accessibility.
- Hours and after-hours instructions.
- Ring/no answer/voicemail/answering service/AI result.
- Whether emergency options are clear.
- Whether the website makes phone contact easy.
- Whether the call path creates confidence for an urgent caller.

What not to do:

- Do not impersonate a real customer with a fake emergency.
- Do not book appointments.
- Do not provide false personal details.
- Do not record calls unless lawful, approved, and necessary.
- Do not repeatedly call and disrupt the business.
- Do not call sensitive businesses where this could interfere with care or emergency service.

Safe result codes:

- `answered-person`
- `voicemail`
- `no-answer`
- `answering-service`
- `ai-or-ivr`
- `unclear-emergency-path`
- `not-tested`

How to summarize findings:

> We checked the public call path at [time window] and saw [neutral result]. That may be fine if your team has another coverage path. If not, some urgent callers may not wait.

Avoid:

- "I caught you missing calls."
- "Your customers are leaving."
- "Your competitor is stealing your leads."
