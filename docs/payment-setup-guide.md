## HOW THE 14-DAY TRIAL WORKS

- Customer clicks the link and enters their card/PayPal
- PayPal shows them: "Your first payment of $X will be on [date 14 days from now]"
- No charge today. No charge for 14 days.
- On Day 15, PayPal auto-charges the monthly amount
- Customer can cancel anytime from their PayPal account
- You can also cancel their subscription from your PayPal dashboard

## MANAGING SUBSCRIPTIONS

### View active subscribers:
PayPal Dashboard → Pay & Get Paid → Subscriptions → **Subscribers** tab

### Cancel a subscription:
1. Find the subscriber
2. Click their name
3. Click **Cancel Subscription**
4. They'll get an email from PayPal confirming cancellation

### Handle failed payments:
- PayPal automatically retries failed payments 3 times over 15 days
- You'll get email notifications for each failure
- If all retries fail, the subscription is suspended
- Contact the customer directly — text them a new payment link
