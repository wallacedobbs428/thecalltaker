# Payment Setup Guide — PayPal Subscriptions

**Time to complete: 15 minutes**
**Goal: Collect $497 or $997 tonight on a live call**

---

## STEP 1: Create PayPal Business Account

1. Go to [paypal.com/business](https://www.paypal.com/business)
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Choose **Business Account**
4. Business type: **Service Provider**
5. Business name: **The Call Taker**
6. Use your email: `thecalltakerai@gmail.com`
7. Complete identity verification (ID + bank account)

> If you already have a personal PayPal, you can upgrade it to Business from Settings → Account Settings → Upgrade to Business Account.

---

## STEP 2: Create the Starter Plan ($497/mo)

1. Log into PayPal Business Dashboard
2. Go to **Pay & Get Paid** → **Subscriptions**
3. Click **Create Plan**
4. Fill in:
   - **Plan name:** `The Call Taker Starter - $497/mo`
   - **Description:** `24/7 AI Receptionist + Basic Booking`
   - **Price:** `$497.00`
   - **Billing cycle:** `Monthly`
   - **Trial period:** `14 days` at `$0.00`
5. Click **Create Plan**
6. Copy the **Subscribe Link** — save it somewhere safe

Your Starter link will look like:
```
https://www.paypal.com/webapps/billing/plans/subscribe?plan_id=P-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
```

## STEP 5: Set Up iPhone Text Shortcuts

This lets you type 3 letters and instantly paste the payment link during a live call.

1. Open **Settings** → **General** → **Keyboard** → **Text Replacement**
2. Tap the **+** button
3. Create shortcut #1:
   - **Phrase:** `[PASTE YOUR STARTER PAYPAL LINK]`
   - **Shortcut:** `pps`
4. Create shortcut #2:
   - **Phrase:** `[PASTE YOUR PRO PAYPAL LINK]`
   - **Shortcut:** `ppp`

Now when you're on a call and they say yes:
- Open iMessage
- Type `pps` → auto-expands to the full Starter payment link
- Hit send

## TROUBLESHOOTING

**They don't have PayPal:**
PayPal subscription links accept credit/debit cards too — they don't need a PayPal account. Tell them: "No PayPal needed, just tap the link and use your card."

**Link doesn't work:**
Double-check the plan is set to Active in PayPal Dashboard → Subscriptions → Your Plans.

**They want to pay later:**
Don't let them off the call without payment. Say: "The 14 days don't start until you activate — let's just get it set up now so Jessica can start catching calls tonight."

**Under 18 PayPal restriction:**
PayPal Business accounts require the account holder to be 18+. If this is a blocker, have a parent/guardian create the Business account, then add yourself as a user. The payment links will still work the same.
