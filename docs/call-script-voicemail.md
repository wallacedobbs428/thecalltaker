# Voicemail Scripts — FB Lead Follow-Up

> Under 20 seconds each. Warm, fast, specific. Always end with a clear next step.

---

## VM #1 — Day 0 (5 min after form submit)

> **Target: 18 seconds**

"Hey [First Name], Wallace with The Call Taker — you just checked out our AI receptionist Jessica on Facebook. She answers your [vertical] calls 24/7 and books jobs automatically. I'm going to text you a link to hear her live — takes 45 seconds. Talk soon."

**→ Immediately send SMS #1 from fb-lead-ads-engine after leaving VM**

---

## VM #2 — Day 1 follow-up

> **Target: 16 seconds**

"[First Name], Wallace again. Quick one — how many [vertical] calls did you miss last night? Jessica would've caught every one of them and booked the jobs. Call me back or text DEMO and I'll send you a live sample. Talk soon."

---

## VM #3 — Day 3 final attempt

> **Target: 15 seconds**

"Hey [First Name], last call from me. If you ever want to hear Jessica answer your phones live, the demo line is (615) 784-5747 — call anytime, 24/7. Free 14-day trial if you like what you hear. Take care."

---

## Delivery Notes

- **Tone:** Conversational, not salesy. Talk like you're leaving a message for someone you've met.
- **Speed:** Slightly faster than normal conversation — you want energy, not rush.
- **Smile:** Literally smile while recording. It changes your voice.
- **No "um" or "uh":** Practice each VM 3x before going live.
- **Caller ID:** Make sure your outbound shows your real number so they can call back.
- **Pair with SMS:** Every voicemail should be paired with an SMS sent within 60 seconds. The VM gets their attention, the SMS gives them a tap-to-act link.
