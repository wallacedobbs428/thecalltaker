## Contacts

Everything related to managing contacts (leads, clients, people in your system).

---

### How to Create a Contact Manually

1. Log into [app.gohighlevel.com](https://app.gohighlevel.com)
2. Click **"Contacts"** in the left sidebar
3. Click the **"+ Add Contact"** button (top right area of the contacts page — it's usually a green or blue button)
4. A form will appear. Fill in:
   - **First Name** — required
   - **Last Name** — optional but recommended
   - **Email** — fill in if you have it
   - **Phone** — fill in if you have it (use format: +1XXXXXXXXXX or just the 10 digits)
   - **Tags** — click the tags field and type any tags you want (e.g., `hvac`, `demo-request`, `cold-lead`). Press Enter after each tag.
   - **Source** — optional. You can note where this lead came from (e.g., "manual add", "referral", "cold outreach")
5. Click **"Save"** or **"Create Contact"** at the bottom of the form
6. The contact is now in your system

---

### How to Search/Find a Contact

1. Click **"Contacts"** in the left sidebar
2. At the top of the contacts list, there's a **search bar**
3. Type any of the following to find someone:
   - Their **name** (first or last)
   - Their **email address**
   - Their **phone number**
4. Press **Enter** or wait for results to appear
5. Click on the contact's name to open their full profile

**If you can't find them by search:**
1. Click **"Filters"** (funnel icon near the search bar)
2. Add a filter:
   - **"Tags" → "contains" →** type the tag you think they have
   - Or **"Date Created" → "is in the last 7 days"** to see recent contacts
3. Click **"Apply Filter"**

---

### How to Add/Remove Tags on a Contact

**Adding a tag:**
1. Go to **Contacts** → find and click the contact's name
2. On their profile page, scroll down until you see the **"Tags"** section
3. Click in the **tags area** or click **"+ Add Tag"**
4. Type the tag name (e.g., `active-client`, `hvac`, `demo-request`)
5. If the tag already exists, it will appear as a suggestion — click it
6. If it's new, type the full name and press **Enter**
7. The tag is added. It may auto-save, or you may need to click **"Save"**

**Removing a tag:**
1. Go to the contact's profile (same as above)
2. Scroll to the **Tags** section
3. Find the tag you want to remove
4. Click the **X** next to the tag name
5. The tag is removed. Save if prompted.

---

### How to View a Contact's Activity History

1. Go to **Contacts** → find and click the contact's name
2. On their profile page, look for a tab or section called **"Activity"**, **"Timeline"**, or **"History"**
3. Click on it (it might be a tab at the top of the profile, or a section you scroll to)
4. You'll see a chronological list of everything that's happened with this contact:
   - Calls (inbound and outbound)
   - Emails sent and received
   - SMS messages
   - Form submissions
   - Tag changes
   - Workflow triggers
   - Notes added
5. Scroll through to see the full history
6. Each entry usually has a **timestamp** and **description** of what happened

---

### How to Merge Duplicate Contacts

When the same person appears as two different contacts (e.g., one from a call and one from a form submission).

1. Go to **Contacts** → search for the person's name
2. If you see two results for the same person, **open one of them** (click their name)
3. On the contact profile page, look for a **"Merge"** option:
   - It might be under a **three-dot menu** (top right of the profile)
   - Or it might be a button labeled **"Merge Contact"** or **"Find Duplicates"**
4. Click **"Merge"**
5. GHL will ask you to **search for the duplicate contact** — type their name or phone number
6. Select the duplicate from the results
7. GHL will show you a **comparison of both contacts** — which fields to keep from each
8. For each field (name, email, phone, etc.), choose which version to keep (or keep both if applicable)
9. Click **"Merge"** or **"Confirm"**
10. The two contacts become one, with the combined information and history

**Important:** You cannot un-merge. Make sure it's actually the same person before merging.

---

### How to Delete a Contact

**Warning: This is permanent. You almost never want to do this.** If a client cancels, just add a `churned` tag — don't delete them.

1. Go to **Contacts** → find and click the contact's name
2. Look for a **three-dot menu** (usually top right of the contact profile)
3. Click it
4. Click **"Delete"** or **"Delete Contact"**
5. A confirmation popup will appear: "Are you sure you want to delete this contact?"
6. Click **"Yes, Delete"** or **"Confirm"**
7. The contact and all their history is permanently removed

## Conversations

How to communicate with contacts via SMS, email, and more.

---

### How to Send an SMS to a Contact

1. Click **"Conversations"** in the left sidebar
2. At the top of the conversation list, look for the **search bar** or **"New Message"** button
3. **Option A — From Conversations:**
   - Click **"New Message"** or the **compose icon** (pencil/plus icon)
   - Type the contact's name or phone number
   - Select them from the dropdown
   - The conversation opens
4. **Option B — From a Contact Profile:**
   - Go to **Contacts** → click the person's name
   - Look for a **"Send SMS"** button, a **text message icon**, or a **"Conversations"** tab
   - Click it to open the messaging window
5. At the bottom of the conversation, you'll see a **text input box**
6. Make sure the channel is set to **"SMS"** (there may be a dropdown or tabs showing SMS, Email, etc.)
7. Type your message
8. Click the **"Send"** button (or press Enter)

---

### How to Send an Email to a Contact

1. Go to the contact's conversation (see SMS steps above to get there)
2. In the conversation window, look for a **channel selector** — it might be a dropdown, tabs, or icons at the top of the message area
3. Switch from SMS to **"Email"**
4. New fields will appear:
   - **Subject** — type your email subject line
   - **Body** — type your email content (you can format text with bold, links, etc. using the toolbar)
5. Optionally attach files by clicking the **paperclip icon**
6. Click **"Send"**

---

### How to View All Conversations

1. Click **"Conversations"** in the left sidebar
2. You'll see a list of all conversations on the left panel
3. By default, it shows recent conversations at the top
4. Click any conversation to see the full message thread on the right
5. Scroll down in the conversation list to see older conversations
6. Use the **search bar** at the top to find a specific conversation by name, number, or keyword

---

### How to Filter Conversations by Status

1. Click **"Conversations"** in the left sidebar
2. Look at the top of the conversation list for **filter options** or **tabs**
3. You might see tabs like:
   - **"All"** — everything
   - **"Unread"** — conversations you haven't read yet
   - **"Starred"** — conversations you've marked as important
   - **"Recents"** — most recent activity
4. Click the tab/filter you want
5. If there's no tab, look for a **funnel icon** (filter icon):
   - Click it
   - Choose your filter criteria (e.g., "Unread only", "Starred", "Assigned to me")
   - Click **"Apply"**

**To star/mark a conversation as important:**
1. Open the conversation
2. Look for a **star icon** near the contact's name at the top
3. Click it — it turns yellow/filled to indicate it's starred

## Phone Numbers

Buying, assigning, and managing phone numbers.

---

### How to Buy a Phone Number

1. Click the **gear icon** at the bottom of the left sidebar to open **Settings**
2. In the Settings left panel, click **"Phone Numbers"**
3. Click the **"Buy Number"** or **"+ Add Number"** button (usually top right)
4. A search window will appear
5. Enter an **area code** (e.g., 615 for Nashville) in the search field
6. Click **"Search"**
7. A list of available numbers with that area code will appear
8. Browse the list and pick one you like
9. Click **"Buy"** or **"Select"** next to the number
10. Confirm the purchase (there may be a monthly fee shown — usually $1-2/month)
11. Click **"Confirm"** or **"Buy Number"**
12. The number is now in your account and ready to be assigned

---

### How to Assign a Phone Number to an AI Agent

1. **Settings** (gear icon) → **"Phone Numbers"**
2. Find the number you want to assign in the list
3. Click on the number to open its settings
4. Look for a field like **"Forward calls to"**, **"Assign to"**, or **"Connected Agent"**
5. Click the dropdown
6. Select the AI agent you want this number to connect to
7. Click **"Save"**

**Alternative method (from the AI agent side):**
1. **Settings** → **"Conversation AI"**
2. Click the agent you want to assign a number to
3. Look for a **"Phone Number"** field or section in the agent settings
4. Click the dropdown and select the number
5. Click **"Save"**

---

### How to Release/Remove a Phone Number

Only do this if you're absolutely sure you don't need the number anymore. Once released, you can't get the exact same number back.

1. **Settings** (gear icon) → **"Phone Numbers"**
2. Find the number you want to remove
3. Click on the number
4. Look for a **"Release"**, **"Remove"**, or **"Delete"** option (might be at the bottom of the settings page, or under a three-dot menu)
5. Click it
6. Confirm when prompted — "Are you sure? This cannot be undone."
7. The number is released back to the carrier

## Voice AI (Conversation AI)

Managing your AI receptionist agents.

---

### How to Find the AI Agents List

1. Click the **gear icon** at the bottom of the left sidebar (Settings)
2. In the Settings left panel, look for **"Conversation AI"**
3. It might be under a section labeled **"AI"** or **"Phone"** — scroll down if you don't see it right away
4. Click **"Conversation AI"**
5. You'll see a list/table of all your AI agents with their names, statuses, and assigned numbers

---

### How to Create a New Agent

1. Go to **Settings** → **"Conversation AI"**
2. Click **"+ Create Agent"** or **"+ New Agent"** button (usually top right)
3. Fill in the configuration:
   - **Name** — Give it a name you'll recognize (e.g., "Johnson Plumbing Receptionist")
   - **Instructions/Prompt** — This is the big text box where you write the script. Include:
     - Business name
     - Greeting (how to answer)
     - Business hours
     - Services offered
     - How to handle appointments
     - What to do when unsure (e.g., "Take a message and say someone will call back")
   - **Voice** — Choose a voice from the dropdown
   - **Phone Number** — Assign a phone number (dropdown of available numbers)
4. Click **"Save"** or **"Create"**
5. The agent is created but may be in **Draft/Inactive** state
6. To turn it on: Find the **Activate toggle** and switch it on
7. Click **"Save"** again if needed

---

### How to Edit an Existing Agent's Script

1. **Settings** → **"Conversation AI"**
2. Find the agent in the list
3. **Click on the agent's name**
4. The agent configuration page opens
5. Find the **"Instructions"**, **"Prompt"**, or **"Script"** section — this is the large text box
6. Click inside the text box
7. Make your changes (update hours, add services, change the greeting, fix errors)
8. Click **"Save"** (usually top right or bottom of the page)
9. Changes take effect immediately — test by calling the number

---

### How to Change Agent Voice/Settings

1. **Settings** → **"Conversation AI"** → click the agent name
2. Look for the **"Voice"** section or tab
3. **To change the voice:** Click the voice dropdown and select a different voice. Some agents offer voice preview — click the play button to hear a sample.
4. **To adjust speed:** Look for a speed slider or dropdown. Move it left for slower, right for faster.
5. **To adjust other settings:** Look for additional options like "interruption sensitivity", "response delay", "end call silence", etc. Adjust as needed.
6. Click **"Save"**
7. Test by calling the number

---

### How to Activate/Deactivate an Agent

1. **Settings** → **"Conversation AI"**
2. Find the agent in the list
3. **To activate:**
   - Click the agent name to open it
   - Find the **Activate/Status toggle** (top right area, or at the top of the settings)
   - Switch it to **ON** / **Active**
   - Click **"Save"**
4. **To deactivate:**
   - Same steps, but switch the toggle to **OFF** / **Inactive**
   - Click **"Save"**
   - The agent will immediately stop answering calls

**Quick check:** Back on the agents list, the status should show "Active" (green) or "Inactive" (gray) next to the agent name.

---

### How to Assign a Phone Number to an Agent

1. **Settings** → **"Conversation AI"** → click the agent name
2. Look for a **"Phone Number"** field, dropdown, or section
3. Click the dropdown
4. You'll see a list of available phone numbers in your account
5. Select the number you want this agent to answer
6. Click **"Save"**
7. Calls to that number will now be handled by this agent

**If the number doesn't appear in the dropdown:** The number might already be assigned to a different agent or user. Go to Settings → Phone Numbers → find the number → check/change its current assignment first.

## Pipelines

Tracking deals, leads, and client stages.

---

### How to View Your Pipeline

1. Click **"Opportunities"** in the left sidebar (it might have a dollar sign icon or funnel icon)
2. If you have multiple pipelines, there's a **dropdown at the top** of the page — click it and select the pipeline you want to view
3. You'll see a **Kanban board** (columns layout):
   - Each column is a **stage** (e.g., "New Lead", "Demo Booked", "Trial", "Active Client", "Churned")
   - Each card in a column is a **contact/deal**
4. Scroll left/right to see all stages
5. The number at the top of each column shows how many cards are in that stage

---

### How to Move a Card Between Stages

**Method 1: Drag and Drop**
1. Go to **Opportunities** and view your pipeline
2. Find the card you want to move
3. Click and **hold** on the card
4. **Drag** it to the column (stage) you want to move it to
5. **Release** (drop) it in the new column
6. The card is now in the new stage

**Method 2: Manual Update (if drag doesn't work)**
1. **Click** on the card (don't drag, just click)
2. A detail panel opens on the right side
3. Find the **"Stage"** field — it's a dropdown
4. Click the dropdown
5. Select the new stage
6. The card automatically moves
7. Close the panel

---

### How to Create a New Opportunity

1. Go to **Opportunities** in the left sidebar
2. Click **"+ Add"** or **"+ New Opportunity"** (usually a button at the top right, or a "+" at the top of a specific column)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Contact** — search for and select the contact this opportunity is for
   - **Pipeline** — select the correct pipeline (if not already selected)
   - **Stage** — choose which stage to put them in (usually "New Lead" for someone just coming in)
   - **Name/Title** — give the opportunity a name (e.g., "Johnson Plumbing - Starter Plan")
   - **Value** — enter the dollar amount (e.g., 497 for a Starter plan)
4. Click **"Save"** or **"Create"**
5. The card will appear in the selected stage column

---

### How to Add Notes to an Opportunity

1. Go to **Opportunities** → find the card → click on it
2. The detail panel opens on the right
3. Look for a **"Notes"** section or tab (scroll down in the panel if needed)
4. Click **"Add Note"** or click in the notes text area
5. Type your note (e.g., "Called 3/1 — interested but wants to think about it. Follow up Thursday.")
6. Click **"Save Note"** or press Enter
7. The note is saved with a timestamp

**Notes are internal only.** The contact will never see them. Use them freely to track conversations, follow-ups, and context.

## Automations / Workflows

Managing the automated processes that send notifications, tag contacts, etc.

---

### How to View Workflows

1. Click **"Automations"** in the left sidebar (might also be labeled **"Workflows"**)
2. You'll see a list of all workflows with:
   - **Name** — what the workflow is called
   - **Status** — Active/Published (green) or Draft/Inactive (gray)
   - **Last edited** — when it was last changed
3. Click on any workflow name to open the workflow builder and see what it does

---

### How to Activate/Deactivate a Workflow

**To activate:**
1. Go to **Automations** in the left sidebar
2. Find the workflow you want to turn on
3. Click on the workflow name to open it
4. At the top right of the workflow builder, look for a **toggle switch** or **"Publish"** button
5. Click it to turn the workflow **ON** / switch to **"Published"**
6. If prompted "Are you sure?", click **"Yes"** or **"Confirm"**
7. The workflow is now active and will run when triggered

**To deactivate:**
1. Same steps — open the workflow
2. Click the toggle to switch it **OFF** / back to **"Draft"**
3. Confirm if prompted
4. The workflow will stop running. No new triggers will fire. (Contacts already in the workflow may still complete their current step.)

**Quick toggle from the list (if available):**
Some versions of GHL show a toggle switch right in the workflow list without needing to open it. If you see a toggle next to the workflow name, you can click it directly.

---

### How to See Workflow Execution History

1. Go to **Automations** → click on the workflow name
2. Look for a tab called **"History"**, **"Execution Log"**, or **"Enrolled"** (usually at the top of the workflow page, next to the builder tab)
3. Click it
4. You'll see a list of every contact who entered this workflow:
   - Their name
   - When they entered
   - What step they're on (or if they completed it)
   - Whether it succeeded or failed
5. Click on any entry to see the details — which steps ran, which actions were taken, any errors

**This is useful for debugging:** If a notification isn't being sent, check the history to see if the workflow is even firing, and if so, where it's failing.

---

### A Note on Creating Workflows

Building new workflows from scratch is more advanced and won't be covered in detail here. Workflows involve triggers, actions, conditions, and timing — and it's easy to mess up.

**If you need a new workflow:**
- Describe what you want it to do (e.g., "When a new contact is created with tag X, send them an SMS and notify me")
- Ask for help building it or find a tutorial specific to that workflow type
- Don't experiment on live workflows — duplicate an existing one first (look for a "Clone" or "Duplicate" option in the three-dot menu next to the workflow name)

---

3. It's usually under a section called **"Business Profile"** or **"Phone"** or **"Channels"**

## Keyboard Shortcuts (If You Want to Be Faster)

GHL doesn't have many keyboard shortcuts, but these browser shortcuts help:

| Shortcut | What It Does |
|----------|-------------|
| Cmd+R (Mac) / F5 (PC) | Refresh the page |
| Cmd+F (Mac) / Ctrl+F (PC) | Search on the current page (useful for finding text in long agent scripts) |

| Cmd+T (Mac) / Ctrl+T (PC) | Open a new browser tab |
