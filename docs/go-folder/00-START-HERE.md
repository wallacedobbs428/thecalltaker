# A CLIENT JUST PAID -- DO THIS NOW

Total time: ~1-2 hours from payment to live.

---

## FIRST 5 MINUTES

- [ ] **Step 1: Add them to GHL** -- Create contact, add tags "payment-confirmed" + their industry + their plan
  - [How to do this](01-add-client-to-ghl.md)

- [ ] **Step 2: Send the intake form** -- Text them the form link so they fill out their business info
  - [How to do this](02-send-intake-form.md)

## ONCE THEY SUBMIT THE INTAKE FORM (usually 10-30 min)

- [ ] **Step 3: Set up their AI voice agent** -- Create their custom receptionist in GHL
  - [How to do this](03-setup-voice-agent.md) + grab their [industry script](voice-scripts/)

- [ ] **Step 4: Buy + assign their phone number** -- Get them a local number
  - [How to do this](04-buy-phone-number.md)

## TESTING (15-20 min)

- [ ] **Step 5: Run 3 test calls** -- Standard, emergency, after-hours
  - [How to do this](06-test-calls.md)

- [ ] **Step 6: Walk client through call forwarding** -- They forward their business number to the AI
  - [How to do this](05-setup-forwarding.md)

## GO LIVE (5 min)

- [ ] **Step 7: Tag them "live" + confirm** -- They're officially on
  - [How to do this](07-go-live.md)

---

## DONE -- THEY'RE LIVE!

Auto-messages fire (welcome, check-ins scheduled). If automations aren't built yet, send messages manually from: [message-templates/](message-templates/)
