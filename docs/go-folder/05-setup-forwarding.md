# Step 5: Set Up Call Forwarding

This isn't something you do in legacy CRM. This is something the CLIENT does on their phone or through their phone provider. Your job is to send them the right instructions.

---

## First: Ask the client what phone carrier they use

Send them this text from legacy CRM Conversations (or from your personal phone):

```
Quick question -- what phone carrier or phone system do you use for your business line? (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Google Voice, RingCentral, landline, etc.)
```

Once they answer, send them the matching instructions below.

---

## Forwarding instructions by carrier

Replace `[AI NUMBER]` with the actual AI phone number you bought in Step 4 (e.g., (615) 555-1234). Format it as just digits when dialing: 6155551234.

### AT&T (cell phone)

**Forward ALL calls (24/7 AI answering):**
- Pick up the phone and dial: `*21*[AI NUMBER]#`
- Press **Call**
- Wait for a confirmation tone or message
- Done -- all calls now go to the AI

**Forward only when BUSY or NO ANSWER (after-hours only):**
- Dial: `*61*[AI NUMBER]#`
- Press **Call**
- Wait for confirmation

**To turn off forwarding:**
- Dial: `#21#`
- Press **Call**

---

### Verizon (cell phone)

**Forward ALL calls (24/7 AI answering):**
- Dial: `*72` followed immediately by the AI number (e.g., `*726155551234`)
- Press **Call**
- Wait for a confirmation beep or tone
- Hang up -- forwarding is active

**Forward only when NO ANSWER (after-hours only):**
- Dial: `*71` followed by the AI number
- Press **Call**

**To turn off forwarding:**
- Dial: `*73`
- Press **Call**

---

### T-Mobile (cell phone)

**Forward ALL calls (24/7 AI answering):**
- Dial: `**21*[AI NUMBER]#`
- Press **Call**

**Forward only when NO ANSWER (after-hours only):**
- Dial: `**61*[AI NUMBER]#`
- Press **Call**

**To turn off forwarding:**
- Dial: `##21#`
- Press **Call**

### Google Voice

1. Open the **Google Voice** app on your phone, or go to **voice.google.com** in a browser
2. Tap or click the **Settings gear icon** (top-right on the website, or in the menu on the app)
3. Tap or click the **"Calls"** tab
4. Under **"Call forwarding"**, tap **"Add a forwarding number"** or **"Add linked number"**
5. Enter the AI phone number: `[AI NUMBER]`
6. Google will send a verification code to that number -- you'll need to retrieve it from the legacy CRM call log or have someone answer. (If this is tricky, call Google Voice support or just forward at the carrier level instead.)
7. Once verified, make sure the toggle next to the AI number is **ON**

---

### RingCentral

1. Log in to the **RingCentral admin portal** (service.ringcentral.com)
2. In the left menu, click **"Phone System"**
3. Click **"Auto-Receptionist"** or **"Call Handling & Forwarding"**
4. Look for **"After Hours"** rules or **"Custom Answering Rules"**
5. Click **"Add Rule"** or edit the existing after-hours rule
6. Under **"Forward calls to"**, select **"External number"**
7. Enter the AI phone number: `[AI NUMBER]`
8. Click **"Save"**

---

### Vonage

1. Log in to the **Vonage dashboard** (my.vonage.com)
2. In the left menu, click **"Extensions"**
3. Find and click on the business extension
4. Click **"Call Forwarding"** or **"Routing"**
5. Click **"Add forwarding destination"**
6. Enter the AI phone number
7. Set when to forward (always, or after a certain number of rings)
8. Click **"Save"**

---

### Grasshopper

1. Log in to **Grasshopper** (app.grasshopper.com)
2. Click **"Extensions"** in the menu
3. Select the extension to edit
4. Under **"Call Forwarding"**, click **"Add Number"**
5. Enter the AI phone number
6. Set it as the primary forwarding number (or the only one)
7. Click **"Save"**

---

### Landlines (AT&T, Spectrum, Comcast, CenturyLink, etc.)

Landline forwarding usually can't be set up with a dial code. The client needs to:

1. **Call their phone company's customer service line:**
   - AT&T: 1-800-288-2020
   - Spectrum: 1-833-267-6094
   - Comcast/Xfinity: 1-800-934-6489
   - CenturyLink/Lumen: 1-800-201-4099
2. Say: "I need to set up call forwarding on my business line"
3. Give them: the business phone number and the AI number to forward to
4. They'll set it up remotely -- usually takes 5-10 minutes on the phone
5. They might charge a small monthly fee ($5-10/mo) for the feature

## Copy-paste text to send the client

Once you know their carrier, send them this message. Replace `[AI NUMBER]` with their actual AI number and `[FIRST NAME]` with their name. Delete carrier sections that don't apply.

```
To activate your AI receptionist, forward your business phone to your new AI number: [AI NUMBER]

Here's how (takes 2 minutes):

AT&T: Dial *21*[AI NUMBER]# and press Call
Verizon: Dial *72 then [AI NUMBER] and press Call
T-Mobile: Dial **21*[AI NUMBER]# and press Call

For after-hours only forwarding:
AT&T: Dial *61*[AI NUMBER]# and press Call
Verizon: Dial *71 then [AI NUMBER]
T-Mobile: Dial **61*[AI NUMBER]# and press Call

You'll hear a confirmation tone when it's active. Don't worry -- you'll still see all your calls, they're just being answered by your AI now instead of going to voicemail.

To turn it off anytime:
AT&T: Dial #21#
Verizon: Dial *73
T-Mobile: Dial ##21#

Text me when you've done it and I'll verify it's working!
```

## After they confirm forwarding is active

1. Call their **business number** (NOT the AI number) from your phone
2. The AI should answer with their business name greeting
3. If it works: you're good -- move on to Go Live (Step 7)
4. If it doesn't work:
   - Ask them to re-do the forwarding code -- they may have dialed it wrong
   - Make sure they dialed the full 10-digit AI number after the code
   - If they're on a landline, it might take 30 minutes to propagate
   - Try calling again in 5 minutes

---

## Common mistakes

**Client dials the forwarding code wrong.**
The most common issue. Have them try again, slowly. Make sure they're including the full phone number (area code + number, no dashes, no spaces).

**Client forwards to the wrong number.**
If they typed the AI number wrong, calls go to some random person. Verify: have them read back the number they forwarded to.

**Landline client expects instant setup.**
Landline forwarding through the phone company can take up to 30 minutes. Set expectations: "It might take a few minutes to activate."

**Forgetting to test after forwarding.**
Always call their business number yourself to verify. Don't just take their word for it.
