## Part 1: Create the Listing

### Step 1 -- Go to Google Business Profile

1. Open Chrome (use the Google account you want to own this listing forever).
2. Go to **https://business.google.com**
3. Click **"Manage now"** (blue button).

### Step 2 -- Enter Your Business Name

1. A box will say "Business name."
2. Type: **The Call Taker**
3. If a dropdown appears and your business is NOT listed, click **"Add your business to Google"** or **"Create a business with this name."**
4. Click **Next.**

### Step 3 -- Pick Your Business Category

1. A box will say "Business category."
2. Type: **Answering service**
3. Select **"Answering service"** from the dropdown. This is the best fit. Google does not have an "AI receptionist" category.
4. Click **Next.**

> If "Answering service" does not appear, type **"Telephone answering service"** instead. Either one works.

### Step 4 -- Do You Want to Add a Location?

1. Google will ask: "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"
2. Select **"No"** -- you do not have a storefront.
3. Click **Next.**

### Step 5 -- Set Your Service Area

1. Google will ask where you serve customers.
2. In the search box, type: **United States**
3. Select **United States** from the dropdown.
4. Click **Next.**

> You serve HVAC companies everywhere, not just Brentwood. Set the service area to the entire US.

### Step 6 -- Add Phone Number and Website

1. **Phone number:** (615) 784-5747
2. **Website:** https://thecalltaker.com
3. Click **Next.**

### Step 7 -- Finish and Verify

1. Google will show a summary. Confirm everything looks right.
2. Click **Finish** or **Continue.**
3. Google will ask you to verify your business (more on this below).

## Part 2: Verify Your Business

Google needs to confirm you are real. For a service-area business with no storefront, you will likely get one of these options:

### Option A -- Phone Verification (fastest)

1. Google calls or texts **(615) 784-5747.**
2. You receive a code.
3. Type the code into Google.
4. Done.

### Option B -- Email Verification

1. Google sends a code to the email on your Google account.
2. Open the email, copy the code.
3. Paste it into Google.
4. Done.

### Option C -- Postcard Verification (slowest)

1. Google mails a postcard to your address with a code. Takes 5-14 days.
2. When it arrives, go to **https://business.google.com**
3. Click **"Verify now"** and enter the code.

### Option D -- Video Verification

1. Google asks you to record a short video showing your business.
2. Film yourself at your desk, show your phone with The Call Taker number, show your website on screen.
3. Upload it.
4. Google reviews it in 1-5 business days.

> Do whichever option Google gives you. You do not get to pick. Phone is the fastest.

## Part 3: Fill Out Your Profile Completely

Once verified (or even before), go to **https://business.google.com** and click on your business. Fill in everything below.

### Step 1 -- Business Description

1. Click **"Edit profile"** (or the pencil icon near your business name).
2. Find the **"Description"** field.
3. Paste this exact text:

> The Call Taker is an AI-powered receptionist built for HVAC companies. We answer every call 24/7 -- nights, weekends, and holidays -- so you never miss a lead again. Our AI handles appointment booking, dispatches service calls, captures customer information, and answers common questions, all without putting a single caller on hold. Built by HVAC people for HVAC people. Your phones are covered. Your calendar stays full. You sleep through the night.

4. Click **Save.**

### Step 2 -- Business Hours

1. In your profile editor, find **"Hours."**
2. For every day of the week (Monday through Sunday), set:
   - **Opens at:** 12:00 AM
   - **Closes at:** 12:00 AM
3. This tells Google you are open 24 hours, 7 days a week.
4. If Google gives you a **"Open 24 hours"** toggle for each day, just turn that on for all 7 days.
5. Click **Save.**

### Step 3 -- Add Additional Categories

1. In the profile editor, find **"Business category."**
2. Your primary category should already be **Answering service.**
3. Click **"Add another category."**
4. Type and add these one at a time:
   - **Business management consultant**
   - **Software company**
5. Click **Save.**

### Step 4 -- Add Services

1. Find the **"Services"** section in your profile editor.
2. Click **"Add a service."**
3. Add these services (type each one and save):
   - AI Call Answering
   - 24/7 Phone Answering for HVAC Companies
   - Appointment Booking
   - After-Hours Call Handling
   - Lead Capture
   - Customer Intake
4. Click **Save.**

### Step 5 -- Add the Website and Appointment Links

1. Find **"Website"** -- confirm it says: **https://thecalltaker.com**
2. If there is a field for **"Appointment link"** or **"Booking URL"**, enter: **https://thecalltaker.com/demo**
3. Click **Save.**

## Part 4: Add Photos

Google ranks businesses with photos higher. You need at least 5 photos to start.

| Photo | What to Do | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Logo** | Upload The Call Taker logo. Use a square version (500x500 or larger). | Shows up in search results. |
| **Cover photo** | Create a simple banner in Canva: "The Call Taker - AI Receptionist for HVAC Companies" with your logo. Make it 1024x575 pixels. | First thing people see. |
| **Dashboard screenshot** | Screenshot your GHL call dashboard showing real call activity (blur out customer info). | Proves the product is real. |
| **Call flow screenshot** | Screenshot or mockup showing how a call flows: Phone rings > AI answers > Appointment booked > Tech notified. | Shows the value in one image. |
| **Website screenshot** | Full-page screenshot of https://thecalltaker.com. | Gives legitimacy. |

### How to Upload Photos

1. Go to **https://business.google.com**
2. Click on your business.
3. Click **"Add photo"** (or find the Photos section).
4. Click **"Logo"** and upload your logo.
5. Click **"Cover photo"** and upload your banner.
6. Click **"Photo"** to upload the remaining images.

> Canva is free at https://canva.com. Use it for the banner. Takes 10 minutes.

## Part 5: Get Your First Reviews

Reviews are the single most important factor for ranking on Google. You need 5+ reviews in the first month.

### Step 1 -- Get Your Review Link

1. Go to **https://business.google.com**
2. Click on your business.
3. Look for **"Ask for reviews"** or **"Get more reviews"** -- click it.
4. Google will give you a short link. Copy it.
5. Save this link somewhere. You will use it constantly.

### Step 2 -- Ask These People First

| Who | How to Ask |
|-----|-----------|
| Any HVAC company using The Call Taker | Text: "Hey [name], would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Here is the link: [paste link]. Means a lot." |
| Friends or family who have seen the product | Same text as above. |
| Business contacts in Brentwood, TN | Same text as above. |

### Step 3 -- Build Reviews Into Your Process

After every new HVAC client has been using The Call Taker for 2 weeks, send this text:

> "Hey [name], you have been using The Call Taker for a couple weeks now. How is it going? If you are happy with it, would you mind leaving a quick Google review? Takes 30 seconds. Here is the link: [paste link]. Appreciate you."

### Step 4 -- Respond to Every Review

1. When a review comes in, go to **https://business.google.com**
2. Click **"Reviews"** in the menu.
3. Click **"Reply"** under each review.
4. Keep it short: "Thanks [name], glad The Call Taker is working for your team. We are here if you need anything."
