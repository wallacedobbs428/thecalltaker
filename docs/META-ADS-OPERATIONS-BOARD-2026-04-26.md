## Hard Rules

- Do not add a third campaign.
- Do not add more than 2 ads per ad set during phase 1.
- Do not swap thumbnails and hooks simultaneously.
- Do not point any cold traffic into `book.html`.
- Do not edit budgets inside the first 48 hours unless delivery is broken.
- Do not pause a control just because a challenger has better vanity metrics.

## Success Standard

At the first real review, we should know:
- best cold call-intent hook
- best cold signup hook
- whether homepage or `try-live.html` is stronger for call-path traffic
- whether `after-hours` or `starter` is the cleaner first conversion path
