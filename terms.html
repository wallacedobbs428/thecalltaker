1. Service Description

The Call Taker provides an AI-powered virtual receptionist for HVAC companies. Here's what that means in practice:

  • We set up an AI phone agent that answers calls to your business 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
  • The AI receptionist greets callers, captures their information (name, phone, address, issue description), assesses urgency, and assures them someone will follow up.
  • After each call, you receive a summary with the caller's details so you can follow up and book the job.
  • We handle the entire setup — you provide your business details and we configure everything.

The Call Taker is a call-answering tool, not a replacement for your technicians. The AI does not diagnose HVAC issues, quote prices, or make scheduling commitments on your behalf.
