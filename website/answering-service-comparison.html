Compare total cost, not a headline price.

Ask for the included calls or minutes, overage rate, setup fee, transfer charges, cancellation terms and billing frequency. Compare the same coverage window and call volume. The Call Taker pricing above describes coverage tiers; it does not imply unlimited calls or identical scope across plans.

For named alternatives, verify the current offers directly: Smith.ai AI receptionist pricing and Ruby plans and pricing. Their products, included usage and commercial terms differ. No savings claim is made from unlike plans.

Five questions to ask any provider

  1. What happens when a caller asks for a person?
  2. What information is required before the call ends?
  3. Who receives urgent requests, and what is the fallback if they cannot respond?
  4. Does the system capture a request or make a confirmed commitment?
  5. Can I test my actual greeting, questions and handoff before forwarding production calls?

Start with your coverage gap.

If calls are missed mostly when the office is closed, review after-hours answering. For daytime overflow, compare broader coverage plans. Use the missed-call worksheet to test your own assumptions.
