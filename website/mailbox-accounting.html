The Call Taker

Mailbox Accounting

The Call Taker Mailbox Accounting is an internal tool that checks outgoing activity in Google mailboxes authorized by their owner. It helps reconcile sending records and apply daily sending limits.

The tool requests read-only Gmail access. That permission lets it inspect mailbox history, sent-message identifiers, labels, dates, and selected headers; it does not permit sending or deleting mail. Account owners can revoke access through their Google Account.

For how this data is used, stored, and deleted, see our Privacy Policy. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to thecalltakerservice@gmail.com.
