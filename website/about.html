How launch works

  1. Choose coverage and complete the required checkout and setup steps.
  2. Confirm your hours, service area, intake questions, urgent-call rules and notification destination with the team.
  3. Review the call flow and forwarding instructions.
  4. Test the agreed routing and summary delivery before production calls go live.

Checkout alone does not activate service. Launch timing depends on completing review and testing; there is no universal instant-activation promise.

Clear limits

Gideon captures appointment requests unless confirmed scheduling is specifically included in an approved setup. It does not independently guarantee availability, dispatch, final prices or a response time. Professional decisions and follow-up remain with your team.
