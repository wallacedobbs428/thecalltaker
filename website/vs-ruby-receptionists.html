How much does The Call Taker cost compared to Ruby Receptionists?

No. Ruby takes messages and relays them to you through their app or email. You then have to call the customer back to schedule the appointment. The Call Taker books appointments directly into your calendar while the customer is still on the phone.

The Call Taker answers every call in under 2 seconds. There is no hold time, no queue, and no routing delay. Ruby routes calls to available human receptionists, which typically takes 10 to 20 seconds. During busy periods, wait times can be longer.
