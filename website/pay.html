Questions?

How fast can I get set up?
Most businesses are live within 24 hours. Premium plan gets same-day setup. We handle everything. Just give us your business name, hours, and services.
Do I need to change my phone number?
Nope. We set up call forwarding on your existing number. Your customers call the same number they always have. They just get an answer every time now.
What happens during the free pilot?
We set up the AI receptionist on your business line for 14 days. It answers calls, books appointments, and texts customers. You see exactly how many calls you were missing. No card required to start.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Month-to-month, cancel anytime with one click. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no hassle.
