Opening Script
“Hey [name], appreciate you hopping on. Before I tell you anything — quick question: what happens right now when someone calls your shop after 5pm?”
Pause after asking Let them talk Take notes

90% will say "voicemail" or "it rings out." That's your opening.

3 Discovery Questions

Ask every single prospect

1
“How many calls do you think you miss after hours in a typical week?”
Gets them to admit the problem. Write down their number — you'll use it in ROI math.
2
“What's your average job worth when someone calls?”
If they don't know, use the industry averages below.
3
“When those after-hours callers hit voicemail — do they leave a message, or do they just call the next company?”
KILL SHOT. They already know the answer. Drop the stat: "85% never call back."

Industry Average Job Values

HVAC: $350 Plumbing: $300 Roofing: $800 Electrical: $350 Dental: $500 Med Spa: $600 Legal: $1,500 Property Mgmt: $400

When someone calls you after hours — nights, weekends, holidays, lunch breaks — instead of voicemail, they get a live, professional receptionist who knows your services, your pricing, and books appointments on the spot.

40% of calls to businesses like yours come after hours. And here's the kicker — 85% of people who hit voicemail never call back. They just call the next company on Google. We make sure that doesn't happen.”

Alternate Close — Urgency

“Every night you don't have this, calls are going to voicemail and those customers are calling your competitors. You said you're missing [X] calls a week — that's [X] potential jobs this week alone. Want to stop the bleeding?”

Alternate Close — Risk Reversal

“Tell you what — try it for 30 days. If we don't catch at least one call that would've gone to voicemail, I'll refund you personally. No questions. Fair enough?”
Every Objection + Exact Response
▶ "That's too expensive"
"$497 is less than one missed job. You told me your average call is worth $[Y]. We pay for ourselves with a single call. What costs more — $497, or losing $[X×Y×4] a month to voicemail?"
▶ "I need to think about it"
"Totally get it. But while you're thinking, your competitors are answering their phones tonight. How about this — start with after-hours at $497. No contract, cancel anytime. If you don't love it after a week, you're out. But I bet you will."
▶ "How do I know it works?"
"Call our demo line right now — (629) 269-9697. That's exactly what your callers will hear. Try to stump it. I'll wait."
▶ "I already use voicemail"
"85% of people who hit voicemail never call back. They just call the next company on Google. Your voicemail isn't losing messages — it's losing customers."
▶ "I don't get that many after-hours calls"
"Most owners are surprised when they see the real numbers. 40% of all calls to service businesses come after hours. Try us for a month — we'll show you exactly how many calls are coming in. If it's truly zero, cancel for free."
▶ "I have a receptionist already"
"That's great for business hours. But does your receptionist work at 9pm? On Saturdays? On Christmas? 40% of your calls come when she's not there. We cover every hour she doesn't."
▶ "I'll just hire someone / my wife answers"
"A full-time receptionist costs $3,000-$4,000 a month. Plus benefits. Plus they call in sick. Plus they can only answer one call at a time. We're $497, available 24/7, and can handle unlimited calls simultaneously. And no PTO."
▶ "What if customers don't like talking to AI?"
"Most callers can't even tell it's AI — it sounds like a real person. But here's the thing: the alternative isn't a human. The alternative is voicemail. And 85% of people hang up on voicemail. Would you rather they talk to our receptionist or talk to nobody?"
▶ "Can I do a trial first?"
"That's basically what this is. No contract, cancel anytime. You're never locked in. Start this week, and if you're not impressed by the number of calls we catch, cancel with one message. Fair?"
▶ "I need to talk to my partner/wife"
"100%. When do you think you'll talk to them? I can follow up tomorrow. In the meantime, have them call our demo line — (629) 269-9697. Once they hear it, the decision usually makes itself."
▶ "How is this different from an answering service?"
"Traditional answering services take a message and email it to you. By the time you see it, the customer already called your competitor. Our receptionist actually books the appointment on the spot, answers questions about your services, and gives pricing — like having your best employee on the phone 24/7."
▶ "What if something goes wrong?"
"You get notifications for every call. You see every booking. Nothing happens without you knowing. And if a caller has something unusual — like an emergency or a complex question — the AI takes their info and texts you immediately so you can call them back. You're always in control."
