Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI answering service?

An AI answering service uses artificial intelligence to answer phone calls for your business. Instead of voicemail or a human receptionist, an AI voice picks up the phone, greets callers with your business name, asks qualifying questions, and can book appointments or take messages. The best AI answering services sound natural and handle calls in seconds.

How much does an AI answering service cost?

Prices range from free to over $1,500 per month. The Call Taker starts at $97 per month with unlimited calls. Ruby starts at $235 per month for 50 calls. Smith.ai starts at $292.50 per month for 30 calls. Goodcall offers a free tier with limited features. Numa charges per location. Most services charge extra per call once you exceed your plan limit.

Do AI answering services book appointments?

Not all of them. The Call Taker books appointments directly into your calendar during the call, so the customer gets a confirmed time before hanging up. Smith.ai and Ruby take messages for you to call back. Goodcall handles basic scheduling for some integrations. Numa focuses on text-based communication.

Is an AI answering service better than voicemail?

Yes. 80% of callers hang up when they reach voicemail, especially for urgent service calls like a broken pipe or a lockout. An AI answering service picks up every call instantly, engages the caller, qualifies the lead, and can book the appointment on the spot. Voicemail just records a message that you may never hear in time.

Do small businesses really need an AI answering service?

If your business misses calls, you lose revenue. Small businesses miss 40 to 60 percent of calls during business hours because the owner or staff are on job sites. After hours, 100 percent of calls go unanswered. For service businesses where one missed call can cost $200 to $15,000 in lost work, the $97 per month pays for itself on the first captured call.
