The problem

After-hours calls disappear when nobody answers.

For service businesses, a call at night or on the weekend can be urgent. It may be a repair request, a quote request, a customer needing help, or a homeowner trying to solve a problem before morning.

Voicemail asks the caller to do the work. The Call Taker helps capture the call while the need is fresh, then gives your team a cleaner starting point for follow-up.
