How Many Calls Does Your Business Actually Miss?

By Wallace Dobbs · 2026-03-29 · The Call Taker

You probably think you're catching most of your calls. Every business owner does. The data says otherwise.

The average small business misses 6 to 10 calls per week. Not because they're ignoring the phone, but because they're on a job, at lunch, in a meeting, or asleep when the call comes in.

At an average job value of $300, that's $1,800 to $3,000 per week walking out the door. $7,800 to $13,000 per month. Most of it going straight to competitors who picked up faster.

Why You Don't Know You're Missing Them

85% of callers who hit voicemail don't leave a message. They hang up and call the next business. You never see the missed call notification because it just looks like a random number that didn't leave a voicemail.

Your phone records might show "3 missed calls today." The real number is closer to 8, because many callers don't even let it ring long enough to register.

How to Find Out Your Real Number

Method 1: Call your own business after hours. Call at 7pm, 9pm, and 6am. What do your customers hear? If it's voicemail, every customer who calls at those times is gone.

Method 2: Check your phone carrier's call log. Look at calls under 10 seconds. Those are people who called, heard ringing or voicemail, and hung up. Count them. That's your real missed call number.
