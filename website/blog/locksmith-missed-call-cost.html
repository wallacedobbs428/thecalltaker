How Locksmiths Lose $4,500/Month from Missed Emergency Lockout Calls

Published February 24, 2026 • 6 min read

You are halfway through a deadbolt installation when your phone buzzes. Then it buzzes again. And again. By the time you finish the job, check your phone, and call those numbers back, two of the three have already booked another locksmith. That is the reality of the locksmith business: your customers are locked out, stranded, and desperate, and they will not wait.

For most locksmiths, missed calls are not just an inconvenience. They represent a massive, ongoing revenue leak that can cost $4,500 or more every single month.

60% of Locksmith Calls Are Emergencies

Unlike many service businesses, the locksmith industry is overwhelmingly driven by urgent, unplanned demand. Industry data shows that approximately 60% of all calls to locksmiths are emergency situations:

  • Residential lockouts: Homeowners locked out of their house, often late at night or in bad weather
  • Car lockouts: Drivers stranded in parking lots, gas stations, or roadside with keys locked inside
  • Commercial lockouts: Business owners or managers who cannot access their property
  • Break-in damage: People who have experienced a burglary and need immediate lock replacement or rekeying
  • Safe lockouts: Urgent need to access a locked safe for medications, documents, or valuables

60% of calls to locksmiths are emergency situations where the caller needs immediate help and will not leave a voicemail.

What makes these calls unique is their urgency. A person locked out of their car at 11 p.m. in a parking garage is not going to leave a message and wait for a callback. They need someone now.

The $225 Average Lockout Job

Emergency locksmith services command premium prices because customers need immediate help. The average lockout call is worth approximately $225, with pricing varying by type:

  • House lockout: $150 - $250
  • Car lockout: $75 - $200
  • Commercial lockout: $150 - $300
  • After-hours emergency surcharge: Additional $50 - $100
  • Lock replacement after break-in: $200 - $500

At $225 per call, every single missed emergency call is a significant loss. But the real damage is compounded by the fact that you are not just missing one call at a time.

The "On a Job" Problem

The fundamental challenge of running a locksmith business is that you are physically unable to answer the phone during your highest-revenue activity. When you are picking a lock, cutting a key, or installing a deadbolt, your hands are occupied, your focus is required, and your customer expects your full attention.

A typical locksmith job takes 20 to 45 minutes. During that time, incoming calls go unanswered. If you are handling four to six jobs per day, that is two to four hours daily where you are completely unreachable. During peak periods, especially evenings and weekends, you could easily miss five to eight calls while working on a single job.

2-4 hours per day the average locksmith is completely unreachable while actively working on jobs.

Customers Call Until Someone Answers

The competitive dynamics of the locksmith industry make missed calls even more costly. Here is what happens when a locked-out customer calls and you do not answer:

  1. They hang up immediately. They are not leaving a voicemail for a lockout situation.
  2. They Google "locksmith near me." Google shows them three to five options instantly.
  3. They call the next locksmith on the list. This takes about 15 seconds.
  4. They call the next one if that one does not answer. They will call three to four locksmiths in under two minutes.
  5. They book the first one who picks up. It does not matter if you are closer, cheaper, or better rated. The locksmith who answers gets the job.

In the locksmith industry, the phone is your storefront. The first locksmith to pick up wins the job nearly every time. There is no consideration period, no comparison shopping, no "let me think about it." The customer has an immediate need and will hire the first person who responds.

The Monthly Revenue Loss Calculation

Here is a conservative estimate of what missed calls cost the average locksmith:

  • Total calls per day: 12 - 18
  • Calls missed while on jobs (estimated 35%): 4 - 6 per day
  • Emergency calls within missed (60%): 2.5 - 3.6 per day
  • Monthly missed emergency calls: 75 - 108
  • Calls that would have converted (estimated 30%): 22 - 32 per month
  • Average revenue per emergency call: $225

$4,500 - $7,200 estimated monthly revenue lost by the average locksmith due to calls missed while working on other jobs.

For a solo locksmith earning $8,000 to $12,000 per month, losing $4,500 or more represents a 35-55% increase in potential revenue that is simply walking out the door. Over a year, that is $54,000 to $86,000 in lost income.

The Ripple Effect: Lost Repeat Business

Beyond the immediate lost job, every missed call represents a potential long-term customer you will never have. A residential lockout customer who is satisfied with your service is likely to call you again for:

  • Lock rekeying when they move into a new home ($150 - $300)
  • Smart lock installation ($200 - $400)
  • Home security upgrades ($300 - $800)
  • Referrals to neighbors, friends, and family (priceless)

A single lockout call that you miss could have been worth $1,000 or more in lifetime customer value.
