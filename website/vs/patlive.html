The verdict

PATLive has been around since 1990. They know answering services. But their model is the same model every answering service uses: charge per-minute and hope you go over.

75 minutes sounds like a lot until you do the math. If your average call is 3 minutes, that's 25 calls a month. One busy week and you're in overage territory at $2.25/minute. A plumber getting 10 calls a day will blow through that in 2 days.

PATLive's biggest weakness is peak-hour hold times. When their call center is busy, your customers wait. And waiting customers call your competitor instead.

The Call Taker answers every call in under 2 seconds. No hold times. No overages. No per-minute billing. $97/month flat. Your busiest week costs the same as your quietest week.
