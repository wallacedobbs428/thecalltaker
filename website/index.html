07 / FAQ

Questions we
hear every day.

Can't find your answer? Call us at (629) 269-9697 and ask Gideon yourself.

How does call forwarding work?

Your customers still call your regular number. We confirm the forwarding path, approved call rules, and handoff method during setup before Gideon is treated as live.

What does GIDEON actually sound like?

It sounds like a real person, not a robot. Call (629) 269-9697 right now and hear for yourself.

Can it handle appointment requests?

It captures their name, number, what they need, and the requested timing. Direct calendar booking is available only when it is included in the approved workflow scope.

What if the caller needs a real person?

Gideon follows your approved escalation rules. If someone needs your team, it captures the context and routes the next step according to the workflow you approved.

How fast can I get set up?

Setup depends on your call flow. Wallace reviews your services, hours, service area, escalation rules, and handoff path before Gideon goes live.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You start through secure checkout, Wallace reviews your setup before Gideon goes live, and you can cancel before renewal if it is not the right fit. No long-term contract.

How is this different from a regular answering service?

Traditional answering services often stop at taking a message. Gideon is configured around your approved business rules, captures the request, filters lower-value calls, and routes a useful handoff so your team knows what to do next.

Does it integrate with my existing tools?

Scoped integrations are available in Operational Infrastructure or custom plans. We review your calendar, CRM, and scheduling tools before promising any direct connection.

What about data privacy and security?

All calls are encrypted in transit. We never share your customer data with third parties. You own your data. We can provide a BAA for HIPAA-regulated businesses like dental and medical offices.

Can I use this for multiple locations?

Absolutely. Each location gets its own trained GIDEON with unique hours, services, and service area. Volume discounts available on the Pro plan for 3+ locations.
