07 / FAQ

Questions we
hear every day.

Can't find your answer? Call us at (629) 269-9697 and ask Gideon yourself, or use the contact options in the footer.

How does call forwarding work?

Your customers keep calling your regular number. After your setup questions are submitted, we guide the forwarding and test-call steps for your phone setup.

What does Gideon actually sound like?

Gideon is designed to sound natural while asking the questions you approve. Call (629) 269-9697 and judge the live demo yourself.

Can it handle appointment requests?

It captures their name, number, what they need, and the requested timing. Direct calendar booking is available only when it is included in the plan you approve.

What if the caller needs a real person?

Gideon follows your approved escalation rules. If someone needs your team, it collects the context and sends the next step to the right person.

How fast can I get set up?

After confirmed enrollment, the setup questions take about 60 seconds. We use those basics to configure your call flow, summary rules, urgent action, forwarding ability, and forwarding/testing instructions.

How does checkout work?

You choose the plan, enter a card through Square, and start a 14-day trial with $0 due today. Unless canceled before renewal, the selected plan renews monthly. Setup submission does not by itself verify billing or activate service.

How is this different from a regular answering service?

Traditional answering services often stop at taking a message. Gideon asks your approved questions, collects the details, and sends a useful summary so your team knows what to do next.

Can it connect with my existing tools?

Yes, on custom plans. We review your calendar, CRM, and scheduling tools before promising a direct connection so the setup stays reliable.

What about data privacy and security?

We keep caller details private, use approved tools, and review compliance needs before any regulated call setup goes live.

Can I use this for multiple locations?

Yes. Each location needs clear hours, service area, urgent-call rules, and the right person to notify before launch.
