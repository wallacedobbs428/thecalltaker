About The Call Taker

What is The Call Taker?

+

The Call Taker is AI call answering for service businesses. Gideon answers missed and after-hours calls, collects caller details, and sends your team a clear summary so you can follow up faster.

How does it work?

+

You forward your business line to us. When a call comes in, Gideon answers, asks the questions you approve, and collects the caller's name, number, reason for calling, and next step. After each call, you get a clear text summary.

Who is The Call Taker built for?

+

Any service business that answers phones. HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, roofers, dentists, law firms, locksmiths, towing companies, pest control, veterinarians, med spas, property managers, and more. If you lose money when you miss a call, this is for you.

What if I already have a receptionist?

+

Perfect. Use Gideon as backup. Set it to answer only when your receptionist is busy, on lunch, or after hours. The $97 After-Hours Capture plan works alongside existing staff so late and overflow callers still get answered.

Can it handle complex questions about my business?

+

Yes. During setup we add your services, pricing, service area, hours, and approved answers. If Gideon gets a question it should not answer, it takes the caller's info and lets you know so you can follow up personally.

What happens if a caller gets frustrated or wants a real person?

+

Gideon can follow your rules for frustrated callers or people who want a real person. It never argues with customers. If someone needs your team, it takes their info and makes sure you get notified right away so you can call back fast.

How quickly does Gideon answer calls?

+

Industries

Which industries does The Call Taker work with?

+

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, dental, legal, locksmith, towing, pest control, veterinary, med spa, property management, auto repair, water damage, landscaping, and more. If your business depends on phone calls, the setup questions give us the basics to configure the right call flow before launch.

How does it adapt to my specific industry?

+

We set Gideon up for your industry and your business. A locksmith setup asks whether the caller is locked out of a car or home. An HVAC setup asks about the unit and whether it is heating or cooling. A dental setup captures new patient appointment requests.

Can it handle emergency or urgent calls differently?

+

Absolutely. We set up priority rules during the internal build. Emergency calls like a burst pipe for a plumber or a lockout for a locksmith get flagged as urgent. Routine calls like scheduling a tune up get handled normally.
