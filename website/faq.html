About The Call Taker

What is The Call Taker?

+

The Call Taker is an AI receptionist that answers your business phone 24/7. It picks up every call, handles customer questions, captures appointment requests and texts you a summary. Think of it like having a full time receptionist who never calls in sick, never takes lunch, and works every holiday.

How does it work?

+

You forward your business line to us (takes about 30 seconds). When a call comes in, GIDEON answers using a voice that's custom trained on your business. It knows your services, your hours, your pricing, and how you like things handled. After each call, you get a text with the caller's name, what they needed, and the request status and next step.

Who is The Call Taker built for?

+

Any service business that answers phones. HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, roofers, dentists, law firms, locksmiths, towing companies, pest control, veterinarians, med spas, property managers, and more. If you lose money when you miss a call, this is for you.

What if I already have a receptionist?

+

Perfect. Use GIDEON as backup. Set it to answer only when your receptionist is busy, on lunch, or after hours. A lot of our customers use the $97 After Hours plan alongside their existing staff so they never miss a single call, even at 2am on a Saturday.

+

Yes. During setup we train GIDEON on your specific services, pricing, service area, hours, and anything else callers might ask. If it gets a question it can't answer, it takes the caller's info and lets you know so you can follow up personally.

+

It takes about 30 seconds. On most phones you just dial *21* followed by the number we give you, then press call. We walk you through it during setup. Works with any phone carrier, landline or cell.
