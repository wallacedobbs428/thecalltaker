The Problem

Andre Williams runs Rapid Key Locksmith in Nashville with one employee. Locksmith work is 60% emergencies — people locked out of their car at midnight, locked out of their house at 3 AM, broken key in a lock at 6 AM. The calls come at the worst times.

"I'd wake up to 4 or 5 missed calls from overnight. Each one of those is a $150 to $250 job. By the time I called back at 7 AM, every single person had already called someone else. You get locked out of your car at 2 AM, you're not waiting until morning."

The Locksmith Math

Average lockout = $175. Missing 5 overnight calls/week = $875/week = $3,500/month in lost revenue. The Call Taker costs $297/month. That's a 12x return just on the calls between midnight and 6 AM.

Why Locksmiths Need This More Than Anyone

Unlike HVAC or plumbing where a customer might wait until morning, locksmith customers need help RIGHT NOW. They're standing outside in the cold, they're stranded at a gas station, their kid is locked in the car. If you don't answer, they're calling the next locksmith in Google — within 30 seconds.

Andre's phone was going to voicemail with a message that said "Leave your name and number and I'll call you back." For a locksmith customer, that's the same as hanging up.
