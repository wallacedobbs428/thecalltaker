The Problem

Marcus Thompson runs Palmetto Comfort Services, a 4-truck HVAC operation in Charleston, South Carolina. Like most HVAC owners, his biggest frustration wasn't the work — it was the calls he couldn't get to.

"I'd look at my phone at 10 PM and see three missed calls. Two voicemails, one hang-up. That hang-up? That's somebody who called my competitor instead. In the summer, that's a $400 emergency AC repair I just lost."

Marcus estimated he was missing 12 to 15 calls per week outside business hours. His answering service cost $289/month and still sent most callers to voicemail because the operators couldn't answer HVAC-specific questions.

The Results — First 30 Days

Day 1
AI receptionist live. Customized with Palmetto Comfort's name, services, service area, and pricing. First after-hours call answered at 7:42 PM — emergency AC repair, customer booked for next morning.
