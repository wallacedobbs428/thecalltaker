The Problem

AllStar's dispatcher couldn't keep up during rush hour. Multiple breakdowns happening at once meant some callers waited on hold or got voicemail. Those drivers called another tow company.

The Solution

The Call Taker AI started answering overflow calls during peak times and all calls after hours. It captured the location, vehicle type, and destination, then texted the dispatcher for immediate truck assignment.

The Results

Answer rate went from 73% to 100%. Average response time dropped from 3 minutes to under 30 seconds. Monthly revenue increased $4,500 from previously lost calls.

“During a 10-car pileup on I-35, the AI handled 6 calls simultaneously while my dispatcher handled 2. We would have lost half those jobs before.”

— Owner, AllStar Towing
