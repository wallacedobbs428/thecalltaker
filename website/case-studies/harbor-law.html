The Problem

Personal injury cases are time-sensitive. When an accident victim calls after hours and gets voicemail, they call the next attorney on Google. Harbor Law was losing 5-10 potential clients per week.

The Solution

The Call Taker AI started handling all after-hours calls. It collected the caller's name, accident details, and contact information, then immediately texted the intake team.

The Results

In 2 weeks, the AI captured 8 potential cases that would have been lost to voicemail. Combined potential value: $28,000. The firm signed 5 of the 8.

“Accident victims don't leave voicemails. They call the next lawyer. Now we never miss those calls.”

— Managing Partner, Harbor Law Group
