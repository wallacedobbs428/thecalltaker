Limitations

Clear promises beat hype.

The Call Taker is for structured call capture and follow-up. It should not pretend to be a licensed professional, make medical/legal decisions, or promise service availability your business cannot honor. Escalation rules are part of setup.

FAQ

Questions service owners ask first.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers business calls, asks approved questions, captures caller details, and prepares a summary so your team can follow up with context.

Can it replace a human receptionist?

It can cover missed, after-hours, and overflow calls. A human is still better for complex judgment calls, sensitive conversations, and situations where your business wants a person to make decisions.

Does The Call Taker go live immediately?

No. Setup, call flow, forwarding, test calls, and escalation rules are reviewed before production call routing starts.
