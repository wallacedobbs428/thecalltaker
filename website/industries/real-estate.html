How Real Estate Agents Lose Leads Every Single Day

Buyers and sellers call when they're ready to move. If you don't pick up, they move on to the next agent. Here's what's happening right now.

Showing Calls While at Another Showing

You're walking a buyer through a $450,000 home when your phone buzzes. A hot seller lead from your yard sign is calling. You can't answer. They call the next agent on the sign. That listing -- and the $13,500 commission -- is gone.

Weekend Buyer Inquiries

Saturday afternoon, a couple sees your listing on Zillow and calls to schedule a showing. You're already at an open house and can't pick up. They schedule with another agent, fall in love with a different property, and you never hear from them.

Seller Leads from Signs and Ads

You're spending $500/month on yard signs and online ads. When a homeowner calls about selling, they expect someone to answer immediately. If they hit voicemail, they call the next agent. Your marketing dollars just went to waste.

Open House Follow-Ups

You hosted an open house, collected 15 sign-in sheets, and now you're buried in callbacks. While you're calling lead #3, leads #7 through #15 are calling you back -- and hitting voicemail. The hottest leads cool off fast.
