Water is flooding their basement. Every minute costs them hundreds in damage. They call you -- voicemail. They call the next plumber -- someone answers. That $650 emergency call with $200 in parts is gone. They're not calling you back in the morning.

Sewage Backup Emergency

Raw sewage backing up into a family's bathroom on a Saturday night. This is a $800-$1,200 emergency job with urgent dispatch needed. The homeowner needs someone NOW -- not a callback on Monday morning.

Water Heater Dies Friday Night

No hot water, family of five, weekend ahead. They call at 7 PM Friday and get your voicemail. By Saturday morning they've already hired someone else for the $1,200 water heater replacement. You never even knew they called.

Lunch Break Calls Pile Up

Your office manager takes lunch from 12-1 PM. That's when homeowners on their own lunch break are calling about clogged drains, running toilets, and slow leaks. Five missed calls per week during lunch = $2,250 in lost revenue every month.
