How Towing Companies Lose Jobs Every Day

You can't answer the phone when you're on a hook. But the stranded driver on the highway isn't going to wait.

Highway Breakdown at 11 PM -- $250+ Tow

A driver's car dies on the interstate at night. They're scared, stranded, and Googling tow companies. They call you first -- voicemail. They call the next company -- someone answers. That $250 tow is gone in 30 seconds. They're never calling you back.

Accident Scene -- Police Calling -- $350+ Job

Police dispatch calls your number for a wreck clearance. No answer? They move to the next company on the rotation list. That's a $350+ accident tow with potential storage fees you'll never see. One missed police call can cost you thousands in rotation revenue.

Dead Battery in a Parking Lot -- $150 Jumpstart

Someone's stuck in a grocery store parking lot with a dead battery. Quick $150 jumpstart job -- easy money. But they need help NOW, not a callback in 20 minutes. They call three companies and go with the first one that picks up.

Behind the Wheel When 3 Calls Come In

You're mid-tow on the highway when your phone rings three times in a row. You can't answer -- you're driving with a car on the hook. By the time you call back, all three have already booked with someone else. That's $750 in lost revenue in 10 minutes.
