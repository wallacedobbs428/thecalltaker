How Auto Repair Shops Lose Jobs Every Single Day

Your customers need their car fixed now. If you don't pick up, they call the next shop. Here's what's happening right now to your business.

Calls While Working Under the Hood

Your best mechanic has his hands deep in a transmission rebuild. The phone rings. And rings. A customer with a $650 brake job needs an appointment today. They call the shop down the street instead. You never even knew they tried.

Tow-In Requests After Hours

Someone's car breaks down at 8 PM on the highway. They need a shop that can take a tow-in first thing tomorrow. They call you, hit voicemail, and call the next shop that picks up. That's a $500-$1,200 repair you lost before you even opened the bay doors.

Estimate Follow-Ups Missed

You gave a customer an estimate last week and they're ready to schedule. They call during your busiest morning, can't get through, and decide to try that new shop their neighbor recommended. Your estimate work was for nothing.

Weekend Breakdown Calls

Saturday and Sunday -- cars break down but most shops are closed or short-staffed. A driver with a check engine light wants to book Monday morning. They call, hit voicemail, and Google "auto repair near me open now." Your Monday slot goes to someone else.
