How Roofing Companies Lose Their Best Leads

Storm season doesn't wait for business hours. Neither do your most profitable leads.

Storm Season Floods Your Phone Lines

Hail hits on a Tuesday afternoon and suddenly you're getting 40 calls in 3 hours. Your office manager can handle one call at a time. The other 39 homeowners are already dialing your competitor while they wait on hold.

Homeowners Call 3-4 Roofers After Hail

Insurance just told them they need a new roof. They Google "roofing company near me" and call the top 4 results. The first company to answer, sound professional, and book an inspection wins that $8,500 job. Every time.

Weekend Inspections Lost to Voicemail

Saturday morning is when homeowners finally look at their roof and see the damage. They call at 9 AM -- but your office doesn't open until Monday. By then, they've already booked with whoever answered on Saturday.

Emergency Leak Calls at Night

A family discovers water pouring through their ceiling at 10 PM. They need a tarp and emergency repair now. That call hits your voicemail, and they find a roofer who answers. You just lost a $2,500 emergency repair plus the full replacement job.
