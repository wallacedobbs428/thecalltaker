# Upsell Scripts for Existing Clients

**Purpose:** Pitch add-on services to clients who have been active for 30+ days and are seeing results.

**Golden Rule:** Only pitch when the client is happy and the add-on genuinely solves a problem their data reveals. Never pitch to a struggling or unhappy client -- fix their issues first.

## 1. Missed Call Text-Back ($49/mo)

### What It Does
When a call goes to voicemail or is missed for any reason, the system automatically sends the caller a text message within 60 seconds:

> "Hi, this is [Business Name]. We're sorry we missed your call! We'd love to help. You can reply to this text to schedule an appointment or let us know what you need, and we'll get right back to you."

Keeps the lead warm. Prevents them from calling the next company on Google.

- Client has **5+ missed calls or voicemails** in their monthly report
- Client mentions they're "too busy to call people back"

> Here's how it works: Any time a call is missed, the system instantly texts the caller back within 60 seconds with a friendly message from your business. No manual work on your end. The caller gets acknowledged immediately, and most of them reply or call back.
>
> It's $49/month, and based on your numbers, if it saves even ONE job per month, it pays for itself 8x over.

## Upsell Dos and Don'ts

### Do:
- Lead with data from their actual report
- Show the math (cost vs. return)
- Pitch only ONE add-on at a time
- Wait for client to be in a good place emotionally
- Make it easy ("I can have it live by tomorrow")
- Follow up once if they say "let me think about it"

### Don't:
- Pitch add-ons to unhappy clients
- Pitch more than one add-on in the same conversation
- Hard-sell or create false urgency
- Promise specific results ("you'll get 20 reviews")
- Pitch during the first 30 days (let them settle in first)
- Pitch during a support/troubleshooting conversation

---

## Follow-Up After a "Maybe" or "Let Me Think About It"

**Wait 5-7 days, then send:**

> "Hey [Client Name], just circling back on the [Add-On Name] we talked about. No pressure at all -- just wanted to see if you had any questions or if you'd like to move forward. Happy to chat through anything."

**If no response after follow-up, drop it.** Revisit at next monthly report if the data still supports it.
