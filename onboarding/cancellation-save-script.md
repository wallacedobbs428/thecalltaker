# Cancellation Save Script

**Purpose:** Retain clients who want to cancel their Call Taker subscription. Goal is to save 40%+ of cancellation requests.

**Key Rule:** Never make the client feel guilty or pressured. Be genuinely helpful. If they truly need to leave, make it graceful so they come back later or refer others.

---

## Step 1: Empathy Opening

**When the client says they want to cancel (email, call, or text):**

> "Hey [Client Name], I appreciate you reaching out and being upfront with me. Before I process anything, I'd love to take 5 minutes to chat. Not to pressure you at all -- I just want to make sure I understand what's going on and see if there's anything I can do. Is that fair?"

**If they agree (most will):**

> "Thank you. I genuinely appreciate your business, and whatever you decide, I respect it. Let me just ask a couple of questions."

**If they insist on immediate cancellation:**

> "Absolutely, I'll take care of it. Just so I can improve -- would you mind sharing the main reason? It'll take 30 seconds and it honestly helps me get better."

---

## Step 2: Ask Why

> "What's the main reason you're thinking about canceling?"

**Listen. Don't interrupt. Take notes. Then match their reason to the appropriate response below.**

---

## Step 3: Common Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "It's too expensive."

**Acknowledge:**
> "I totally understand. Every dollar matters when you're running a business, and I respect that you're watching your expenses."

**Reframe with ROI:**
> "Let me pull up your numbers real quick. Last month, your AI receptionist answered [X] calls and booked [X] appointments. Based on average job values in your industry, that's roughly $[X] in revenue that came through calls we answered.
>
> So you spent $497 (or $997) and captured an estimated $[X]. That's a [X]:1 return on your investment. Would it help if I walked you through those numbers?"

**If they still push back:**
> "What if we could find a plan that fits your budget better? I have a couple of options I can offer."

*(Go to Step 4 -- Alternatives)*

**Math to have ready (by industry):**
- **HVAC/Plumbing/Electrical:** Avg repair call = $350-$600 | Install lead = $5,000-$10,000
- **Roofing:** Avg repair = $500-$1,500 | Full roof = $8,000-$25,000
- **Dental:** New patient lifetime value = $3,000-$10,000
- **Med Spa:** Avg treatment package = $1,200-$5,000
- **Legal:** Avg case value = $3,000-$50,000+
- **Property Management:** Avg new tenant = $1,200-$2,400/yr in management fees
- Just ONE missed lead per month = cost of 1-2+ years of service
- After-hours/emergency call = $500-$1,000+

---

### Objection 2: "I'm not getting enough calls."

**Acknowledge:**
> "That's a fair concern. If the phone isn't ringing, it doesn't feel like it's worth it."

**Diagnose:**
> "Let me check a couple of things on my end. Sometimes it's a configuration issue, not a volume issue."

**Things to check:**
- [ ] Is the forwarding set up correctly? (Calls may be going to old number)
- [ ] Is the AI answering on the right number?
- [ ] Has the client been advertising the right phone number?
- [ ] Is it a seasonal lull? (Shoulder seasons are slower)
- [ ] How does their call volume compare to similar businesses in their industry and area?

**After checking:**
> "[Client Name], I found [the issue / here's what I see]. Let me fix that and give it another 30 days. If you still don't see value after that, I'll process the cancellation and refund you for that month. Fair?"

**If it's genuinely low volume (seasonal or cyclical):**
> "Here's the thing -- most service businesses have busy and slow seasons. Right now is [slow season]. But when [busy season] hits and that phone starts ringing at 6 AM on a Saturday, you're going to want this in place. What if we paused your account for [X] weeks and reactivated before the busy season?"

*(Go to Step 4 -- Alternatives)*

---

### Objection 3: "It doesn't sound good enough" / "Callers don't like it."

**Acknowledge:**
> "I appreciate you telling me that. The whole point is for your callers to have a great experience, so if it's not there yet, that's on me to fix."

**Dig deeper:**
> "Can you tell me specifically what's not working? Is it the greeting? The way it handles certain questions? The voice? I want to understand exactly what you're hearing."

**Common sub-issues and fixes:**
| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Greeting sounds robotic | Rewrite greeting, adjust tone settings |
| AI doesn't know their services | Update knowledge base with more detail |
| AI gives wrong info | Audit and correct training data |
| Callers complain | Pull specific call recordings, identify pattern |
| AI can't handle complex questions | Add custom responses, improve escalation |

**Offer:**
> "Here's what I'd like to do. Give me 48 hours. I'm going to personally retrain your AI based on what you just told me. I'll also pull your last 20 calls and audit them myself. Then I'll send you a test call so you can hear the difference. If you're still not happy after that, I'll cancel with no hard feelings. Deal?"

---

### Objection 4: "I found a cheaper competitor" / "I'm switching to [Competitor]."

**Stay calm and curious:**
> "No problem at all. Mind if I ask who you're looking at? I just want to make sure you're comparing apples to apples."

**Common competitors and differentiators:**

| What They Might Say | Our Advantage |
|---|---|
| "They're cheaper" | Do they specialize in your industry? Generic AI doesn't know the terminology, procedures, or urgency signals your callers use. |
| "They have more features" | Features don't matter if the AI can't book an appointment correctly. What specific features? |
| "My buddy recommended them" | Totally fair. But you've already got a system built, trained, and customized for YOUR business. Starting over means another ramp-up period. |
| "They include texting" | We offer Missed Call Text-Back for $49/mo -- and it's built specifically for your industry's workflows. |

**Response:**
> "I respect that you're shopping around -- that's smart business. Here's what I'd ask you to consider:
>
> 1. Your AI receptionist is already trained on YOUR business. Your services, your hours, your emergency protocol, your scheduling. That took time to build. A new provider means starting from scratch.
>
> 2. We specialize in service businesses -- HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, dental, med spa, legal, and property management. Every improvement we make is built for businesses like yours. Generic AI answering services are built for everyone, which means they're optimized for no one.
>
> 3. You have my direct number. When something needs to change, you text me and it's done. Will you get that from [Competitor]?
>
> What if I matched their price for the next 3 months so you can do a real comparison without any risk?"

*(Go to Step 4 -- Alternatives)*

---

### Objection 5: "I'm closing / selling the business."

**Response:**
> "Congratulations -- or condolences, depending on how you feel about it! Either way, I completely understand. Let me cancel that for you effective [date].
>
> Quick question: would the new owner want to keep the service? If so, I can transfer everything over seamlessly. It might even be a selling point -- 'AI receptionist already in place and trained.'"

---

### Objection 6: "I just don't need it. I answer my own phone."

**Response:**
> "I respect the hustle. Seriously. But let me ask you this -- when you're in the middle of a job at 2 PM and the phone rings, what happens to that call?
>
> Last month, [X] of your calls came in during business hours when you were likely on a job. [X] of those turned into booked appointments. That's an estimated $[X] you would have missed.
>
> But if you truly feel like you've got it covered, I understand. What if we paused for 60 days and you tried it without us? If you find yourself missing calls, you can reactivate with one text."

---

## Step 4: Offer Alternatives

**Present these in order. Stop as soon as one lands.**

### Alternative 1: Downgrade Plan

> "What if we dropped you down to our Starter plan at $497/month? You'd keep the core answering and booking, and if you want to upgrade again later, it's seamless."

*(Only if they're on Pro)*

### Alternative 2: Pause Account

> "What if we paused your account for 30 days? No charge during the pause. It gives you a breather, and when you're ready, we flip it back on -- no setup, no re-training, everything is exactly how you left it."

**Pause terms:**
- Maximum pause: 60 days
- No charge during pause
- All settings and training preserved
- Reactivation with one text/email
- One pause per 12-month period

### Alternative 3: Loyalty Discount

> "You've been a great client, and I'd hate to lose you over price. What if I knocked $50/month off your plan for the next 3 months? That gives us time to prove the value, and if you're still not feeling it after 3 months, no questions asked."

**Discount guidelines:**
- $50/mo off for up to 3 months
- Only offer after downgrade and pause are declined
- Requires Wallace's approval for anything beyond $50/mo

### Alternative 4: Custom Solution

> "Tell me what WOULD make this worth it for you. What would the service need to look like for you to stay? Let me see if I can make it work."

*(Listen and negotiate in good faith)*

---

## Step 5: Last Resort Offers

**Only use these if ALL alternatives above are declined.**

### Last Resort 1: Free Month

> "Let me do this -- I'll give you next month completely free. No charge. Use the full service for 30 more days, and if you still want to cancel at the end of it, I'll process it immediately with zero pushback. You've got nothing to lose."

### Last Resort 2: Money-Back on Current Month

> "Tell you what -- I'll refund this month's charge right now AND give you the rest of the month free. That way you're not paying for something you're not sure about, and you've got a few weeks to decide with zero financial pressure."

### Last Resort 3: Graceful Exit

**If nothing works, do NOT beg. Exit with class.**

> "[Client Name], I hear you, and I respect your decision. I'm going to process the cancellation right now. Here's what you should know:
>
> - Your service will remain active through the end of your current billing period on [date].
> - All your call recordings and reports will be available for 30 days after cancellation.
> - If you ever want to come back, everything I built for you is saved. One text to me and you're back up and running in 24 hours.
>
> I genuinely wish you the best, and I hope we get to work together again. My door is always open."

## Post-Cancellation Follow-Up

### If they cancel:

- **Day of cancellation:** Send a thank-you text. No selling.
  > "Thanks for giving The Call Taker a shot, [Name]. Wishing you a great rest of the season. You've got my number if you ever need anything."

- **30 days after cancellation:** Check-in email.
  > "Hey [Name], just wanted to check in and see how things are going. Hope business is booming. If you ever want to reactivate, just say the word -- everything is saved and ready to go."

- **Before next busy season:** Re-engagement email.
  > "Hey [Name], [summer/winter] is right around the corner. Last year you had [X] calls during this period. If you want to make sure every one of those gets answered this time, I can have you back up and running in 24 hours. Just reply to this email."
