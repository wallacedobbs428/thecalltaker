## 1. Services

Provider agrees to provide Client with AI-powered receptionist and call answering services for Client's HVAC business ("Services"), which include:

**a) 24/7 Call Answering.** Provider will answer inbound calls to Client's designated business phone number(s) using artificial intelligence technology, 24 hours per day, 7 days per week, 365 days per year.

**b) Customer Information Capture.** The AI receptionist will collect caller name, phone number, address, and a description of the service needed for each inbound call.

**c) Appointment Booking.** The AI receptionist will book appointments directly onto Client's designated calendar system based on Client's scheduling rules and availability.

**d) Text and Email Notifications.** Provider will send real-time text and/or email notifications to Client's designated recipients after each call, including a summary of the call and any actions taken.

**e) Emergency Dispatch.** For calls identified as emergencies per Client's defined emergency criteria, the AI receptionist will follow Client's designated emergency protocol, which may include notifying an on-call technician.

**f) Common Question Handling.** The AI receptionist will answer frequently asked questions about Client's business (hours, services, service area, pricing ranges, etc.) based on information provided by Client.

**g) Follow-Up Sequences.** Provider will configure automated appointment confirmation and reminder messages sent to customers via text message.

**a) Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues on a **month-to-month basis** until terminated by either Party.

**b) Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing **thirty (30) days written notice** to the other Party via email. Written notice must be sent to the email addresses listed in this Agreement.

- Client is responsible for removing call forwarding from their business phone number(s).

## 5. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

**a)** Provide accurate and complete business information on the Client Intake Form, including but not limited to: business name, services offered, business hours, service area, pricing information, and emergency protocols.

**b)** Maintain an active and accessible calendar system for real-time appointment booking.

**d)** Respond to service-related communications from Provider within **twenty-four (24) hours**.

**e)** Notify Provider within **two (2) business days** of any changes to business information, hours, services, pricing, staff, or emergency contacts.

**f)** Set up and maintain proper call forwarding from Client's business phone number(s) to Provider's designated number. Provider will supply instructions but Client is responsible for execution with their phone carrier.

**g)** Ensure all information provided to Provider is accurate and does not violate any laws or regulations.

## 6. Call Handling and AI Technology

**a) AI-Powered Service.** Client acknowledges and agrees that calls are answered using artificial intelligence technology, not live human operators.

**b) Training.** The AI receptionist will be configured and trained based solely on the business information provided by Client. The quality and accuracy of the AI's responses depend on the completeness and accuracy of the information Client provides.

**c) Limitations.** Provider does not guarantee that the AI will correctly handle every possible call scenario. Complex, unusual, or hostile calls may not be handled optimally. In such cases, the AI will attempt to capture the caller's information and flag the call for Client follow-up.

**d) Continuous Improvement.** Provider will monitor call quality and make reasonable adjustments to improve AI performance over time.

**e) Call Recording and Transcription.** Calls may be recorded and transcribed for quality assurance and service improvement purposes. Client consents to this recording. Client is responsible for ensuring compliance with any state or local laws regarding call recording notification in their jurisdiction.
