# Client Kickoff Call Script

**Purpose:** First call with a new paying client. Get everything needed to launch their AI receptionist within 48 hours.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes

**Before the call:** Confirm payment received, send calendar invite, have onboarding form ready.

---

## 1. Welcome & Rapport (3 minutes)

> "Hey [Client Name], this is Wallace with The Call Taker. First off, congratulations on making a great decision for your business. I'm excited to get your AI receptionist up and running so you never miss another call again.
>
> Before we dive in, I just want to say thank you for trusting us. We work with service businesses across 8 industries, so everything we do is built specifically for how your business operates. You're going to love this."

**If they seem nervous or unsure:**

> "I know AI can sound a little intimidating, but I promise this is going to be one of the easiest things you've ever set up for your business. I handle everything. All I need from you today is some basic info about how you run things, and I'll take it from there."

---

## 2. Set Expectations for This Call (2 minutes)

> "Here's what we're going to cover today:
>
> 1. I'll collect some basic info about your business -- hours, services, how you handle emergencies, that kind of thing.
> 2. I'll walk you through exactly how your AI receptionist will work.
> 3. We'll get your calendar connected so appointments book automatically.
> 4. I'll explain the timeline -- spoiler alert, you'll be live within 48 hours.
> 5. And then I'll open it up for any questions you've got.
>
> Sound good?"

## 4. AI Receptionist Customization (5 minutes)

### Greeting & Personality

> "When someone calls, here's what they'll hear..."

**Default greeting:**
> "Thank you for calling [Business Name], this is [AI Name]. How can I help you today?"

- **Preferred AI name:** (e.g., Sarah, Jessica, or just "your receptionist")
- **Tone preference:** Professional / Friendly / Southern Friendly / Casual
- **Any specific phrases you always want used?** (e.g., "We appreciate your business")
- **Any phrases to avoid?**

### Call Handling Rules

- **Maximum hold time before escalation:** _____ seconds
- **What if the caller asks for pricing?** (Give ranges / Don't discuss pricing / Specific quotes)
- **What if the caller asks for the owner?** (Transfer / Take message / Schedule callback)
- **How to handle solicitors/spam?** (Politely decline / Hang up / Take message)

## 5. Explain What Happens Next (3 minutes)

> "Alright, here's what happens from here:
>
> 1. **Today:** I take everything we just talked about and build out your custom AI receptionist. I program the greeting, the services, the scheduling rules, the emergency protocol -- all of it.
>
> 2. **Tomorrow:** I'll send you a test call so you can hear exactly how it sounds. You call in, pretend to be a customer, and make sure you're happy with it. If you want anything tweaked, just tell me.
>
> 3. **Within 48 hours:** Once you approve the test, we flip the switch. Your AI receptionist goes live. Every call that comes into your business number gets answered -- first ring, every time.
>
> 4. **First week:** I'll personally check in to make sure everything is running smoothly. You'll also get access to your dashboard where you can see every call, every booking, everything in real time.
>
> 5. **Ongoing:** You'll get a monthly performance report from me showing exactly how many calls were answered, how many appointments were booked, and how much revenue we helped you capture.
>
> Any concerns so far?"

## 7. Q&A (5-10 minutes)

> "What questions do you have for me? No question is too basic -- I want you to feel 100% confident."

### Common Questions & Answers

**"What if the AI can't handle a call?"**
> "Great question. If the AI ever gets confused or the caller specifically asks for a real person, the call gets transferred to your backup number immediately. You're always in control."

**"Can I listen to the calls?"**
> "Absolutely. Every call is recorded and available in your dashboard. You can listen to any call anytime. Most of our clients love this because it's also great for quality control on your own team."

**"What if I need to change something?"**
> "Just text or email me. Need to update your hours for a holiday? Change your emergency number? Add a new service? I handle all of that, usually same day."

**"Will callers know it's AI?"**
> "Some might, some won't. What they WILL know is that their call was answered immediately, they were treated professionally, and their appointment got booked. That's what matters."

**"What if I don't get many calls?"**
> "Then you're paying for peace of mind that when calls DO come in -- especially that high-value lead on a Saturday morning -- it gets answered. One missed call can cost more than a full year of our service."
