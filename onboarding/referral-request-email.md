# Referral Request Email Templates

Three email templates for asking happy clients for referrals, timed to different moments in the client relationship.

## Referral Incentive Structure

Before sending any of these, decide on your referral reward. Here are two options:

### Option A: 1 Free Month
- For every referral that signs up and stays active for 30 days, the referring client gets **1 month free** on their next billing cycle.
- Simple to explain, easy to fulfill, directly reduces their cost.
- Best for: clients on monthly plans who are price-conscious.

### Option B: $100 Account Credit
- For every referral that signs up, the referring client gets a **$100 credit** applied to their account.
- Credits stack (2 referrals = $200 credit).
- Best for: clients on annual plans or higher-tier pricing where "1 free month" feels less impactful.

**Recommendation:** Start with Option A (1 free month). It is easier to explain in a text or quick conversation, and business owners immediately understand the value. Switch to Option B if you move upmarket or raise prices.

## Version 1: After 30 Days (The Subtle Ask)

**When to send:** 30 days after go-live, once the system is running smoothly and they have seen results.

**Why it works:** You are not asking for a favor -- you are sharing good news about their results and planting the seed naturally.

**Subject:** Your first month with The Call Taker

Talk soon,

### Why this version works:
- Leads with **their results** so the email feels valuable, not self-serving.
- The referral ask is buried at the bottom, after the value delivery.
- Frames it as "just an intro" and "a free audit" -- zero pressure on the friend.
- The incentive (free month) is mentioned casually, not as the main event.

## Version 2: After 60 Days (The Direct Ask)

**When to send:** 60 days after go-live. They are now a proven, happy client. Time to be direct.

**Why it works:** By day 60, they trust you. A clear, specific ask with a concrete incentive converts better than being subtle.

**Subject:** Quick favor (takes 30 seconds)

---

Hey [First Name],

Quick question -- do you know 1 or 2 other companies in your industry that are probably missing calls right now?

I am looking to bring on a few more shops this month, and honestly, my best clients have come from referrals from guys like you.

Here is why I am asking you specifically: your numbers have been great. [X] calls answered, [X] jobs booked that would have gone to voicemail. You get it. You know what this does for a business.

**Here is the deal:**

For every owner you send my way who signs up, **you get a free month of service**. No limit. Send me 3 referrals that sign up, that is 3 months free.

All I need is:
1. Their name
2. Their company name
3. Their phone number or email

I will take it from there. I will run a free missed call audit for them, show them what they are leaving on the table, and if it makes sense, get them set up. Your name comes up as the person who told me about them -- nothing weird, no cold pitch.

You can just reply to this email with their info, or text me at (615) 653-9004.

Appreciate you, [First Name].

Wallace | The Call Taker
(615) 653-9004
thecalltaker.com

---

### Why this version works:
- **Opens with a question**, not a statement. Questions get replies.
- **"1 or 2"** is specific. Asking for "anyone you know" is too vague and gets ignored.
- **Shows their own results** to remind them why this works.
- **Spells out exactly what you need** (name, company, phone/email). Reduces friction.
- **Explains what you will do** so they are not worried you will spam their friend.
- **Stacks the incentive** (no limit) to make it feel generous.

## Version 3: After a Compliment / Positive Feedback (Strike While Hot)

**When to send:** Immediately after the client says something positive -- a text saying "this thing is awesome," a good review, a comment about a job they booked from the AI, anything.

**Why it works:** Reciprocity is strongest in the moment. When someone just told you they are happy, they are primed to help.

**Subject:** Glad it's working -- quick thought

---

Hey [First Name],

Really glad to hear that. Seriously, that kind of feedback makes my day.

Since things are clicking, I have a quick ask. Do you know any other business owners -- maybe people you see at industry events, folks in your Facebook groups, or even competitors in a different zip code -- who are probably missing calls?

I would love to do the same thing for them that I did for you. I will run a free audit, show them the data, and if it makes sense, get them set up.

And because you referred them: **your next month is free** for every one that signs up. Just reply with their name and number and I will handle the rest.

No pressure at all. Just figured I would ask while you are in a good mood about it.

Thanks again, [First Name]. Means a lot.

Wallace | The Call Taker
(615) 653-9004
thecalltaker.com

---

### Why this version works:
- **Acknowledges their compliment first.** Do not skip this. It validates them and deepens the relationship.
- **"While you are in a good mood about it"** -- self-aware and a little funny. Disarms the ask.
- **Gives them specific places to think** (supply houses, Facebook groups, competitors in other zips). This helps them mentally scan their network instead of drawing a blank.
- **Low friction ask** -- just a name and number. Do not ask for an email intro or a warm handoff. That is too much work for most people.

## Follow-Up If No Response

If they do not reply to any referral request:

- **Do not follow up on the referral ask specifically.** It feels pushy.
- **Wait 30 days** and try a different angle. For example, text them: "Hey [First Name], random question -- you know anyone else in your industry who complains about missing calls? Trying to help a few more businesses like yours."
- **The best referral generator is great service.** Keep delivering results and the referrals will come naturally over time.
