Our Story

Why We Built This

Wallace started in the trades. He knows what it's like to be on a roof, under a unit, covered in grease — and the phone won't stop ringing. Except when it does stop ringing. That's when you check your voicemail and find out three people needed emergency service... two hours ago.

He watched HVAC companies — good companies, with great techs and solid reputations — lose thousands of dollars every month to missed calls. Not because they didn't care. Because they were busy doing the work. The same companies that would never miss a job in person were missing 30, 40, 50 calls a month.

So he built something about it. The Call Taker is an AI receptionist that picks up every single call, day or night. It talks to the customer like a real person, gets their info, books the appointment, and texts you the details. You wake up to a full schedule instead of a full voicemail box.
