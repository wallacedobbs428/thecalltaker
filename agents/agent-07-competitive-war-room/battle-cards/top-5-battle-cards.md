## BATTLE CARD #1: SMITH.AI

### THE OVERVIEW
Smith.ai offers both human receptionists and AI answering services. They're positioned as premium, targeting law firms, home services, and professional services. Well-established brand (founded 2015).

### PRICING (AS OF 2025)
- **Starter:** ~$210/mo (30 calls) = $7/call
- **Business:** ~$480/mo (80 calls) = $6/call
- **Pro:** ~$850/mo (150 calls) = $5.67/call
- **AI Answering (Sara):** Starting ~$100-300/mo (separate product)
- **Setup Fee:** $0
- **Contract:** Month-to-month, but volume discounts require commitments
- **Overages:** $7-10 per additional call

**100 calls/month = ~$650-850 depending on plan**

### FEATURES
**Included:**
- Live human receptionists (not AI on main product)
- Call answering, screening, transfer
- Appointment scheduling
- Lead intake and qualification
- Bilingual (English/Spanish)
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
- SMS answering available
- After-hours and overflow coverage

**Extra Costs:**
- Outbound calls
- SMS replies (charged separately)
- Advanced integrations
- Chat support

### TARGET MARKET
- Law firms (primary)
- Home services (secondary)
- Professional services, healthcare
- 5-100+ employee companies
- Companies wanting human touch, not AI

### TECHNOLOGY
- **PRIMARY:** Human receptionists (24/7 team)
- **NEW:** "Sara" AI answering (separate product launched 2023)
- Hybrid model available

### STRENGTHS
- Premium brand with strong reputation
- Excellent for complex legal intake
- Bilingual capability (real humans)
- Robust CRM integrations
- Professional, well-trained staff
- Good for businesses requiring nuanced judgment calls

### WEAKNESSES
**General:**
- Expensive — $7-10 per call adds up fast
- Per-call pricing unpredictable (busy month = huge bill)
- Humans = inconsistency (staff turnover, training gaps)
- Not HVAC-specific (generic scripts)
- AI product (Sara) is new, separate offering, less mature

**For HVAC Specifically:**
- Not built for emergency triage (gas leaks, no heat in winter)
- Generic home services scripts, not HVAC terminology
- Per-call pricing punishes busy seasons (summer AC rush)
- Humans sleep — handoffs between shifts create gaps
- No specialization in equipment types, urgency assessment

### REVIEWS (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot — 2024-2025 themes)
**Positive:**
- "Professional and polished"
- "Great for legal intake"
- "Integrations work well"

**Negative:**
- "Too expensive for high call volume"
- "Billing surprises when we had a busy month"
- "Inconsistent quality depending on which receptionist answers"
- "Script changes take forever to implement"
- "Not great for technical industries"

**Rating Summary:** 4.3-4.5/5 stars (strong, but price complaints common)

### WHY THE CALL TAKER WINS

**In the Demo:**
1. **Predictable Pricing:** "Smith.ai charges $7 per call. If you get 100 calls, that's $700. We're $497 flat. If you get 200 calls in July when every AC in town breaks, you still pay $497. They'd charge you $1,400."

2. **Built for HVAC:** "Their receptionists use generic scripts. Ours knows HVAC — it asks about system type, age, symptoms. It knows the difference between 'my AC isn't cooling' and 'my furnace smells like gas.' One needs an appointment. One needs 911."

3. **Consistency:** "Humans are great, but they have bad days. They quit. They forget details. AI shows up the same way every single time, 3am or 3pm."

4. **Speed:** "You can update our AI's knowledge in 10 minutes. With Smith.ai, script changes take days or weeks because they have to train an entire team."

**The Knockout Line:**
"Smith.ai is a premium service for law firms who need a human touch for $10,000 retainer calls. You're an HVAC company. You need someone who answers every call, books the job, and doesn't cost you $1,000+ in busy months. That's us."

## CROSS-COMPETITOR TALKING POINTS

### When Prospect Says: "I'm comparing a few options..."

**Wallace's Response:**
"Smart move. Here's what to ask them:

1. **What's my bill if I get 200 calls next month?** (Most can't answer or the number will shock you.)
2. **Can I cancel anytime or am I locked in?** (Watch for 12-month contracts.)
3. **Is this AI or humans, and what's the backup plan?** (Humans get sick, quit, have bad days.)
4. **How fast can I update the script?** (Most: days/weeks. Us: minutes.)
5. **Do you specialize in HVAC or is it generic?** (Most: generic home services.)

Then: 'We're $497 flat, no contract, HVAC-only, AI-powered with GPT-4o, and you can update it in real-time. Most of our competitors can't say all five.'"

### When Prospect Says: "This other service is cheaper..."

**Wallace's Response:**
"Let's compare apples to apples. What's included in that price?

- How many calls or minutes?
- What happens when you go over?
- What's the setup fee?
- What's the contract length?
- Is it AI or human?
- Does it integrate with your scheduling software?

Most 'cheaper' options are cheaper because they're limited — 50 calls, basic features, long contracts, or outdated tech. We're $497 for UNLIMITED calls with the best AI available. If someone's actually cheaper for the same thing, I'd love to see it — because that's probably too good to be true."

### When Prospect Says: "I want a human, not AI..."

**Wallace's Response:**
"I totally get that. Here's the thing — when you call a business and get a receptionist, do you care if they're human or AI? Or do you care if they're HELPFUL?

Our AI is trained specifically on YOUR business. It knows your pricing, your hours, your services, your equipment. It never has a bad day, never forgets, never puts someone on hold for 5 minutes.

Most human answering services use generic scripts. They don't know HVAC. They just take messages.

Here's my offer: call our AI demo line right now. (615) 784-5747. If it sounds robotic or unhelpful, stick with humans. But if it sounds like a real receptionist who knows what they're doing — let's talk."
