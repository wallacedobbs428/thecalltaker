## TARGET PROFILE

- Location: US-based, starting with Kansas, expanding to neighboring states

- Large corporations with complex procurement

- Companies with less than 10 Google reviews (too small/new)
- Companies with perfect 5.0 ratings and glowing phone reviews (no pain point)

## LEAD SCORING CRITERIA

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 10/10 | Owner-operated, 5-50 employees, negative reviews about responsiveness, goes to voicemail during AND after hours, active social media (easy to DM), direct owner email found |
| 9/10 | All above but answers during business hours (still needs after-hours help) |
| 8/10 | Most criteria met, owner name/email found, clear pain indicators in reviews |
| 7/10 | Good fit but missing some intel — no owner direct contact or limited reviews |
| 6/10 | Fits target profile but no clear pain indicators — may need more research |
| 5/10 | Marginal fit — slightly outside target size or has some answering solution already |
| 4/10 | Has an answering service but it's getting bad reviews — potential switch opportunity |
| 3/10 | Too small (under 5 employees) or too new (under 2 years) — risky |
| 2/10 | Doesn't fit target well — franchise, too large, or outside service area |
| 1/10 | Not a fit — wrong industry, wrong size, or unreachable decision maker |
