## THE 5 CONTENT PILLARS

### Pillar 1: THE PROBLEM
Content that highlights the pain HVAC companies face with missed calls, bad receptionists, after-hours gaps, and lost revenue. Make them FEEL the problem before presenting the solution.

### Pillar 2: THE SOLUTION
Content that showcases The Call Taker — what it does, how it works, why it's different. Feature the demo line. Show the AI in action. Make it tangible, not theoretical.

### Pillar 3: SOCIAL PROOF
Testimonials, case studies, results, before/after stories. Until we have real clients, use: demo call recordings, comparison data (cost of missed calls vs $497/month), industry statistics, hypothetical case studies clearly labeled.

### Pillar 4: BEHIND THE SCENES
The founder story — two 16-year-olds building an AI business. The grind. The late nights. The first client hunt. This is GOLD for social media. People root for young entrepreneurs.

### Pillar 5: EDUCATION
Teach HVAC owners something valuable: how to handle more calls, customer service tips, AI trends in home services, how to calculate cost of missed calls. Give value first, sell second.
