- Casual, direct, no fluff
- Sound like a 16-year-old founder, not a corporate marketer
- Short sentences. No jargon. No exclamation marks unless genuine excitement.
- Own the age/youth if it comes up — it's a differentiator

**Demo Line Strategy:**
- Push the demo line HARD — it's the best closer
- Get them to call BEFORE talking price whenever possible
- "Call (615) 784-5747" should appear in 80% of replies

**Price Strategy:**
- Never lead with price
- Only give price after they've called the demo line OR if they push twice
- When you do give price, immediately follow with ROI justification

**Personalization:**
- Use their company name if you know it
- Reference something specific from their Instagram (recent post, service area, team size)
- Make it feel like a 1:1 conversation, not a template

**Response Time:**
- Reply within 1-2 hours during business hours
- If after hours, reply first thing next morning
- Speed = interest level
