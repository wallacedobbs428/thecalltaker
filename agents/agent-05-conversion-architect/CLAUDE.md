## PAGE STRUCTURE & COPY RULES

### Headlines
- Max 10 words
- Specific, not vague: "Never Miss Another HVAC Call" not "Better Customer Service"
- Include a number or specific claim when possible
- Test question headlines vs statement headlines

### Subheadlines
- Max 20 words
- Expand on the headline's promise
- Address the "how" or "why"

### Body Copy
- Short paragraphs — 2-3 sentences max
- One idea per paragraph
- Use bullet points for features/benefits
- Write at an 8th grade reading level — no jargon, no fluff
- Every paragraph must move the reader toward the CTA

### CTAs (Calls to Action)
- Action-oriented: "Book Your Free Demo" not "Learn More"
- Specific: "Call (615) 784-5747 Now" not "Contact Us"
- One primary CTA per section, max two per page view
- Button color: Orange (#f97316) on dark navy (#0a1628) background
- Include "free" on EVERY landing page — "Free Demo," "Free Trial," "Risk-Free"

### Social Proof
- Specific numbers: "Answers calls in under 2 seconds" not "Fast response times"
- Before/after framework: "Before The Call Taker: 40% of calls missed. After: 0% missed."
- Cost comparison: "$497/month vs. $3,000+/month for a full-time receptionist"
- Trust badges: money-back guarantee, 24/7 availability, no contracts

### SEO Requirements
- Target keywords per page — primary and secondary
- Meta title: max 60 characters, keyword + brand
- Meta description: max 155 characters, keyword + CTA
- H1: one per page, includes primary keyword
- H2/H3: include secondary keywords naturally
- Image alt tags: descriptive, include keywords where natural
- Internal links between all pages
- Schema markup: LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ
