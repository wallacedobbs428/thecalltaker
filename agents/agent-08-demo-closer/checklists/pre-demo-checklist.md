# The Call Taker — Pre-Demo Checklist
## Everything You Need Before, During, and After the Demo

**Purpose:** Show up to every demo fully prepared, confident, and ready to close. No winging it.

---

## PHASE 1: BEFORE BOOKING (When Lead Comes In)

### Research Their Company (5-10 minutes)
- [ ] **Google their business name** — do they have a website? What do they offer?
- [ ] **Check Google Reviews** — how many? What's the average rating? Any complaints about unreachability?
- [ ] **Visit their website** — what's their service area? Do they list hours? Any mention of emergency service?
- [ ] **Find the owner's name** — check website About page, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Google Business profile
- [ ] **Estimate company size** — how many employees? Small owner-operated (1-5) or larger operation (10-50)?
- [ ] **Test their phone system** — call their number (after hours if possible). Do they go to voicemail? Answering service? How long does it ring?

### Document What You Found
Create a quick profile:
```
COMPANY: [Name]
OWNER: [Full Name]
LOCATION: [City, State]
GOOGLE REVIEWS: [Count] | [Avg Rating]
PHONE TEST: [Voicemail / Answering Service / No Answer]
PAIN POINTS: [From reviews or obvious gaps — e.g., "3 reviews mention hard to reach"]
PERSONALIZATION HOOK: [One specific thing to mention — e.g., "Saw you've been in business 15 years"]
```

**Why this matters:** Personalization wins demos. "I saw you have 4.8 stars on Google but a couple people mentioned they had trouble reaching you" is 10x more powerful than a generic pitch.

### Prep Your ROI Math
Use your research to estimate their numbers:
- **Calls per week:** [Estimate based on company size — small = 15-30, medium = 40-80]
- **% to voicemail:** [Estimate 30-50% if after-hours test went to voicemail]
- **Missed calls per month:** [Calls per week × % to voicemail × 4]
- **Average job value:** [Research typical HVAC job prices in their area — $300-800 for repairs, $5K-15K for installs]
- **Monthly missed revenue:** [Missed calls × 10-20% conversion × avg job value]

**Example:**
- 40 calls/week → 30% voicemail → 48 missed calls/month
- 10% of those = 5 missed jobs
- $500 avg job value → $2,500/month in lost revenue

Write this down so you can reference it during the demo.

## PHASE 3: 15 MINUTES BEFORE THE DEMO

### Technical Setup
- [ ] **Close all unnecessary tabs** — keep only: Zoom, their website, your notes, and a calculator
- [ ] **Open the Zoom link early** — make sure camera and mic work
- [ ] **Have your phone ready** — you'll be calling the demo line on speaker
- [ ] **Test screen share** — make sure you can share your screen if needed
- [ ] **Pull up their company website** — have it open so you can reference it during the call
- [ ] **Silence your notifications** — phone on Do Not Disturb, computer notifications off

### Mental Setup
- [ ] **Review your notes** — owner name, personalization hook, their pain points
- [ ] **Review the demo script** — know the flow: Rapport → Problem → Demo Call → ROI → Close
- [ ] **Set your intention** — you're not a salesperson, you're a consultant. You're here to help them solve a problem.
- [ ] **Get in the right headset** — confident, curious, conversational. You built something that works.

**Mindset Reminder:**
- You're 19. They might think you're young. That's fine — own it.
- You're not asking them for a favor. You're offering them a solution.
- If they say no, it's not personal. You're learning either way.
- The AI sells itself. Your job is to show them the problem and let them hear the solution.

## PHASE 4: DURING THE DEMO

### Kick-Off (First 60 seconds)
- [ ] **Join on time** (1-2 minutes early is ideal)
- [ ] **Smile, be human, build rapport**
- [ ] **Use their name at least 3 times** in the first 5 minutes
- [ ] **Set expectations:** "This is only 15 minutes, and we'll call the AI live together so you can hear it yourself."

### Discovery (Minutes 0-2)
- [ ] Ask how they currently handle calls
- [ ] Ask what happens after hours
- [ ] Ask about missed calls or voicemail backlog
- [ ] **Take notes** — you'll reference this during ROI

### Problem Amplification (Minutes 2-5)
- [ ] Walk through the missed call math using their numbers
- [ ] Reference their specific pain points (from reviews or your research)
- [ ] Paint the picture: "First one to answer gets the job"

### Live Demo Call (Minutes 5-10)
- [ ] **Call (615) 784-5747 on speaker**
- [ ] Let the AI answer
- [ ] Pretend you're a customer with an HVAC issue
- [ ] Let the conversation play out naturally
- [ ] **Debrief:** "What did you notice?" → highlight how it sounded real, asked the right questions, etc.

### ROI Breakdown (Minutes 10-13)
- [ ] Use their actual numbers (from discovery)
- [ ] Show that 1 extra job per month pays for it
- [ ] Compare to cost of receptionist ($2,500+/mo) or answering service ($600-1,000/mo)

### The Close (Minutes 13-15)
- [ ] Ask: "Does this make sense for your business?"
- [ ] If yes → outline next steps (proposal, onboarding form, 48-hour setup)
- [ ] If maybe → identify objection, address it, lock in follow-up
- [ ] If no → ask why, learn from it, end on good terms

### Throughout the Call
- [ ] **Use their company name** multiple times
- [ ] **Listen more than you talk** — especially during discovery
- [ ] **Watch for objections** — address them as they come up, don't ignore them
- [ ] **Stay conversational** — this isn't a presentation, it's a conversation

## PHASE 5: IMMEDIATELY AFTER THE DEMO

### Document Everything (Within 5 minutes)
- [ ] **Update CRM** — demo outcome (yes / maybe / no), next steps, follow-up date
- [ ] **Write down their objections** — even if you addressed them, note what they said
- [ ] **Note what worked and what didn't** — this is how you get better

### Follow-Up Actions (Within 1 hour)
- [ ] **Send proposal email** if they're interested (template below)
- [ ] **Send thank-you email** if they need time to think
- [ ] **Send calendar link** if you scheduled a follow-up
- [ ] **Update their contact** in GHL with tags: Demo Completed, Outcome (Interested/Maybe/No)

## PHASE 6: DEBRIEF (End of Day)

### Reflect and Learn (5 minutes)
- [ ] **What went well?** (Write it down — reinforce what's working)
- [ ] **What could've gone better?** (Be honest — this is how you improve)
- [ ] **Which objections came up?** (Review the Objection Bible and tighten your responses)
- [ ] **Did you stay on time?** (If the demo went over 15 minutes, figure out where you can tighten)
- [ ] **Did you sound confident?** (If not, what made you hesitate? Practice that part.)

### Celebrate Progress
- [ ] **You did a demo.** That's more than most people ever do. Whether they said yes or no, you showed up and did the work. That matters.
