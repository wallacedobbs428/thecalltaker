# The Call Taker — Full Demo Script
## 15-Minute Demo Call Structure

**Remember:** You're a consultant helping them solve a problem, not a salesperson. Be conversational, curious, and confident. You built something that works — show them.

---

## 0:00-2:00 — RAPPORT & PAIN DISCOVERY

### Opening (First 30 seconds)
**YOU:** "Hey [OWNER NAME], great to meet you! Thanks for jumping on. I'm Wallace — I run The Call Taker with my partner Mills. Before I show you how this works, I want to make sure this is actually a good fit for you. Cool if I ask a few quick questions about your business first?"

*Wait for agreement. Build rapport — smile, be human.*

### Discovery Questions (Next 90 seconds)
Ask 3-4 of these based on what you know about them:

**YOU:** "So tell me — how do you currently handle incoming calls? Is it just you, or do you have someone answering?"

*Listen. Take mental notes.*

**YOU:** "And what happens after hours? Do calls go to voicemail, or do you have an answering service?"

**YOU:** "How often would you say you're in the middle of a job and a call comes in that you can't pick up?"

**YOU:** "When someone leaves a voicemail, how quickly do you usually get back to them? Same day, or does it sometimes take longer?"

**YOU:** "Have you ever had a customer mention they had trouble getting ahold of you? Or noticed jobs you quoted that went to a competitor?"

### Transition to Problem
**YOU:** "Got it. So it sounds like [SUMMARIZE THEIR SITUATION — e.g., 'you're handling most calls yourself, but when you're on a job site or it's after 5pm, things go to voicemail and sometimes you don't get back until the next day']. Does that sound right?"

*Wait for confirmation.*

**YOU:** "Okay, cool. That's exactly the problem we built this to solve. Let me show you why that's costing you more than you think, and then we'll call the AI together so you can hear it yourself."

---

## 2:00-5:00 — PROBLEM AMPLIFICATION

**YOU:** "So here's what most HVAC owners don't realize: every missed call isn't just a missed call — it's a missed job. And in HVAC, the person calling you is usually calling 3-4 other companies at the same time. First one to answer? That's who gets the job."

### The Math (Use their numbers if you have them)
**YOU:** "Let me show you the math real quick. How many calls would you say you get in a week? Ballpark."

*They give a number — let's say 30.*

**YOU:** "Okay, 30 calls a week. And of those, how many do you think go to voicemail because you're busy or it's after hours? Maybe 30%? 40%?"

*Let them estimate. If unsure, suggest 40%.*

**YOU:** "So that's about 12 calls a week going to voicemail. That's 50 calls a month. Now, not every call is a job — some are existing customers, some are people just price shopping. But let's be conservative and say 1 in 5 of those missed calls was a real job you could've booked. That's 10 jobs a month you didn't even get a chance to quote."

**YOU:** "What's your average job worth? Like, if someone calls for an AC repair or a maintenance call, what do you usually charge?"

*They give a number — let's say $500.*

**YOU:** "So 10 missed jobs a month at $500 each... that's $5,000 a month walking out the door because your phone went to voicemail. Over $60,000 a year."

*Pause. Let that land.*

**YOU:** "And that's being conservative. Some of those calls could be full system replacements at $8K, $10K, $15K. You see what I'm saying?"

### Transition to Solution
**YOU:** "That's the problem. The solution is simple: answer every call, every time, 24/7, with someone who sounds professional, asks the right questions, and books the appointment while the customer's on the phone. That's what The Call Taker does. And the best part? It's not a person — it's AI. So it never sleeps, never takes a day off, and never costs you $3,000 a month for a receptionist."

**YOU:** "Let me show you. You ready to call it together?"

---

## 5:00-10:00 — LIVE DEMO CALL

### Setup
**YOU:** "Okay, here's how this works. I'm going to call our demo line on speaker so you can hear it. The number is (615) 784-5747. This is set up for a fake company called 'Demo HVAC Services' right now, but when you sign up, we'll customize everything to your business — your name, your services, your questions, all of it."

**YOU:** "I'm going to pretend I'm a customer calling about my AC not working. Just listen to how it handles the call. Ready?"

### Make the Call
*Call (615) 784-5747 on speaker. Let the AI answer.*

**AI:** "Thanks for calling Demo HVAC Services, how can I help you today?"

**YOU (as customer):** "Yeah, hi, my AC stopped working this morning and it's getting really hot in here. Can you guys come out today?"

*Let the AI respond. It should ask for details — name, address, what's happening, etc.*

**YOU (as customer):** *Provide realistic answers — name, address, describe the issue.*

*Let the conversation play out naturally. The AI should collect info, assess urgency, and offer to book an appointment or have someone call back.*

**YOU:** "Awesome, thanks."

*End call.*

### Debrief the Call
**YOU:** "Okay, so what did you notice?"

*Let them respond. Highlight positives:*

**YOU:** "Right? It sounded like a real person. It asked the right questions — name, address, what's wrong, when do you need service. It didn't try to diagnose the problem over the phone, which is smart. And it was ready to book me in or take my info for a callback."

**YOU:** "Now imagine that's happening 24/7 for your business. Someone calls at 9pm on a Saturday? The AI answers. Someone calls while you're on a ladder installing a unit? The AI answers. You're never missing another call."

**YOU:** "And here's the kicker — this costs you $497 a month. No setup fee, no contract, cancel anytime. Compare that to hiring a receptionist at $15 an hour, 40 hours a week... that's $2,600 a month. Or an answering service that charges you $3-5 per call, which adds up fast."

### Address Immediate Reactions
*If they look skeptical:*
**YOU:** "I know it sounds too good to be true. What's your biggest concern about something like this?"

*If they look interested:*
**YOU:** "Make sense so far? Want me to walk you through the ROI real quick so you can see exactly how this pays for itself?"

---

## 10:00-13:00 — ROI BREAKDOWN

**YOU:** "Let me show you the numbers specific to your business, because I want you to see this is a no-brainer."

*Pull up notes or calculator. Use their actual numbers from discovery.*

### The Math (Personalized)
**YOU:** "You said you get about [X] calls a week, and roughly [Y%] go to voicemail. That's [Z] missed calls a month."

**YOU:** "Now, even if only 1 in 10 of those missed calls was a job you could've closed — and that's really conservative — that's [Z/10] jobs a month."

**YOU:** "You said your average job is around [$XXX]. So that's [$XXX × Z/10] in potential revenue you're losing every month."

**YOU:** "The Call Taker costs $497. So if this helps you book just ONE extra job a month, it's paid for itself. Everything after that is pure profit."

*Pause.*

**YOU:** "And that's not even counting the time you save. How much is your time worth? Right now, you're stopping what you're doing to answer calls, or you're spending your evenings returning voicemails. What if you could just focus on the actual work, and let the AI handle the phone?"

### The Bigger Picture
**YOU:** "Here's how I think about it: you're in the business of fixing HVAC systems, not answering phones. For $497 a month, you get a 24/7 receptionist that never sleeps, never calls in sick, and costs less than one day of your labor. It's the easiest ROI you'll ever see."

**YOU:** "Does that make sense?"

*Wait for response.*

---

## 13:00-15:00 — THE CLOSE

### If They're Ready to Move Forward
**YOU:** "Awesome. So here's what happens next. I'll send you a quick proposal after this call with everything we talked about. If you want to move forward, you'll just fill out a short form with your business info — company name, hours, services, that kind of thing. Then we'll build out your AI agent, and within 48 hours, you'll be live."

**YOU:** "You'll get a dedicated phone number that forwards to the AI, or you can forward your existing number to it. Totally up to you. And we'll test it together before you go live to make sure it sounds perfect."

**YOU:** "Sound good? Any questions before I send that over?"

*Answer questions. Then:*

**YOU:** "Cool. I'll get that to you in the next hour. And hey — I'm 19, running this with my partner Mills. We're both young, but that's actually a good thing for you, because we're hungry and we're going to make sure this works perfectly. You're not just another account number to us. You're one of our first clients, and we're going to take care of you. Deal?"

*Wait for confirmation.*

**YOU:** "Awesome. Talk soon, [OWNER NAME]."

---

### If They Want to Think About It
**YOU:** "Totally fair. What's the main thing you're unsure about? Is it the price, the technology, the setup, or something else?"

*Listen. Address the concern. Then:*

**YOU:** "Here's what I'd suggest: let me send you the proposal so you have everything in writing. Take a day or two to think it over. I'll follow up with you [DAY OF WEEK] — does morning or afternoon work better for you?"

*Lock in a follow-up time.*

**YOU:** "And if you have any questions between now and then, just text or call me. I'm not going to hound you — I just want to make sure you have everything you need to make the right call. Sound good?"

---

### If They Say No
**YOU:** "No worries, I totally get it. Can I ask — is it just not the right time, or is there something about the service that doesn't fit your business?"

*Listen. This is feedback gold.*

**YOU:** "Got it. Well, if things change or you want to revisit this down the road, feel free to reach out. And honestly, I appreciate you taking the time to hear me out. Best of luck with the business, [OWNER NAME]."

*End on good terms. You never know — they might refer someone, or come back later.*

---

## POST-CALL ACTIONS

**Within 1 hour:**
- Send proposal email with pricing, ROI breakdown, and next steps
- Send calendar link for follow-up if needed

**Within 24 hours:**
- Update CRM with demo outcome, notes, and next steps
- If they said yes, send onboarding form
- If they said maybe, schedule follow-up
- If they said no, note objection for future learning

---

## KEY REMINDERS

1. **Be conversational.** This is a conversation, not a pitch. Ask questions, listen, adapt.
2. **Use their name.** Personalize everything to their business.
3. **The live demo is the kill shot.** That's when they realize it actually works.
4. **Own your age.** You're young and hungry — that's an advantage, not a weakness.
5. **Don't oversell.** The product sells itself. Your job is to show them the problem and the solution.
6. **Confidence.** You built something that works. You're helping them make more money. Act like it.

**You've got this.**
