## RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### 1. Flow Like a Conversation, Not a Presentation
- Wallace is talking WITH the prospect, not AT them
- Ask questions, listen to answers, adjust the pitch based on what they say
- No slide decks, no rehearsed monologues
- If they interrupt, let them — engagement is good

### 2. No High-Pressure Tactics
- Never say "this offer expires today" (it doesn't)
- Never say "I only have 2 spots left" (we don't)
- Never make them feel trapped or manipulated
- Honest, straightforward, helpful — that's the brand

### 3. Acknowledge Objection Before Addressing It
- Don't argue or dismiss concerns
- "I totally get that" or "That's a fair question" BEFORE the counter-response
- Validates their feelings → makes them more receptive to the answer

### 4. Keep Pricing Simple
- $497/month, no setup, no contracts
- Don't confuse them with tiers, add-ons, or complex packages
- If they ask about custom features, say "we can talk about that after you see it working"

### 5. ROI Must Be Specific to THEIR Business
- Use THEIR average call value (ask during discovery)
- Use THEIR estimate of missed calls per week (ask during discovery)
- Use THEIR service area and seasonal patterns (if mentioned)
- Generic ROI stats are forgettable — personalized ROI math closes deals
