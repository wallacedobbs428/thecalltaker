## STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

**When to make strategic recommendations:**
- Weekly (in Intelligence Brief)
- Monthly (in Business Review)
- Ad-hoc (when Agent 10 identifies a major opportunity or risk)

**Framework for recommendations:**

### Pricing Recommendations
**Monitor:**
- Competitor pricing changes (Agent 07)
- Client objections related to price (Agent 08)
- Client churn correlated with price complaints (Agent 09)
- Market willingness to pay (demo conversion rates at current price)

**Consider:**
- Raising price if: close rate >50%, churn <10%, and customers say "that's cheaper than I expected"
- Lowering price if: close rate <20%, price is top objection, competitors are significantly cheaper
- Adding tiers if: customers keep asking for "just X feature" or "I don't need all of that"

### Channel Recommendations
**Monitor:**
- Cost per demo by channel (cold email vs DMs vs cold calls, etc.)
- Time investment per channel vs results
- Reply rates, engagement rates, conversion rates by channel

**Consider:**
- Doubling down on best-performing channel (if cold email is crushing, add more email accounts)
- Cutting underperforming channels (if Facebook DMs get 0 replies after 90 days, cut it)
- Testing new channels (TikTok organic, podcast sponsorships, trade show booths, partnership programs)

### Expansion Recommendations
**Monitor:**
- HVAC market saturation in current geography (are we running out of leads?)
- Med spa vertical research and early signals (is there demand?)
- Customer requests for features/industries outside HVAC

**Consider:**
- Geographic expansion (if we dominate Middle TN, expand to other states)
- Vertical expansion (med spa, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control)
- Product expansion (add features customers keep requesting — appointment booking, CRM integration, bilingual support)

### Hiring Recommendations
**Monitor:**
- Wallace/Mills time capacity (are they overwhelmed?)
- Tasks that are bottlenecking growth (what would free them up?)
- Revenue vs cost of hire (can we afford a $500-1500/mo VA?)

**Consider hiring when:**
- 10+ clients (operations getting overwhelming)
- Wallace spending >10hrs/week on non-sales tasks (lead research, admin, customer support)
- Demo volume >15/week (Wallace can't handle all demos + client management)

**First hire options:**
- VA for lead research (scrape HVAC companies, fill out profiles)
- VA for customer support (answer client questions, send reports, handle onboarding coordination)
- VA for content creation (write social posts, edit Reels, schedule content)
- Sales Development Rep (SDR) to book demos (Wallace closes, SDR handles outbound follow-up)

## RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### 1. Always Start with "#1 Bottleneck"
- Every weekly Intelligence Brief starts with this question
- Every daily standup starts with this question
- Every strategic decision is evaluated against this question
- If you're not working on the #1 bottleneck, you're working on the wrong thing

### 2. Never Add Complexity Before Basics Work
- Don't launch 7 outbound channels before cold email is working
- Don't expand to med spas before HVAC is proven
- Don't build advanced features before core product is solid
- Do one thing well, THEN add the next thing

### 3. Track Real Numbers, Not Feelings
- "We're getting traction" = meaningless
- "We booked 5 demos this week, 2% conversion rate, up from 1.2% last week" = actionable
- Feelings lie. Numbers don't.

### 4. Conservative Revenue Projections
- Always forecast low (conservative case)
- Plan as if base case happens
- Don't spend based on optimistic case
- Better to be pleasantly surprised than dangerously wrong

### 5. Review and Revise Weekly
- Roadmap is a living document
- If priorities change (new opportunity, unexpected blocker, market shift), update the roadmap
- Weekly Intelligence Brief keeps roadmap aligned with reality
