Bottom Line

The Call Taker costs less, answers faster, and books the appointment live instead of taking a message. Smith.ai is a better fit if you need a human voice on every call and are willing to pay $292+ per month for it. For service businesses that want speed and automatic booking at a lower price, The Call Taker is the stronger option.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does The Call Taker cost compared to Smith.ai?

The Call Taker starts at $97 per month with unlimited calls. Smith.ai starts at $292.50 per month for 30 calls, with additional calls charged at $9.75 each. A business receiving 60 calls per month would pay $97 with The Call Taker and $585 with Smith.ai.

Can The Call Taker book appointments?

Yes. The Call Taker books appointments directly into your calendar during the call. The customer receives a confirmed time before hanging up. Smith.ai takes a message and emails it to you to follow up.

How fast does The Call Taker answer?

The Call Taker answers every call in under 2 seconds. There is no hold time, no queue, and no routing delay. Smith.ai routes calls to available human receptionists, which typically takes 10 to 30 seconds.

Does The Call Taker work for plumbers, HVAC, and locksmiths?

Yes. The Call Taker serves 17 service industries with industry-specific training. GIDEON knows locksmith terminology, HVAC service types, plumbing emergencies, and other industry-specific workflows. Smith.ai uses general receptionists.

Is there a free trial?

The Call Taker offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Forward your after-hours calls and see real results before paying. Smith.ai does not offer a free trial.

What is the best alternative to Smith.ai for small businesses?

The Call Taker is the most affordable Smith.ai alternative for small service businesses. It costs $97 per month compared to Smith.ai's $292.50, answers in under 2 seconds, and books appointments automatically. Other alternatives include Ruby Receptionists ($235/month for 50 calls), Abby Connect ($329/month), and AnswerConnect ($325/month).
